Vera v0.1 — Initial Decision Analysis analyst: causal startup-success chain, TAM/SAM/SOM/REM, dilution, PWMOIC/IRR and outcome distributions.
Iteration lesson: useful structure, but probability calibration was too subjective and needed empirical reference classes.

Vera v0.2 — Calibration upgrade: require reference classes before probabilities; return UNCALIBRATED when evidence cannot support precision.
Iteration lesson: causal reasoning alone is not enough; evidence-backed priors and uncertainty discipline become mandatory.

Vera v0.2.1 — Research Coverage Matrix: systematic search of terms, filings, team, customers, market, competition, adverse evidence and references.
Iteration lesson: every material research area must be marked searched/not-found/inaccessible/not-material rather than silently skipped.

Vera v0.2.2 — Research Sufficiency: weighted completeness gates FULL/LIMITED/INSUFFICIENT.
Iteration lesson: source count is not the same thing as decision-quality coverage.

Vera v0.2.3 — Adaptive Research: Value-of-Information prioritizes unknowns most likely to change INVEST vs PASS.
Iteration lesson: stop low-value research and focus effort where new evidence can change the decision.

Vera v0.2.4 — Evidence Weighting: authority, independence, directness, recency and corroboration determine evidentiary weight.
Iteration lesson: issuer claims, user reviews and independent filings must not move probabilities equally.

Vera v0.2.5 — Contradiction Resolution: conflicting credible sources become explicit scenarios/ledger entries.
Iteration lesson: never silently choose a preferred number when valuation, traction, timing or milestones conflict.

Vera v0.2.6 — Sector-Specific Adverse Research: negative research adapts to SaaS, biotech, fintech, hardware, healthcare and marketplaces.
Iteration lesson: “not found” means searched-not-found, not proof that no adverse issue exists.

Vera v0.2.7 — Source Access & Reference Sources: substitute authoritative sources when a primary path is inaccessible.
Iteration lesson: inaccessible evidence must remain visible and cannot be silently replaced by weaker evidence.

Vera v0.2.8 — Reference-Class Registry: auditable stage/sector benchmarks with cohort, date, applicability and refresh rules.
Iteration lesson: benchmarks guide priors/bounds but are not automatically startup-specific success probabilities.

Vera v0.2.9 — Discussion & Review Bias Controls: positive/negative sampling, duplicate removal and reviewer-context checks.
Iteration lesson: investor enthusiasm and community sentiment remain separate from independently verified facts.

Vera v0.2.10 — Tiered Diligence: SCREEN, STANDARD and DEEP DILIGENCE with escalation triggers.
Iteration lesson: diligence depth should match decision importance without under-researching or wasting effort.
