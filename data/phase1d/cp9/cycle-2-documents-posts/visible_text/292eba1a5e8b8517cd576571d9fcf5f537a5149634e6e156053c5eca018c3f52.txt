Delee Q2-Q3 2023 Updates
Dear investors,
Season's Greetings!
We are delighted to share some uplifting news and progress updates about Delee as we approach the end of the year. Our journey through the Big in Bio program at Ganesha Labs last summer, a renowned Global Biotech Scaleup for science and technology startups in LATAM, has been truly remarkable. We are thrilled to announce that we received the prestigious “Excellence Award” for our exceptional performance in this year's BIGinBIO cohort, a testament to our team's hard work and dedication.
This award opens an exciting door for us: the chance to present at the upcoming RESI JP Morgan 2024 on January 9th, as part of the 50+ Innovator’s Pitch Challenge (IPC). This platform is not just a stage for us; it's a golden opportunity to connect with new investors and like-minded startups that will play a crucial role in scaling our company in the coming year.
Another highlight for Delee was our participation in the Miami Biohunt Summit last month, organized jointly by Ganesha Labs and CIC Miami. This annual summit is a convergence point for early-stage biotech startups from Latin America and U.S. investors and industry leaders. It's a vibrant showcase of Latin American science and innovation. Our presentation was a resounding success, leading to a new Lead Investor for our next investment round and the chance to collaborate with a top-notch regulatory consultant next year.
Looking ahead, we are filled with optimism. The network we've diligently built and nurtured this year is our springboard for accelerated growth in the coming year. Your unwavering support has been instrumental in our journey, and we can't thank you enough.
As we wrap up this year, we look forward to continuing this incredible journey with you and reaching new heights together.
Wishing you a joyful holiday season and a prosperous New Year!
Warm regards,
The Delee Team💙
Liza Velarde
Delee
