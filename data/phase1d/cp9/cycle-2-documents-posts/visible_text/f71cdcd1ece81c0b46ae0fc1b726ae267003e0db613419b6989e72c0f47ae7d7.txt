Here we grow again!
To all ACFN SAFE investors thank you for your patience as we work to grow our business and create value. We have been busy completing a small acquisition and working large corporate relationships to add quality locations to our network supporting our franchisees and increasing transaction volume through our network.
Acquisition Completed
We just completed the acquisition of a small ATM company with locations in OR and WA. This added a total of 55 locations to our portfolio including a relationship with a Federal Credit Union that we hope to grow and develop. This acquisition will add an estimated 120,000 transactions per year and based on initial feedback from existing franchisees will enable us to recruit 2 or 3 more franchisees to help absorb the new business. It’s a great add-on and we are already looking for the next acquisition to keep this momentum going!
Large Corporate Relationships
Malls - As you may remember from a previous release we signed a national deal with one of the largest mall owner/manager companies in the US late last year. As of today we have already installed 50 ATMs at malls they own and manage and have a road map to get an estimated 30 more installed in the coming months. This is quickly becoming a large and profitable relationship with positive impact on many participating franchise owners.
Supermarkets - we just secured an agreement to add 28 supermarkets to our networks in May. This group of supermarkets has more than 300 locations and with this latest deal we are now servicing more than 60 so we have 20% with a great opportunity to add many more. This deal was well received by existing franchisees and absorbed completed within 2 weeks. We expect to do more of these in the coming months and years.
The above are stand-outs and are in addition to single location we continue to add on a regular basis. We have also had healthy interest from new franchisees joining our business and have experienced an uptick in franchise sales so far in 2024.
We are busy focused on growing our business and will report back again soon!
Jeffrey D. Kerr
American Consumer Financial Network
