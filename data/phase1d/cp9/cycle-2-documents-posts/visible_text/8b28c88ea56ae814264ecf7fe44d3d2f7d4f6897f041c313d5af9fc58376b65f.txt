High Tide Deal Cues Up  Next Year's Global Expansion
Hot off the press!
Yesterday we executed our biggest deal to date, and this morning it hit the newswire.
We have entered into a trademark license agreement with Valiant Distribution Canada Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of High Tide Inc. The partnership will empower our team to design branded vaporizers, glass filtration devices, grinders, rolling trays, and a few other ideas we have been cooking up.
As a retail-focused cannabis company enhanced by the manufacturing and distribution of consumption accessories, High Tide is a perfect fit. They are the largest Canadian retailer of recreational cannabis as measured by revenue, with 66 current locations spanning Ontario, Alberta, Manitoba and Saskatchewan. High Tide’s retail segment has been serving consumers for over a decade through its numerous lifestyle accessory businesses, including e-commerce.
Thrilled to be able to share!
Grateful for your early support and wishing you a peaceful week,
Jane
