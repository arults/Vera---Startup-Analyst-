✅ ALERT: New Campaign. New Pivot. New Patent. AND MORE!!! 🚀
Happy Feb!! 👋
That's right Republic investors, we've got a series of awesome updates just for you. It's been a minute but we've been hard at work behind the scenes working on a new app called SelfTact (same corporation you invested in that has done nearly $1.2M in sales to date!).
Visit
SelfTact.com
to Learn more!
Back when you invested we only had HelloWoofy.com which focused on AI marketing (with granted patents) for small businesses but slowly and surely, our side project within the company started to gain recognition from small business owners / customers like you to help capture leads via selfies (we say smart selfies). Some of the top companies in the world pivoted to their internal products they built to run their business only soon to realize the path forward was to make that product public...read on!
So we decided to pivot to this entirely and help SMBs scale lead capturing with AI and empathy baked in...less crowded market, bigger market, easier to sell, and much more. Plus who doesn't like taking selfies? :D
What is SelfTact? 🎉
You go to networking events and struggle to remember who you took a selfie or photo with but yet know you must follow up to close the lead. Bummer. You also have business cards from folks you can't remember what they look like, high gloss cards with smeared ink from the notes you took and crumpled cards after a week of sitting in your back pocket. BUUUMMMMER!!!
Visit
SelfTact.com
to Learn more!
SelfTact is simple. You take a smart selfie, it uses facial recognition to connect you with your lead, AI to write an intro message with the selfie and show you where you met on a beautiful map for context. Done. Plus easy to see in a carousel "rolodex" all your smart selfies!
In the future this app will allow you to manage all your leads and contacts just like David Rockefeller did (but with giant index cards). Send gifts, letters and more to your top clients in one tap...with the smart selfie attached!
The true professional network for networkers.

Our goal (no guarantees) is to hit 1M free professional users in 12 months time and introduce a paid program after to drive revenue just as we successfully did with HelloWoofy.com once before.
