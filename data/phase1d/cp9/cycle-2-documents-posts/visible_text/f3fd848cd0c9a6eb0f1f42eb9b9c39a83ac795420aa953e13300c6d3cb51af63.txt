Inahsi Naturals Q4 Update
2022 has definitely been a busy year and we plan to finish with a bang.  Since the close of our campaign in April we have been quite busy growing our business and launching new products along the way.  While a number of businesses in our space are struggling with Supply Chain issues and reporting lower year over year sales we have seen our supply chain be pretty stable and our sales have been steady.
As of today we are trending +14% in annual Revenue compared to 2021 and we expect to maintain or even increase this trend as we move into our busiest time of the year.  We have seen an increase in our international wholesale business with the addition of new distribution partners, and we have also launched 2 new product collections since the close of our crowdfunding campaign.
Our Rescue & Repair Collection was launched in June and it has been selling well all summer and into the holiday season.  This collection gives us products in the Bond Strengthening category and helps to set us apart when compared to other indie brands.  To date Inahsi Naturals is the only small indie brand to have its own Bond Strengthening product line.
We are also launching our new Moisture Supreme Collection this month and into November as we lead up to BFCM (Black Friday Cyber Monday).  This product line appeals to our customers seeking Fragrance Free and Protein Free products that also maximize moisture which is one of the key pain points for consumers with curly textured hair.
As usual we have big plans for the end of this year as well as 2023.  Our products will be featured at the Beauty World Exhibition Middle East in Dubai which is being held this year from October 31st thru November 2nd.  This is the largest Beauty Trade Show in the Middle East and we are excited to have our products showcased at this impressive event.
We will also be expanding our product offerings on Amazon in Q4 and into Q1 2023 as we continue to expand our customer base in the United States and Canada.  We have some cool content for our curly textured hair community in the works as well and we are working on another potential showcase for Inahsi Naturals products at CurlyCon 2023 in Toronto, Canada in April of 2023.
As you can see our plate is full and we are busy serving our customers and continuing to get the word out about Inahsi Naturals all over the Globe.  Thank you all for your investment and support, we are happy to have you in our #InahsiFam and excited about the growth to come as we wind down 2022 and enter 2023.
Brian
Brian Marshall
Inahsi Naturals
