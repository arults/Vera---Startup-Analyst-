Beacons 2.0 and Beacons 2023 Update
Hi Republic! Sharing more about what we’ve been working on this year below :)
Today we launched Beacons 2.0! Learn more and please do reshare with the links below.
LinkedIn
Twitter
Blog
Product
We’ve become an all in one platform for creators. Earlier this year, we launched our app store with 14 apps. Our apps span many different categories from income dashboard on the business management side to post activity tracker on the content side to pricing calculator on the brand deals side.
We are now focusing on 4 key apps:
Email marketing to help creators own their audience
Store to help creators monetize their audience
Link in bio for all creators
Beacons for Managers to help creator managers better manage their creators
We’re integrating AI in the platform as well. We’ve added AI to onboarding, have dedicated apps built on generative AI (such as the AI brand deal outreach), and have the Beacons Cortex that is a shared data layer across the entire platform serving as the system of record for each creator.
Growth
We are now at over 3.6 million creator users! We’re also quickly growing our email marketing, store, and manager product user bases.
Team
We are now 40 people full time! We recently had our team offsite in San Diego.
Exciting Announcements
We launched our
top creator agencies
earlier this year. We built the list from our database of creator managers. We used the list to generate inbound interest for our manager product. The list has gotten over 12K views since launch.
As mentioned, we launched Beacons 2.0, our biggest ever product launch. Through Beacons 2.0, we announced our all in one platform, AI features, revamped link in bio, and Beacons for Managers. Links to share again below:

Asks
