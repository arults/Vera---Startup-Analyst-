📈Investor Dashboard and Perks, Buy Bitcoin Live, PLUS a lot of New Video Content
I want to start things off by covering all of the perks and dashboard functionality our investors have been patiently waiting for.  It took a few extra weeks to actually close things out with Republic as we were letting people in from the waitlist.  However, that's all done and we're ready to move forward!
Investor Dashboard
The investor dashboard is live and all 3,719 of you should have received an email from Securitize.  Securitize is our transfer agent who not only powers our dashboard, but also enables Ternio to digitize our securities in the future if we decide to take that path.
Ternio Investors
can access their dashboard from our "Investors" page or by clicking the link below.  Please take a few minutes to set everything up as you will want to have a record of your investment.
👉
Ternio Investor Dashboard
Investor Perks
Many of you also qualified for Investor perks including RFID wallets, Ternio T-shirts, and more.  Depending on your investment tier, some very early investors also qualified for our
"
Top 100 Investors
"
page.  The items were shipped out this week so (depending on where you live) you should be receiving them in the next 1-2 weeks.  The Top Investors page is also live.   Thank you all again so much for your support in Ternio!
Buy Bitcoin Live
Our
Buy Bitcoin
feature is live for US users and being tested with a group of early
BlockCard Bank Account
users.   Things have been going great so far and we are continuously opening it to additional BlockCard Accounts.  If you want to try it out, please make sure you have a BlockCard Bank Account that is funded with US Dollars.
Our Buy Bitcoin feature is very unique, as we enable our users to seamlessly purchase Bitcoin (BTC) with their USD BlockCard Bank Account balance, then provide near instant settlement of Bitcoin purchases to their self-custodial wallet.
In the coming weeks we will also open this same "Buy Bitcoin" capability to International residents (with the exception of those on the OFAC list).  Our goal is to become one of the standard platforms anyone in the world can use to purchase BTC!
Video Content
We have continued to push out a lot of video content surrounding all of Ternio's initiatives.  The first is our continued expansion of the
BlockCard Learn video series
.   We added an additional 13 videos that are now available on our youtube channel (bringing the total to 36 so far).  We have more in production now and will continue to roll these out to help educate the broader cryptocurrency ecosystem on the benefits of different cryptocurrencies, as well as BlockCard.
Daniel Interview on GD Payments
In the last update, I shared one of our white-label partners - GD Payments.  There has been a lot of interest on GDP and how it works, so Daniel went on Jason Appleton's youtube channel to give an overview of all things related to BlockCard as well as GDP.   It's a great educational watch if you're curious on how some of our partners are using Ternio's platform.
GD Payments Interview:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4RhDEdLowLo
Litecoin Card Announcement
In the last update, I mentioned that Litecoin Foundation had announced the upcoming Litecoin Card.  The inaugural Litecoin Series video is now up, so you can watch me discuss some details about the upcoming card.   The entire video is 56 min, but only about the first 10 min is specifically related to the Litecoin Card.
The Litecoin Card website is live and we're finishing up the testing.  Phase 1 will be releasing the Litecoin Card to early supporters and then move to Phase 2 which is all US users.
Litecoin Card Announcement:
https://youtu.be/gtJcpgMRfxg?t=103
Concluding With A Teaser
In the last 2 weeks we finalized a significant partnership with a multi-billion dollar company that everyone at Ternio is very excited about.  We're still working through the details of what we can share and how we can share it.  I personally believe this could be Ternio's biggest announcement of 2020 and will put us on an amazing trajectory going into 2021. 😎
While I do hate to leave everyone hanging, I did want to call out special attention that the next Republic update you receive from Ternio will be one you should pay special attention to.
That's it for now.
GREAT
things on the horizon!
Ian Kane
