Additional Partners, Litecoin Card Live, More press, Upcoming AMA, and More!  🚀
2020 isn't over yet and we're pushing hard to end the year with a
bang!
I have a bunch of updates I'm happy to share with you today, but don't worry - this
won't be the last update
of the year.  Let's kick things off!
The Litecoin Card is live for all US residents!
As you know, we've been working for a long time to get the
Litecoin Card
live.  I'm happy to report that anyone can signup now, fund their account, pass KYC, and get a Litecoin Card.   Overall, there has been a lot of excitement for the card in the first week, and I anticipate this growing in the weeks to come!
Charlie Lee is the founder of Litecoin and sent out the following tweet this week....
Using my Litecoin Card at Starbucks!
Here is a quick video I made demonstrating the ease of use of the Litecoin Card for everyday purchases.   I also talk briefly about some of the other functionality the card offers.
New White-label Partner!
Ternio's newest white-label partner is QuiverX, a crowdfunding platform that leverages blockchain.   QuiverX Capital provides a platform that "gives the everyday investor a chance to earn like a pro."  They will be leveraging our card platform as a way to give their customers an easy way to spend digital currencies!
You can read more about the partnership here:
https://quiverx.medium.com/quiverx-quiverx-ternio-blockcard-partnership-71213d19971a
More Press!
There is a great article in Tech Bullion titled:
The Ternio Story, How This Small FinTech Is Revolutionizing Payments With Digital Currency
Please take a change to read it and share it with anyone you think could benefit!
BitBoy Crypto
BitBoy Crypto is a popular Youtuber with a big crypto following.  He recently made a video about one of our partners GD Payments and BlockCard's integration.  He talks about Ternio a bit as well as BlockCard as a product.  You can check it out here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zu6rVvQBcVk&feature=emb_title
Ask Me Anything (Ian+Daniel Live Stream)
I know most of you have only become familiar with Ternio this year.  However, every year Daniel and I typically do a year in review video.  You can check out previous
Ternio Year In Review videos
on our youtube channel.   If you have about 45 minutes to spare, it may be interesting to see how Ternio started and where we are today.
A lot has changed!
I am planning to publish a 2020 Year In Review in mid-December, so stay tuned for that. 😎
However, this year we wanted to mix it up a bit and do a LiveStream AMA to give people a chance to ask us questions live on youtube.  It's something we did in 2018 when we launched Ternio, but haven't done another in almost 3 years.
When: December 18th, 2020
Time: 3pm Eastern Time (8pm Universal Time)
Where: Ternio's youtube channel ->
https://www.youtube.com/c/TernioToken/
However, I know all of you may not be available to attend in real time, so I created a Reddit thread where you can post any questions you have ahead of time.   We will do our best to address everyone's questions.
Ternio AMA Reddit Thread:
Post your questions
https://www.reddit.com/r/TernioToken/comments/k4qtoo/post_your_questions_ternio_livestream_ama_with/
That's it for now, but stay tuned for 1 or 2 more updates before the end of the year.  As I said above, we're going to finish the year strong! 💪
