New Features, New Partners, & More.  Don't miss this update! 🔥
Hi Everyone - Ian here with another big update.  Let's get right into it!
3 New Dashboard Features
Our team released a number of new features within the
BlockCard Dashboard
since the last update.  This includes the following:
Link your BlockCard Bank Account
to another Bank Account
using Plaid
.  Great for easily transferring USD between bank accounts.
Google Authenticator 2FA for
added account security
.  This is the standard in the crypto industry and something our users have been asking about for a while.
UNI
(uniswap) and
CRO
(cro coin) as
depositing assets
to fund your BlockCard account.
We're Hiring 💼
Do you know someone who could be a great fit for our team?  Have them check out the Ternio careers page.  We're hiring for a number of roles right now such as
marketing, communications and development
, but will also be scaling up even more after our next raise.   A good referral goes a long way in helping us build an incredible team to take Ternio to the next level.
You can find all the open roles here:
https://careers.ternio.io/
By the way, we also made a fun
video meme
for hiring on social media 😀
New White-Label Partner: Haven
Haven Protocol is our newest white-label partner (that I can share) and will be launching the
Haven Card
.  The card will spend their asset xUSD.
You can see their announcement on Twitter:
https://twitter.com/HavenXHV/status/1361412806621335552?s=20
or read more on their blog
https://havenprotocol.org/2021/02/15/haven-xusd-card/
More to come on this partnership (and many others)!
Daniel and Ian interview with PYMNTS
Daniel and I sat down with Karen Webster, the CEO of PYMNTS to talk about a few things going on in the cryptocurrency space, but most important -
Ternio's upcoming Reg A+
.   This was a great interview and I highly recommend taking 20 minutes to watch it when you can.
https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2021/ternio-enlists-users-as-alternate-route-to-raising-growth-capital/
Rebrand: A New Ternio is coming! 🚀
If you watched the PYMNTS video above then you probably saw the mention of a rebrand.  I wanted to save the best for last!  Over the course 2021 we will be rebranding Ternio and BlockCard and bringing them together under one new brand.   We have already acquired a one-word .com domain and are in process of setting everything up related to this new brand.
We believe that this new brand will be the name that
takes Ternio, LLC to billion-dollar company status.
We're making this change for a number of reasons, but ultimately believe this new brand will speak to the plethora of services we are currently offering (or plan to later this year).   It's something we're very excited about and I will be sharing more as it becomes relevant in the coming months.
This rebrand will take place throughout 2021 and we want to ensure that all of our investors, customers, and partners are part of this journey!  The transition will happen gradually but will mark a new era for our company.
That's it for now, but PLENTY more coming in the future 😎
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
