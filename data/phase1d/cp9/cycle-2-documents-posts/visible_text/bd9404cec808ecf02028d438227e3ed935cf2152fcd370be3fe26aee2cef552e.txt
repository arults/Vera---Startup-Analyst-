Updates
Jul  5
2020
Thank you
We did it!
With your help, we were able to raise over $200,000! It took a lot of hard work, patience, but most importantly, it took you believing in us - in our mission, and today the real work begins. It's time for us to go harder, grow more prominent, and show you our investors that your reward will be worth the risk.
Here's what you can look forward to as our new investor:
Investor Perk: As stated on our campaign with each level of investment, you receive several "thank you" perks. Once the campaign's logistics are settled, you can expect your gifts in the mail within the next 30 - 60 days. FYI, your initial investment perk of 20% off our sauce, will expire on August 1st CST.
Sienna Sauce Subscription Program: Customers will now be able to purchase Sienna Sauce on subscription, launching this month. Our sauce lovers can choose between 3 flavors of the sauce, sent to them regularly at the frequency of their choosing. The subscription program will include our new e-cookbook (to the first 100 subscribers), free shipping, and packaged in our new boxes!
New Product Launch: This one is a secret, but since you're now apart of the Sienna Sauce family, we're letting you in on it. This quarter we'll be launching a new product! We can't share too many details, but we're in the final production stages, and super excited for you all to see it. You can look forward to more exciting news about our new product!
Now being an investor, you must be wondering what we plan to do with your contribution. First on the agenda is growing our team. Our sauce may have big flavor, but we're still a small business that's proliferating. As we grow, your capital will go towards hiring staff to keep Sienna Sauce running efficiently and competing in the ever-expanding condiment industry. Speaking of growing - remember that new product launch we mentioned above, well, that's just the first of many! We're going to take the condiment industry by storm with our unique flavors, and with you backing us, Sienna Sauce will do just that. To ensure our success, marketing and advertising of the Sienna Sauce brand is a must. As we've already started expanding our marketing efforts, your investment will further develop Sienna Sauce's marketing team and strategy.
Lastly, we want to say Thank you! You all will never know just how much your investment means to us, but we'll do our best to show you. We promise to plant your investment and grow nothing but greatness in return. You'll be hearing from soon, and don't forget to Live, Love and Sauce it up!
The Sienna Sauce Team
Monique Crayton
Sienna Sauce
