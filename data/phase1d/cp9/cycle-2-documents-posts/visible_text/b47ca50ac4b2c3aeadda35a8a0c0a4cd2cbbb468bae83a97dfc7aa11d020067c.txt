Ember Fund | Company Update
Hi,
It's been a while so I just wanted to give an update and check in with the community. Also, we just received the final list of investors, so we will be working on sending out perks in the next few weeks.
Before we jump into it.
Want to help us grow?
Go to the
Apple
or
Google Play
store from your mobile device and leave us a 5-star review if you love the app.
If you want to chat with us, join our Telegram group
here
.
A few company metrics
We had the biggest month ever in terms of transaction volume in May processing well over $3 million.
We had the biggest week earlier this month in terms of number of deposits. Almost 300 unique deposits.
May 2020 was the biggest month in company history in terms of revenue.
What we're working on
As promised, we've completely re-designed the app. More UI/UX upgrades in progress.
We're testing different growth channels and are seeing some good results.
Continuing to work on our smart contract technology. We will be in testing next week.
Challenges
Continuing to produce compelling content to educate and align users on the goal of our Quant portfolio.
We're growing but attribution (figuring exactly which channels are working) continues to be a challenge.
Cheers,
Alex
Alex Wang
