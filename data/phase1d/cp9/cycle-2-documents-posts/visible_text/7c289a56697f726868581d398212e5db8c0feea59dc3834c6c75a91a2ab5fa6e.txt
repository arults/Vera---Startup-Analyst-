The path to becoming the first ever Social Search platform  (In response to many of your questions)
Many of you are asking questions surrounding “social search” and whether our vision for Applaudable is more of a Social Media or an Online Search platform. I thought I’d try to combine answers to all related questions here for all to see. I strongly recommend reviewing this for a better understanding of the process and its significance.
To answer questions about utility first... The way I see it, the greatest potential for Applaudable is that it becomes a medium for intent-based search for a broad range of products, places, brands, and activities. We believe we are truly on a path to revolutionizing online search by having search results represented entirely by authentic, visual user general experiences, rather than by advertiser or promotional content. That said, the path to this is through a social network that allows users to authentically and objectively share their greatest experiences, and discover (through a feed) those of others based on relevant interests. This is exactly how we started with Moro, focusing on the interiors and home furnishings categories, where we quickly achieved critical mass. As critical mass is now achieved within additional individual categories, feed tags will be shown to enable filtering within feeds. This would be the final stage before ultimately enabling search functionality when the content and data support likely relevant results.
Based on extensive testing, I'm very confident in our ability to grow users, and so most of our energy and resources focus on the value these users will both produce for and get from the platform. From a sharing standpoint, the three tenets for me are:
Awareness
- For an active platform user, the process of starting to consciously even think about the experiences they're lucky to have, is a benefit in and of itself, and a contrast to how most people operate/appreciate today.  You may have heard me say in one of the videos: "Awareness and appreciation of what has meaning is actually what gives it meaning" - This is obviously true for all of that we're lucky to have.
Recognition
- the act of sharing great experiences improves the experience, hence is why platforms like Instagram are successful.
Altruism
- In addition to sharing offering similar benefits to sharing on IG or anywhere else, I believe there is additional value in being able to share experiences in a way that makes the same experience possible for others. This is something we'll report against so users understand the impact their posts have on others (how many people read and loved xyz book because of my Applauding it).
These are the benefits of sharing. And maximizing this is our top priority beyond getting users to register. From a consumption standpoint, as I mentioned, initially through a discovery feed, users will be shown the most relevant experiences of other users, until we reach critical mass to begin allowing some more control of what they see using filters and ultimately search functionality.
Regarding the business model, Affiliate which makes feed/search results accessible on-platform (similar to how purchases of tagged/shoppable items work on IG) is what I believe is right, both in the early stages, but also in a fully actualized version of the platform. I believe it is the greatest value add for users and more scalable than an e-comm play that would warrant our involvement with the user/consumer beyond the point of sale.
We appreciate the questions - please keep them coming.
Very Best, Andrew Christodoulides, Founder & CEO
