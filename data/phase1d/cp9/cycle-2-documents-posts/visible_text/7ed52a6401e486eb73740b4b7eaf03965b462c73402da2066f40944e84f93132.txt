Creating Value – work, Innovation, Progress!
To all ACFN SAFE investors,
Thank you for participating in our Crowdfunding campaign. As you know the campaign closed March 23
rd
this year and we have been hard at work so forwarding this update to bring you up to speed on progress made.
Hot off the press
We just signed a
national deal with Brookfield Properties
one of the largest global retail players with a portfolio encompassing an estimated 134,000,000 square feet of space. As a first wave we will be installing ATMs in 21 Malls across multiple states in the next 30 days. There are many additional malls that will be converting over to ACFN as current agreements expire mostly over the next 12 months. Cumulatively by the end of 2024 we expect this deal will increase our transaction volume by an estimated 5% and have a larger impact on bottom line earnings. These types of locations tend to be higher volume, are very attractive to advertisers and can generate additional income from Dynamic Currency Conversion (“DCC”) and Bitcoin transactions. After initial deployment is completed successfully we plan to present additional products and services to Brookfield such as our Cell Phone Battery Rental Stations and Digital Pharmacy products to continue to build the relationship and increase long-term value.
Innovations - new product in testing
We are currently researching and testing Vape Vending. This is a smart compact vending unit that is able to perform digital ID checks for buyers to confirm that they are 21 years old before a product is dispensed. We think there could be a lot of interest in this product from entertainment venues we currently work with.
Growth - acquisitions opportunities
One of our long-time competitors is for sale at this time and we are engaged in conversation to determine if this transaction would be a good fit for ACFN. We expect multiple additional opportunities to acquire large and small competitors between now and the end of 2024 when a required hardware upgrade needs to be completed. The associated cost and time required in combination with current wage inflations, high interest rates and high fuel costs will drive some operators to sell in the next 12 months. Acquisitions are a competitive process but having already completed two successful acquisitions in 2018 and 2020 we have a clear and proven strategy and expect to be able to secure multiple acquisitions.
Capital Initiatives
One of the key elements of a successful acquisitions strategy to achieve rapid growth and build value for shareholders is access to capital. We are currently in active conversations with two Private Equity firms. Both have large funds that invest in companies to drive growth and build value. We are just starting our work in this area and will report more about this in future as things develop.
Independently we are also researching requirements and qualifications to do a capital event is Canda and/or the UK. We have contacts that are experienced in this area and are assisting ACFN to reach the right people and gather information. This is forward-looking and will be applicable after we complete two or more acquisitions and scale the business.
More updates to come in future months as we continue to grow our business and progress towards a capital event.
Jeffrey D. Kerr
American Consumer Financial Network
