QuickLoadz appearence on Podcast
A freight startup which was not doing well pivoted to providing a dedicated news outlet for logistics and freight. I was of the "That will never work" opinion. It was a long struggle but FreightWaves has emerged into that role and is the goto for people in the industry. Super impressive what the founder Craig Fuller did. Freightwaves has done a couple of more pivots and most of their revenue comes from subscriptions to the massive amount of data that they collect in real time, it is called "Sonar". Once again, really impressive.
Their most popular Podcast started out as a joky show called What The Truck?!? Which although entertaining, was mainly a complaint fest from truck drivers. It has evolved into a really interesting show that focuses on various startups and what are the trends in freight. Tim Dooner the producer keeps it interesting while  doing a great job of finding different ways to see what is going on in the logistics industry. Tim interviews everyone from pallet manufacturers, who see the trends in advance to autonomous truck to Amazon store owners. Really interesting variety of information.
QuickLoadz appeared on the show for a 3rd time yesterday for a live demo during FreightWaves Global Supply Chain Week 2023. FreightWaves does a lot of conferences, really impressive how well run they are. QuickLoadz did one in Bentonville AR where I loaded a sea shipping container live at our plant in Athens from the stage in Bentonville.
Yesterday's appearance was a nice bit of free promo. Tim the host likes having us on because we keep it entertaining. Here is the clip that he has reposted all over the place.
Please enjoy the clip where you get a quick tour of QuickLoadz, see a customer's truck load a container for the first time. Please repost it anywhere you can.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3mbYUGsMk4
