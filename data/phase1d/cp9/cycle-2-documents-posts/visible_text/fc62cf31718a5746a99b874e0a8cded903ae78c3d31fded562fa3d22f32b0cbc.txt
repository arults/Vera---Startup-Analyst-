AlphaFlow DOUBLES Best Month Ever in May!
Hello everyone,
AlphaFlow has closed out May and we've had a massive growth swing, beating our previous record month by 2.14x! This performance level puts us at over a $7M run rate revenue just on bridge loans, and we'll be adding rental loans soon. Our CEO, Ray Sturm, shares the full update in the short video below:
With this massive growth we have received tremendous support from our institutional backers and they also want to participate in this raise. If you’ve invested already, you’re in! We’re going to keep the campaign open for a week or two more to let the Republic community participate in this growth but we’ll likely cap things around $1M-1.5M here. We won’t cut out people who invest before we close the raise, so if you’re in, we’ll honor that investment and welcome you to our extended AlphaFlow Family (clearing Republic's compliance, of course). Thank you all for the amazing support! We're just getting going.
- The AlphaFlow Team
Ray Sturm
