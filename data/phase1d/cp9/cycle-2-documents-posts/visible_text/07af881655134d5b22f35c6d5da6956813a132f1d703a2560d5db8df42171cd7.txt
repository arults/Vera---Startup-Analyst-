BLUE is going LIVE on UniSwap July 1st 2024! 🦄 *UPDATED POST*
Dear Republic Investors,
I hope this posts finds you well and filled with excitement for the journey ahead with Blue Social.
I'm writing to share some thrilling updates regarding the launch of our BLUE token on UniSwap. After careful consideration and exciting developments, we've decided to move the launch date to
July 1st, 2024
.
Why the change, you might ask? Well, I'm excited to inform you that we've received significant interest from Crypto VC's who are eager to invest in our project. While I can't disclose all the details just yet, rest assured that some exciting moves are happening behind the scenes to drive the growth and development of Blue Social to new heights.
This extra time allows us to create a more impactful market presence on platforms like Twitter and Discord before the launch. We'll be hosting Twitter Spaces to engage with the crypto community and get them involved in our project. It's an incredible opportunity to build momentum and excitement leading up to our launch.
But what about your tokens? Don't worry – the tokens you've claimed will still be airdropped into your wallet shortly after the
Token Generation Event
(TGE) listing on UniSwap. This delay gives us the chance to ensure everything is in place for a successful launch and allows us to make a bigger splash in the market.
Thank you for your understanding and continued support. Together, we're shaping the future of social networking with Blue Social.
Stay tuned for more updates and announcements as we countdown to our new launch date. The best is yet to come!
Warm regards,
Jose Montero CEO, Blue Social
P.s. Make sure to claim your airdrop tokens here if you have not yet.
https://claim.blue.social
ONLY if you were part of our Republic Campaign. If not, you will not receive airdrop.
Jose Montero
