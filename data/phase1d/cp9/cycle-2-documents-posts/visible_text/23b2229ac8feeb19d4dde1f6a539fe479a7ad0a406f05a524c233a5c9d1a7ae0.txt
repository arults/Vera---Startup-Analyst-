Updates
Nov 11
2020
New White-Label Partner, Additional Press, and more! 🚀
Hi Everyone - Things have been going great at Ternio, especially after our
VISA Fast Track
announcement! There are a handful of great things I want to share, so let's get right into it.
Paxful Debit Card
I'm happy to share that Ternio will be powering
Paxful's upcoming cryptocurrency debit card
, using our BlockCard platform. Paxful is one of the leading Bitcoin marketplaces,
with almost 5,000,000 users across the world
. Paxful users will get an elegant way to quickly spend their cryptocurrencies at tens of millions of merchants globally! Launching first for residents in the US, then internationally.
You can signup at
http://paxfulcard.com/
Gilded and Ternio Partnership
We have also partnered with Gilded for B2B payments.  Gilded users can accept payment in Bitcoin, Ethereum, and stablecoins—directly to their BlockCard account. When you invoice with Gilded and accept crypto payments with your BlockCard, you can spend your crypto instantly with zero transaction fees.
https://ternio.io/ternio-gilded-are-breaking-down-crypto-barriers/
Read more on our blog and be sure to check out the video below!
GDPayments and Crypto Crow
This is actually a big win for one of our partners,
GDPayments who integrated BlockCard
.  Obviously when our partners win, Ternio does as well so I'm happy to share!  Crypto Crow is a well known influencer in the space and has been working with GDPayments to promote their product and ultimately has made a good bit of money from their program by referring new users to the program which ultimately enroll for a BlockCard.
You can read more about GDPayments here or watch the video below.
https://www.serendipitytech.io/blog/2020/7/30/ternio-partners-with-gdpayments-offering-even-more-functionality-to-the-blockcard
Press & More
There is a great article in the International Business Times about
Ternio
titled
"How Ternio's BlockCard Platform Is Becoming The Standard For Digital Currency Debit Cards"
Ternio has opened up new possibilities for the crypto and FinTech businesses around the world by becoming the “Xerox” of the 2020s. Back in the early 90s when Xerox was launched, people would not ask for a photocopy of a document but instead say “Xerox it”. Very similar to this, people are now saying “BlockCard” when referring to crypto debit cards instead of asking for making a payment any other way for cryptos. This is such a big achievement for the Ternio team as they have become the ideal digital currency debit card platform.
Please share the above when you have time 👆
BlockCard Learn Videos:
Part of working in the digital currency space is helping educate newcomers on all of the different digital currencies on the market. We have release a bunch of additional
BlockCard Learn
videos on our YouTube Channel and Blog.  We're now up to 61 in total and have some additional ones in the works!
Here is the whole playlist:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5KZFxrYeDwo&list=PLFQirr7pBWb520mjyY0wdiWDyS5BYw3MW
That's all for now.  We're hard at work on some other great things which I hope to be able to share by the end of this year.   As 2020 begins to wind down I can't help, but look back at what Ternio has accomplished.   What we're doing is no easy task, so I couldn't be more proud of our team and how far they have taken Ternio.   I'm even more excited about what 2021 has in store.   2020 has by far been our best year, but from what I'm seeing..... 2021 is going to be
even bigger
! 😎
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
