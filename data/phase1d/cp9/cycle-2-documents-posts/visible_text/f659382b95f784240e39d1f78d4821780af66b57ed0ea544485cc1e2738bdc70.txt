Update on New Games Added to the Portfolio
Hello Everyone,
We wanted to update you on the state of Fig Portfolio Shares, and the games we will be funding with them. We’ve had some exciting new games join us since we last updated you all. As you know, this campaign’s goal is to help fund at least ten game projects. And we have lined up some awesome projects thus far.
First, let me recap and provide a bit more details on some of the projects already mentioned in this campaign:
Wizardy 1 - 3 Re-releases
The Wizardry games were known as one of the most popular role playing games of the time, and helped establish the genre worldwide. These games have become near impossible to find and play, so we are extremely excited to help bring them back to this generation.
Rose City Games - New IP
Rose City Games is known for developing The World Next Door and Floppy Knights, and publishing Garden Story. All of which have received glowing reviews and stellar press. We are very excited to be working with them on a brand new IP game project.
A Long Journey to an Uncertain End
This is a narrative space opera where you recruit eclectic crew members, explore the unknown, travel the galaxy, and make real choices that drive your journey. It is being developed by Crispy Creative, a new developer formed from former developers from Obsidian, Riot, Telltale Games, Bungie, and more.
Now let me tell you about some of the new games joining the Fig Portfolio Shares:
Party for Introverts - New IP
Party for Introverts is a game studio formed by Arseniy Klishin, former  manager of production for PlayStation, and Laura Gray, acclaimed voice-actor for Horizon Forbidden West, Thing-in-Itself, and From Head to Toe. Together they have created several interactive short-stories that have been nominated for numerous awards. We are very excited to be working with them on their new IP game project, which is a narrative role-playing game.
Clam Man 2: Headliner
Clam Man 2: Headliner is a stand-up comedy CRPG by Martin Hanses/marafrass where jokes are loot and shows are boss fights. In 2020, a free prologue to Headliner called Open Mic was released to highly positive reviews, and was played over 50,000 times, garnering thousands of wishlists. Clam Man 2: Headliner builds on the already critically praised Open Mic with more content, deeper RPG mechanics, and multiple endings and main quests to choose from
Three Other Game Projects - Pending
Fig is working with three other game developers on remastering a beloved classic RPG, helping rerelease a beloved LGBTQ game from the 90’s, and bringing to market a new social deduction game. And beyond those, we are actively
seeking out 1 - 2 additional game
projects to add to this portfolio. We are thrilled to share more details shortly.
We hope you’re as excited as we are about bringing some of these games to light. And we can’t wait to share more details with you.
Lastly, we wanted to share that
Fig has a new campaign from a VERY well known videogame brand that should be starting in the coming weeks
.
Ash Leavenworth
Fig Portfolio Shares
