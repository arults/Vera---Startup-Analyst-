IP3 Q2 Update – July 2025
Laying the Tracks for What’s Next
We’re using this summer to continue building the infrastructure and partnerships that will allow us to hit the ground running in the fall. From commerce and distribution to creative production and platform development, every move is designed to support the long-term vision of IP3 and the creators we work with.
🔧 Comics + Collectibles Infrastructure
We’ve been building out the foundation for our presence in the collectibles space:
• Development is underway for Amazon and eBay storefronts to support future creator drops, IP-branded products, and exclusive releases.
•  We’ve secured a partnership with GTS Distribution, one of the largest distributors in North America for trading cards, games, and collectibles. GTS supplies over 2,000 retailers — opening a direct path to the hobby ecosystem.
📍 Comic-Con International
We’ll be on the ground at Comic-Con International this month with a packed schedule of meetings. It’s a key moment for us to connect with creators, collaborators, and potential brand partners across entertainment, comics, and collectibles — all aligned with our mission to support original IP from the ground up.
🤝 Creator + Platform Partnerships
•  We’re advancing our partnership with Pixel Canvas, focused on immersive, interactive storytelling formats.
•  Additional creator conversations are in motion, with more to share as we ramp toward the fall.
🌏 IP3 Asia – Expanding the Footprint
While our Tokyo office is now slated for Q4, we’ve already begun building the foundation for IP3 Asia through new production partnerships:
• We’re in active discussions with a short-form content studio and an animation partner based in Asia. Both are designed to be AI- and Web3-inclusive, giving us an edge in testing new production pipelines and staying ahead of industry disruption.
🏙️ Concept Store & Pop-Up Strategy
Plans for our flagship concept store are still in motion. We’re currently working to secure locations and brand partnerships for pop-up experiences to test format, audience, and product-market fit. Two pilot sites are in development: one in the DTLA Arts District and one in the heart of Culver City — both selected for their creative energy and connection to our core community
Thank you again for being part of this journey. We’re focused on building smart, staying aligned with creators, and setting the stage for everything to come.
Michael Cheng
