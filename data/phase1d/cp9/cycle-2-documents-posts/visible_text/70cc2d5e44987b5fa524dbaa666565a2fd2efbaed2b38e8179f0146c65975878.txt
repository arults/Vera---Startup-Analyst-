Announcing AlphaFlow's New Digital Lender Platform!
Today we are so excited to share that
AlphaFlow
has launched our new Digital Lender Platform! We believe local private lenders are the lifeblood of residential real estate investing. These lenders are where real estate developers go to fund their projects and over the last decade, their role in the U.S. real estate industry has exploded. Our new platform now makes it easier than ever to sell their loans and grow their businesses. Take a look:
You can read our full post on the new platform launch
HERE
and see the press release
HERE
. Thank you for all of your support and for those who have already liked and shared our LinkedIn post, we truly appreciate it!
If you were not able to invest in this round but wanted to do so, we still have an
accredited investor
offering page up until February 15th. Thank you all and have a fantastic week!
- The AlphaFlow Team
