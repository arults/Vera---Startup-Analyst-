Round closing and what’s next
As this stage of our Series A closes, I wanted to first express excitement and gratitude to each of you. We have deep conviction in what we’re building, and the significance of how much our user growth has validated market demand can’t be overstated. None of this would be possible without each of you. I thank you for your belief and support. Many of you are colleagues, family, friends, and partners. Many others I don’t know personally. I feel a deep sense of obligation to each of you, and I am committed to making your investments extraordinarily successful.
We are wrapping up the offering on Republic today and will be sharing details on what’s next for the company, including, of course, the public release of Applaudable. For now, I wanted to share some very exciting news briefly. We have reached a verbal agreement to expand our product team to include 8-10 engineers, designers, and product strategists, who led key development for Triller, which achieved a $5 billion valuation. We hope to have this finalized in the coming days. This product team expansion and the immense experience they bring will help ensure we deliver the type of product our tens of thousands of users expect. With the level of user growth we’ve already experienced, delivering a great user experience, I believe, is the single most important thing we can do to drive enterprise value. And you’ve seen the comps to Clubhouse and others.
Stay tuned.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
Founder & CEO
