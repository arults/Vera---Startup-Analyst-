Updates
Dec 21
2021
The last update of 2021, AND not one to miss! 🔥🚨
It's hard to believe this year is almost over.   2021 was a great year for Unbanked and we grew at an amazing rate (in terms of revenue and headcount).   While this past year was awesome, I'm even more excited for what 2022 has in store.    However, before I formally end 2021 for Unbanked, I wanted to share some updates that are important.
The Bankers NFT Drop
Unbanked will be releasing an NFT (non-fungible token) collection called
"Bankers."
We commissioned a collection of 10,000 unique digital collectibles on the Ethereum blockchain from Antoine Mingo. If you're not familiar, Antoine is the digital artist behind Pudgy Penguins and now the Bankers NFT collection. His last NFT drop reached a market cap of over 12,000 ETH with select pieces selling for 130 ETH!
Antoine and I did an interview which you can watch below:
Still not sure what an NFT is?  I wrote an article in IB Times titled "
NFTs Are More Than Just JPEGs. They Are The Future Of Community, Access, And Ownership
".  It may help clarify.
You may recall in the last update I shared the upcoming release of UNBNK.com.  Owning a “Banker” NFT will give you free lending/borrowing access on the UNBNK protocol - for life.  In addition, you will own the IP rights to the Bankers you purchase.
Visit the Bankers NFT page on Unbanked to get more details:
https://unbanked.com/bankers/
Early Access - Mint Pass
We want to give Unbanked customers the best chance possible of getting a Banker. You can read more about getting a Mint Pass ahead of the drop.
https://unbanked.com/the-bankers-nft-mint-pass-secure-your-bankers-in-advance/
On Monday, December 20th, we will launch a special Mintpass for 72 hours, with a flat price of 0.087 ETH (approximately $350). This will allow anyone interested in a Banker to get in before the public sale.
https://unbanked.com/bankers/mint-pass
👈
purchase link
Mintpass holders will be able to mint a Banker on the day of the
public sale, Monday, January 31, 2022,
or anytime thereafter.
The public sale of the Bankers drop will be similar to a dutch auction.  This means we start with the highest bid first to initiate the Banker sale. The price will continue to decline until all Bankers are sold out or the floor price is reached.  Bidding will start at $800 worth of ETH and decline every 30 minutes until it reaches a floor of $200 worth of ETH.
Once the public sale is live, any Bankers left over after 72 hours will be burned
There has been a lot of excitement around our NFT drop so I look forward to sharing more updates in 2022 as the community and utility around Bankers expands.
Unbanked Vault
In 2022, we are releasing a new product called
Unbanked Vault
! This will allow anyone, anywhere in the world to stake their UNBNK and earn more UNBNK. The more you stake, the more you make. Unbanked offers one of the most competitive rates in the industry thanks to the UNBNK staking program. Reward tiers will go from 1% all the way to 8% based on your UNBNK holdings.
Staking Terms:
6-month lock for all tiers.
XLM UNBNK is staked, rewards are paid on ERC-20 UNBNK denominated in USD.
You can break the 6-month lock but will forfeit all rewards + pay a 10% “burn” tax on existing XLM UNBNK holdings.
After 6 months (and assuming no early withdraw) the customer receives their ERC-20 UNBNK
Capital Raise Update - Reg A+ / Reg CF
As you know, we have been going through the Reg A+ approval process with the SEC for many months.  It's a grueling process with many parties involved - Unbanked Team, SEC, 3rd party auditors, & 3rd party lawyers.   I had hoped that we would have been done with everything by now, but the holidays have slowed things down.  It's likely we are still a few months away from having everything ready and approved to take investment.
With that said, I've seen how eager people are to invest in Unbanked and we don't want to lose the momentum that we have established so far.  I want to ensure Unbanked continues to grow and we stay on the trajectory we have established.  Therefore, we're going to be filing for a
Reg-CF in January for a maximum capital raise of $5M.
The approval process to get live is much faster and will likely be in January to begin accepting investments. We will be using Republic again for this raise.
To be clear, Unbanked is still in process for the Reg A+.  The plan will be to raise the $5M via a Reg-CF in the near term and then potentially do an even larger raise via the Reg A+ at the end of Q1 or the beginning of Q2.   This Reg-CF gets Unbanked the capital we need to continue to grow revenue and headcount and will hopefully put us in an even better position for a larger capital raise (once approved).  The legal limit on a Reg A+ is $75M, but it's currently too early to determine how big of a raise we will do at the time.
That's all for now.  I hope you all have a safe and happy holiday and get to spend some much-needed time with friends and family.   On behalf of the entire Unbanked team, I want to thank each and every one of you for your ongoing support of Unbanked.  2021 was great, but 2022 will be even better!  See you next year.  🎄
