Michael Zwonitzer
Aloha Bruce!
We are regularly reviewing our menu and sales information to ensure there is sufficient appeal and demand for all our offerings.  In addition, we continue to consider new offerings that would increase guest demand and align with our operational efficiencies.
In the past we have introduced hand held options, however the demand did not warrant continuing the offering.  We will continue to be mindful of ways to increase our sales and expand our customer base, while maintaining our streamlined menu and speed of service.
Our franchisees contribute to a national marketing fund and we work closely with them to optimize how those dollars are spent.  While we are not able to disclose the specifics, contributions are inline with industry practices.
