Nanno Investor Update - September 2024
Hello, Nanno Investors,
We've gotten a lot of inquiries lately regarding the status of Nanno, so I thought it best to address them here.
At the end of 2021, we made the decision to pause operations and evaluate the best path forward for our company. At that time, COVID was still very much in effect, the child care market was in absolute chaos, and while we'd created a valuable platform used by many people, the impact of COVID made it seem impossible to scale the business as we'd always hoped. So, at that time, we decided to pursue two parallel strategies.
On one hand, we sold our ongoing operations to a company called Call Emmy, which is a similar on-demand marketplace platform, but unlike Nanno (which focused only on child care), Call Emmy focuses on myriad other aspects of the family-care market. Call Emmy was (and still is) early stage, so this was not a cash-out exit. In exchange for our technology and user base, we (Nanno) received a significant equity stake in Call Emmy, which we continue to hold in trust for our investors. We thought (and still think) of this as the best chance for Nanno's investors to eventually earn a return on their investment, though that return will likely not come until Call Emmy either exits or raises enough outside capital to redeem our investment (i.e. cash us out).
On the other hand, we retained the right to use and continue to develop our core technology, and to license it for use by other marketplace businesses outside the child-care and family-care markets.
So, the short story is that Nanno is still here, as a corporate entity, but is no longer operating our core child care platform.
All of this was communicated to investors as it happened, though I don't see those updates here, so I wanted to come and make sure the information is available for anyone who is looking for it.
Please feel free to reach out to me at
liz@nanno.com
if you would like more information.
Warmest regards,
Liz
Liz Oertle
Nanno
