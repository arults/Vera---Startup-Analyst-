New Milestone: Encubay Angel Network Makes Debut Investment Into Ready Set Jet plus more
Dear Ready Set Jetters -
Staying true to our mission, we are pleased to announce an investments of over $100k into Ready Set Jet from a number of dynamic and talented female angel investors.  The first being from Encubay Angel Network of 50 Lakh (approx $75,000.00)  as their debut investment into Ready Set Jet and the following from Mary Gaede, Antares Capital and Tammy Landes.
Encubay Angel Network, a network of angel investors, aspiring investors, entrepreneurs has made an investment to the tune of 50 Lakhs in Ready Set Jet.
Out of 10,000+ angel investors in India, only 1% percent are women. This is not due to lack of intent but due to lack of awareness. Staying true to its ethos of driving diversity in the start-up ecosystem, the Encubay Angel Network brings together women-led founders, founders building products for women, and individuals keen on investing in diverse start-ups with a keen focus on encouraging first-time investors.
Commenting on this, Deeksha Ahuja, Founder, Encubay,
says,  “Encubay has always looked at creating diversity in the most innovative way to level the playing field for women founders. Forming the Encubay Angel network to get more women to start investing as angels have been no exception. The network’s debut investment in Ready Set Jet Is an example of how diversity can create exponential value in the startup ecosystem. We are bullish on inclusive beauty and of course, Shalini’s grit makes for the kind of women founders we are inclined to back with capital, mentorship, and sisterhood of some amazing women!”
Encubay Angel Network is a strategic partner of Ready Set Jet in India and this investment in addition to the constant support and introductions aims at encouraging the common goal of both companies is building a sustainable ecosystem through diversity in people, profit, and building for the planet.
Shalini Vadhera, Founder, Ready Set Jet, says, “It has been an absolute pleasure working with Deeksha Ahuja and the Encubay Team. From strategically bringing us best-in-class angel investors to the incredible work ethic and passion for women lead companies, Deeksha and her team have gone above and beyond and continue to be a strong ally for the Ready Set Jet brand and mission. Working with the Encubay Angel Network has been a game changer for our business.  We look forward to continuing a very successful partnership.”
“Ready Set Jet ,as the name suggests, embodies all the ingredients for a company to soar high. An entrepreneur who not only has the credentials, vision, courage & confidence but also the unique ability to think global & act local. Shalini understands not only her products but the pulse of the Indian & global markets. The cosmetics industry in India is pegged to grow at 25% CAGR and touch 20 billion by 2025 and Ready Set Jet products and strategy is well placed to gain market share.”
Aarti Sinha, Director Account Management, Mastercard India Services Private Limited.
We are thrilled to welcome our newest investors:
Sushma Kaushik - Partner at Aavishkar
