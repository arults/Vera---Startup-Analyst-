Updates
Dec  1
2020
Greetings Reflex Protect Republic Investors!
I thought it a good time to offer a mid-quarter update on the exciting progress we’re continuing to make at Reflex Protect and to express my gratitude to you all for your ongoing support and encouragement. We filed a prior update with Republic at the end of Q3, but it doesn’t appear to have been forwarded to you all via email, so this is another good opportunity to get you up to date.
We’re on schedule and on track for Q4 to represent our full and successful re-entry into an economy that shut us out (along with much of the rest of the world) back in March through an empowered launch of Reflex Protect Tactical into a marketplace in demand of the products and services we now offer.
Here are a few highlights of where we stand now:
We have one final contract left to sign with a top-flight sales agency for the Upper Midwest and Midwest territories, after which we will have
complete national coverage
via seven experienced and effective representative organizations for law enforcement sales, from agencies and departments to dealers and distributors. These agreements represent no fewer than 40 new sales representatives covering the law enforcement market in all 50 states.
Notwithstanding the cancellation of SHOT Show, we still have plans for the Vegas Range Day we had scheduled during SHOT for attendance by our Western Regional sales agency and their top less-lethal customers, followed by taking that range demonstration around the country as “Road SHOT” to locations such as Fort Work, Miami-Dade, Cleveland, and Philadelphia to meet in-person with all our representative agencies and customers.
Covid-19 will dictate the timing for us to do this safely, but it has the potential to cost less and be far more effective than trying to compete with some 100,000 SHOT participants in January. While the big guys will go without SHOT entirely this year, we’ll actually create our own without competition from anyone.
We have established a relationship with the largest EU distributor of less-lethal tech, located in the Netherlands. They have already made an initial order and are enthusiastic about the international potential for Presidia Gel and Reflex Remove alike. They are planning on attending our Vegas Range Day as well.
While Covid-19 has once again locked down the Middle East, we also have an agent there who expects a rescheduling of a recently canceled meeting with the Kuwait Army to demonstrate Reflex Remove – they also have tremendous enthusiasm for the potential there.
The foregoing evidence the continued success of our hand-in-glove strategic alliance with the truly outstanding team at Melanie & Matt Shaefer’s Tactical Defense Training, LLC in Canton, OH. Cannot say enough about how our meeting at SHOT last January turned out to be a game-changer for all of us. Special thanks to them!
Over the course of the past month, we have published two scholarly, neutral White Papers supporting the implementation of our products and training.
The first, by Dr. Bayne French, explains for law enforcement medically, chemically, and practically why Presidia Gel is an entirely different and new controlling force less-lethal option, better than pepper spray or traditional CS gas and safer and more effective than Taser and other existing options.
Tactical Medical White Paper
The Healthcare White Paper is intended to provide nurses’ unions, caregivers, security, and others advocating for safer healthcare workplace conditions and violence prevention training and equipment with compelling efficacy, safety, and most importantly, economic arguments for adopting our Active Defense Solution.
Healthcare White Paper
Healthcare Supervisor Summary
This latter White Paper and other new marketing materials aimed specifically at COVID-SAFE training options is being distributed widely by our healthcare group purchasing organization partner HRS, with whom we were hardly underway last January when the pandemic shut us down. Having this 30-strong sales team now able for the first time in months to focus their customers on something other than PPE is a key opportunity for which we are well prepared.
We have completed our new clamshell packaging for consumer retail sales and are presently setting up distribution opportunities online and in brick & mortar locations such as Dick’s Sports, Big 5, and a wide range of other retailers for Q1.
We are also completing the R&D process for a purely consumer-oriented product (i.e. a keychain or clip-on Presidia Gel for joggers, hikers, etc.) specifically for this market.
We are presently in advanced discussions with one of our existing strategic alliances to piggy-back on this entry into consumer retail and advancing our reputation and presence in the LE market will be considerably expanded by way of the release of the “Street Ready” less-lethal bundle (holster, 1.9 oz PG, aerosol RR) for patrol officers and consumers who want to “Use What the Heroes Use” everywhere.
We are catching the attention of the national press more frequently, and although we still haven’t had the breakout coverage of an incident (most of which are understandably kept secret), coverage of national conversations around police shootings, use of force, workplace violence, an armed and divided citizenry, and civil unrest without mention of the existence and availability of an all-new less-lethal option responsive to the needs of the day is becoming untenable. Several journalists have suggested they expect our story to hit the news when the present election cycle slows a bit and during a time when good news might be welcomed.
We wish you all a healthy and safe holiday season. Warm regards.
Jessica Adanich
Reflex Protect
