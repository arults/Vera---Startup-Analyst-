Applaudable extends its raise
Applaudable, is having one of the best months for a crowdfunding round in recent memory.  We’ve raised over $500k in April alone and just crossed the $1 million mark.
With high investor demand, we’ve briefly extended our Series A capital raise.  More details are at http://republic.com/applaudable to invest while it is still open to the public.
We’re disrupting the $177B social media advertising market with a new social media platform focused on sharing life’s most meaningful experiences in a way that allows other users to emulate and purchase those experiences, right within Applaudable’s platform.
We’re already growing faster than Clubhouse did over their first six months.   And we have a credible plan to reach 7.5 million Applaudable users in year one.  If we get anywhere near that trajectory, we will have built one of the most valuable new media properties created in years.
We’ve just released the latest timetable for the launch of the Applaudable, and expect the platform to be live in beta on the web in June 2022, and live in beta on mobile in Q3 or Q4, with general availability by year-end.
Very Best,
Andrew Christodoulides
