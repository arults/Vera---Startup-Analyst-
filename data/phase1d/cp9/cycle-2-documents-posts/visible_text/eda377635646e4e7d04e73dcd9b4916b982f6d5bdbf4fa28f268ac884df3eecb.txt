🔥 Asylm launch + .COM acquisition + Bonus Perk
We’re almost there!
A huge thank you to all of our incredible investors, your belief in Watrfall has brought us to the edge of completing our raise. Our campaign may be ending, but the momentum is not. Watrfall is just getting started — and so are the big announcements.
Big news: Asylm — Ron and Willie’s new production studio — launched yesterday
Yesterday,
Deadline dropped
the news on our co-founders Ron and Willie launching the new film, TV, and new media production studio, Asylm Studios. The studio’s first project will be Kodak Super-XX.  The psychological thriller will star Kelly Marie Tran (
Star Wars sequel trilogy, Raya and the Last Dragon
) and David Dastmalchian (
Murderbot, The Dark Night, The Suicide Squad
) and will be the feature debut for director Auden Bui (
The Lost City of Tomorrow
)
Asylm will be developing films, shows, and documentaries and will become the
first studio committing exclusive content rights to the upcoming Watrfall watch platform.
As Willie himself put it: “Streamers promised additional revenue and distribution, but what actually happened is they destroyed traditional revenue streams and then took away any real equity with their opaque business models. Asylm seeks to change that, but it's only possible with future-facing platforms like Watrfall."
.COM domain acquired
We’ve heard your feedback that the
.io
domain wasn’t feeling serious enough and we’re super excited to share that we have now acquired the
watrfall.com
domain address! It’s already redirecting to our Republic page, and shortly after the campaign concludes will direct the audience to the Watrfall platform.
Final stretch! Plus, announcing a bonus perk
Despite being almost at our goal of $1M, we have to keep pushing — as there’s space that can open up in the backend admin processing of prior investments.
We need everyone’s help to hit — and even overshoot — our target of $1M.
Plus, to celebrate the win, we’re announcing that every investor (not just the new ones!) will receive an additional bonus perk:
A special credit in the first Watrfall production.
That means your name (or your chosen display name) will appear on-screen as part of the founding group that made Watrfall possible*. Your name will also be listed on the Founding Members page on
Watrfall.com
This is a community-built studio, and we want your name to be part of its story.
Share the Watrfall story
To help us push our message as far and wide as possible, please share Watrfall with your friends, family, and fellow film lovers and let’s make history for creator-led, audience-owned storytelling!
To make it easier, here’s some ideas for messaging you could use:
💬
Short social posts:
I invested in Watrfall, the new studio co-founded by Ron Perlman that’s giving power (and profits) back to fans and creators. They’re 99% funded with less than 2 days left — your last shot to get in before the round closes. 🎥
republic.com/watrfall
Just joined Ron Perlman’s new studio, Watrfall — where fans actually own a piece of the movies they help greenlight. It’s already 99% funded with just hours left. Don’t watch from the sidelines — join the founding members:  👉
republic.com/watrfall
I’m officially part of Watrfall, Ron Perlman’s fan-owned studio that’s rewriting Hollywood’s rules. 99% funded, just hours left — and investors get their names in the first film’s credits. This is the last call:
republic.com/watrfall
🎬
Longer social post:
I just invested in Watrfall, a new kind of studio co-founded by Ron Perlman that’s flipping the Hollywood model upside down.
Instead of studios deciding what gets made, fans and creators now call the shots. Anyone can pitch. Investors vote to greenlight projects. Everyone shares in the profits.
Watrfall is 99% funded and has just hours left to hit $1M. When it does, all investors — no matter the size of their contribution — get credited in the first Watrfall production.
After decades of Hollywood middlemen taking the lion’s share, this feels like a rare chance to build something fair — transparent, creative, and fan-powered.
I got in. If you care about storytelling, this is your last window to do the same.
republic.com/watrfall
Thank you all for your support. Let's do this!
Yours truly,
Dana
* Note: This additional perk is separate from the IMDb + film credit perk at the $5,000 tier, which will include a more prominent on-screen credit.
