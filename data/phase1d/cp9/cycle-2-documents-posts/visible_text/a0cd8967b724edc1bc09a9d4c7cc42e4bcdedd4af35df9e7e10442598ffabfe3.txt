Blue Social Transitions to Web3 - Public Presale Begins NOW!
We are excited to announce that our Public Presale is now LIVE. You can purchase Blue Social Tokens [BST] at $0.10 each. This is a
44% discount
before we get listed on Uniswap!
We are revolutionizing the way people network with our new Web3 Socialize-to-Earn model. Our Web3 mobile application will be released early next year where users can earn tokens for being social in person! These interactions are verified by our new
Bluetooth Proof-of-Interaction
(POI) protocol that verifies two users are being social within 6 feet.
The Blue Social Token is an Ethereum-based token which will become the in-app utility token to access functionalities such as:
Bluetooth Discovery - Find users who are within 150 feet proximity of you and be matched based on interest. Great for schools, conferences and conventions.
Break the Ice - Send a direct notification to users nearby letting them know you want to meet.
Offline Bluetooth Payments - Pay users nearby without the need of Wifi or internet. Great for cross border payments in countries that have little or no internet.
Events on Demand - Discover and check in to events happening in your area.
Geo-Drops - Businesses can advertise to users using Bluetooth beacons where users can receive discounts, coupons and more! Businesses will pay users for engaging with their ads allowing users to earn income and take control of their data. A $65B industry waiting to be capitalized on!
Unlock Blue Pro - Rather than paying our $5/mo subscription fee, users can use their tokens they earn to unlock premium features like having multiple profiles, adding unlimited links, downloading interaction data and more!
Earn Rewards and prizes from brands we partner with in our marketplace.
We are in a bear market, but a bear market is when builders and innovators can thrive! You can read more on our whitepaper found in our presale page here:
https://presale.blue.social
Blue Social is the future and we believe our technology will leapfrog the dozens of other companies who copied our nfc products from 2019. This past year we have been focused spreading Blue Social across the universities in the US. We have made over 65+ partnerships with students organizations and schools that are adopting Blue Social as a better way to connect with other students. This is what Facebook did back in the day that grew their users of early adopters to millions and then eventually billions of users worldwide!
