May 10

All Games Supported by Fig Portfolio Shares
Hey Everyone!
It has now been a bit over a year since this campaign closed. During this campaign we raised over $2.2M to invest into at least ten game projects. I know many of you are eager to learn more about what has come of the projects this campaign is supporting. With this update I want to provide details on the 11 game projects that this campaign is supporting. Some of these titles have already been announced, and have store pages where you can wishlist them (which I will link below). However, some of these titles have not been fully announced yet, so I can only share a small amount of information.
Here are the games that Fig Portfolio Shares is supporting:
For Bail!
- developed by Brainchild Studios - expected to release in 2023
"Home Alone meets Among Us in this asymmetric social deduction game that pits crazed Cultists against undercover Journalists. Cartoon slapstick humor combines with devious multiplayer antics as players attempt to take down a cult through hilarious sabotage combinations."
Cabernet
- developed by Party For Introverts - expected in Q2 2024
Wishlist:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/2401410/Cabernet/
"A 2D Narrative Role-Playing Game set in the 19th century. It follows a young female vampire, Liza, as she grapples with her own morality while navigating the horrors and power struggles of aristocratic vampiric society."
Clam Man 2
- developed by Team Clam - expected in Q2 2024
Wishlist:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/1278380/Clam_Man_2_Headliner/
"A narrative, combatless roleplaying game where jokes are loot and boss fights are comedy shows."
A Long Journey to an Uncertain End
- developed by Crispy Creative - expected in Q2 2023
Wishlist:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/1164830/A_Long_Journey_to_an_Uncertain_End/
"A narrative, management space opera where you recruit eclectic crew members, explore the known - and unknown - galaxy, make choices to drive your journey, and escape the clutches of your abusive ex."
Project Treefarm (working title)
- developed by Rose City Games - expected in Q2 2025
A new IP for the studio
Wizardry 1, 2, and 3 rereleases
- expected in H1 2024
Unannounced Wizardry Project
- expected in H1 2024
Two Unannounced Atari Projects
- expected in 2023
As some of you may recall, we originally launched the Fig Portfolio Shares campaign with Atari titles included, but we updated the campaign to remove those Atari titles and add in Wizardry. Well, I'm happy to announce that two Atari titles are being supported from Fig Portfolio Shares! While these two Atari titles are currently unannounced, I expect  they will be announced soon, as they are expected to release this year. Lastly, on Atari, I'll mention that these two unannounced titles are completely separate from the titles that are going to be supported by the current Atari Game Shares campaign that Fig is also currently running on Republic. You can check out the Atari Game Pool campaign here:
https://republic.com/atari
I hope that you are excited and intrigued by these game projects, and I can't wait to share more details about them as those details get announced.
All the best,
Ash
Ash Leavenworth
Fig Portfolio Shares

Status per Chat GPT referencing SEC filings and asking about shutdown status, looks like Rose City Games was shutdown:
1. A Long Journey to an Uncertain End (Crispy Creative — expected Q2 2023)
Fig provided the full amount of Fig Funds ($228,325) to Crispy as part of the revenue-sharing agreement to publish this title.
2. Cabernet (Party For Introverts — expected Q2 2024)
Fig provided $225,000 of its Fig Funds under the agreement to publish Cabernet, fulfilling its allocation.
3. Clam Man 2 – Headliner (Team Clam — expected Q2 2024)
Fig has committed between $205,000 and $250,000; $205,000 has been allocated although it was planned for early 2023 and may still be pending actual disbursement.
4. Project Treefarm (Rose City Games — expected Q2 2025)
Fig has provided $220,000 of Fig Funds toward this new IP under Rose City’s development agreement.
According to their official announcement, as of April 28, 2025, Rose City Games closed its doors after a decade of creating indie titles. Despite launching their VR game LOVESICK earlier in March 2025, they were unable to secure further funding to sustain the studio and chose to cease operations
5. Blood for Baal (Brainchild Studios — associated with "Bail!" from Brainchild) expected originally in 2023
Fig allocated $224,000 to Brainchild for a game titled Blood for Baal (likely the “Bail!” project), but as of the latest filing, it remains in development; no release update has been disclosed.
❌ Projects Without Clear Funding or Progress
Wizardry 1, 2, and 3 rereleases, an Unannounced Wizardry Project, and Two Unannounced Atari Projects (expected mid‑2023 to H1 2024) do not yet have Fig Funds allocated in the current filings. They remain in the pipeline but have not advanced to funded status per public SEC disclosures.
