😍 HelloWoofy Asked to Keynote Speak at Youth Marketing Summit for Digital Marketers! 🎙️
Click to Learn More
Dear Republic Investor 👋,
Hope you're having an amazing Friday going into the weekend ahead. We wanted to
make one more announcement
today (
yeah we love hustling all day every day
haha). Over the past few days, we've been communicating with a
marketing focused event organization that asked us to join their session later this year as a Keynote speaker
at the
Youth Marketing Summit
. The speaking session will include
topics we're intensely passionate about and leading the industry with our data science research but specifically how it impacts the youth generation from a marketing perspective.
Here's what they had to say:
"For over 10 years, Voxburner has been providing leading brands across the world with insights & best practices on GenZ marketing & the future of social media. With HelloWoofy leading the way on innovation in the social media space, it was a natural fit to reach out to Arjun and team about taking to the stage at our flagship event, YMS:ONLINE USA. We're excited to welcome the whole team to what's going to be a great event!"
-Joe Watson, Global Head of Partnerships, Voxburner
In addition to the event speaking session, we will also be
hosting a virtual exhibit that looks very much like a real life exhibition table or booth but with customizable videos, GIFs, etc. showcasing our brand and research
. Attendees can "walk" by exhibits and interact at scale with companies as well as learn more about the product via a rich chat room. As a result,
instead of speaking with one person at a time, as in traditional events, we can address questions from dozens of people at a time!
As you can see,
this is a great joint effort
and an event we look very much forward to. If you are considering
investing into our startup equity crowdfunding campaign or have already invested but might consider increasing your investment, now is the time!
We count every dollar and invest it carefully into growing the business one step at a time.
Wishing you a lovely weekend ahead.
👍
Best,
Arjun Rai,
Founder + CEO,
HelloWoofy.com
