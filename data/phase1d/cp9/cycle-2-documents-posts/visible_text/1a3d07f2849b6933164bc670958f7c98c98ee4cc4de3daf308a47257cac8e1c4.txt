New CRM Integration with 3,000+ apps
Hello friends,
We just launched our CRM integration with over 3,000+ apps you can choose from. Automate your interactions directly into Mailchimp, SalesForce, Gmail and thousands more!
Our goal is to enhance your networking capabilities - instead of collecting old paper business cards and then manually inputting them into your CRM, Blue can do it for you!
Check it out and make sure to upgrade to Blue Pro.
We appreciate everyone who has downloaded our latest version of Blue 3.0
We are growing 100+ users daily and are now producing recurring revenue through our in-app subscription. With many competitors now in the market of selling "NFC tags" we are changing our strategy to focus on app downloads. 📲
Allowing us to scale, increase in-app subscriptions, and funnel users to purchase NFC products once they're a Blue user. We are software as a service focused now.
Please leave us a review in the app store
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blue-networking-made-easy/id1151689697
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=blue.social.app&hl=en_US
Jose Montero
