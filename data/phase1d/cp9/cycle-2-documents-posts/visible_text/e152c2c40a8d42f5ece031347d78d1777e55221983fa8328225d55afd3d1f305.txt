Update / RealtyFeed
To Our Valued Investors,
Over the past four to five years, the real estate industry has undergone profound transformation. Some shifts have unfolded at a pace few anticipated, fundamentally reshaping market expectations, technology adoption, and data governance models.
In response, we undertook a comprehensive reassessment of RealtyFeed’s long-term strategy. Rather than reacting incrementally, we redesigned our roadmap to align with the evolving structural realities of the market.
To ensure we are positioned to meet these new demands, we have implemented the following strategic initiatives:
1. Strategic Pivot to Real Estate Infrastructure
We transitioned from a social-networking-oriented platform to a real estate API and infrastructure provider for now. This shift significantly increases scalability while reducing capital intensity, allowing us to grow without relying on additional external financing.
2. Migration to a SaaS Revenue Model
We moved from a one-time sales structure to a recurring SaaS subscription model. Today, the majority of our revenue is subscription-based, providing predictable cash flow and strong financial stability to support disciplined, self-funded growth.
3. First Association Client Onboarded
We successfully onboarded our first association client, demonstrating validation of our infrastructure model in the institutional marketplace.
Case study:
https://realtyfeed.com/SDMLS_CaseStudy
4. Corporate Structuring for Focused Execution
RealtyFeed has now been formally positioned as a subsidiary of Realtyna, with a clear and dedicated mission: serving real estate associations and MLS organizations.
5. Leadership for the Next Phase of Growth
To execute our ambitious development roadmap, we welcomed Mr. Patrick Pichette as CEO of RealtyFeed.
Leadership Profile
As the CEO of RealtyFeed, Patrick Pichette leads at the intersection of high-growth technology and complex stakeholder governance, specializing in building the digital infrastructure that powers the real estate industry. With over 25 years of experience, including a tenure as the founding CEO of
REALTOR.ca
(where they spearheaded the platform’s evolution to #1 market share), Patrick is now championing the Data Ecosystem model through the MLS Router™ platform, RealtyFeed's flagship product. This work enables MLS organizations to move away from legacy silos toward secure, API-driven governance, allowing them to reclaim the value of their data as a strategic, revenue-generating asset. Patrick provides board-level oversight on digital transformation, ensuring that accountability and public trust remain at the core of every technological advancement.
Again, Thank you for your investment and trust.
Ali Attar
RealtyFeed
