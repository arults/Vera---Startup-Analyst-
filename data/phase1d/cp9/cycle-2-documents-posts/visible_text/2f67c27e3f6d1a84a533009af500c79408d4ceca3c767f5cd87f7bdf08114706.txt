Q4'25 Currently Wine Co Investor Update
2025 Wins and Learnings
Over $100k revenue in first year in market, with majority in MA.
Expect warm-weather seasonality given product type and geography.
Initial thesis around outdoor use cases, events, boats is still being tested, with some positive results to show. Currently was in more than 150 retail locations, but really need to focus sales effort on on-premise accounts that can drive consistent volume.
Adding Harvard Athletics and Boston Museum of Science in Q4 are good steps in this direction. Hy-Line Ferry was best account in 2025, serving over 100 cases of Currently.
Consistent and predictable volume will also help with reducing any inventory aging issues.
Brand
We’re building a coastal lifestyle wine brand
We’re active on Instagram.
Follow us here.
Our “Currently Engaged” Campaign for bachelorette parties has performed well and gotten good user generated content
Building In Public on LinkedIn has helped bring in investor interest, sales intros and also gotten the attention of large future acquirers of Currently.
Follow me here
to see updates on Currently 5x/week.
Go-to-Market
Our GTM strategy is 90% 3-tier (restaurants/stores), meaning our online sales are 10% of our business
Harvard Athletics began serving Currently in Nov 2025. Large caterer, Sodexo, manages this account. Working on getting additional Sodexo accounts (Aquarium, Zoo, theater district venues in Boston).
Had some early traction sales in South Carolina in Q4.
Biggest win
: Boston Museum of Science now carrying Currently in Jan 2026.
Working on a target account list of large on-prem and catering accounts for 2026.
Intervine, a cruise and airline sales brokerage, is now representing Currently. We will be participating in RFPs for late 2026/2027 sales.
Product
Spring 2026 new product: ros
é
. Sneak peak at bottle design below! Product will be available May 2026.
Finance
Cash as of 12/31/25: $545k.
2025 Revenue: $111k
Runway: >18 months
2025 Goals (with Q4 commentary in parenthesis)
Successful market launch in MA, especially Cape Cod, Martha’s Vineyard and Nantucket in the summer (MA has been successful, but only 1,000 cases of the 2,000 goal sold.. The MA islands did almost no volume; didn’t get into any of the power on-prem volume accounts and short season)
Successful FL market launch in hotels, restaurants and events (changing this to South Carolina; need to get some “power” accounts to move product)
Bring on celebrity endorsement (not pursuing actively; not sure if need it)
Continued high-quality sourcing (buyer’s market for wine, on plan)
Improve product margin by 10% (logistics of lower volume bottlings hurt margin, so not trying to expand margin at this time and instead focus on sales volume)
2026 Goals
2,500 cases sold
Launch new product (rose)
Get “sips to lips” in summer
Attract attention in market from press and future acquirer
Sign on a large 2027 account in cruise or airport lounge

Cheers,
-Daniel
