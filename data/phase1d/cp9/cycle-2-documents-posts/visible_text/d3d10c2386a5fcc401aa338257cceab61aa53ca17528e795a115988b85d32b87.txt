Applaudable is Getting Even Better
As Applaudable gets out “into the wild” we’re thrilled that the early results suggest that our hypothesis is dead on: that people are hungry for a more meaningful, more authentic social media platform.
Learn more about our current raise
here
.
That authenticity is built into our DNA.
But we aren’t stopping there.  We’re excited to give you a sneak peek into some key updates coming in our next release.
Velvet Rope Access:
Our initial public release will include a unique "velvet rope" feature. Users will need an invitation to gain access, ensuring we can maintain optimal network density and control our growth trajectory during the early stages.
Highlights:
Upon publishing a post, users will have the option to enhance it by adding highlights. These highlights serve as a way for users to applaud specific parts of their original post. Imagine pointing out the standout moments of a trip as they unfold.
Video Integration:
Users can now incorporate videos into their posts. A single post can feature up to 10 photos and/or videos. Even better? Each highlight within the post can contain its own set of 10.
Disappearing Posts:
Posts will automatically vanish after 24 hours. However, users can immortalize their most notable experiences by adding requisite details to the original post, making the shared experience replicable for others. While vanished posts leave the public eye, they'll stay in the user's private diary, ready to be made public again at the user's discretion.
In addition to these new features, we're constantly refining the platform, especially our "Real Life Stories" segment. Our vision remains clear: to position Applaudable as the premier platform for capturing and sharing the world's most memorable experiences. We’re looking forward to you being a part of what we believe will be the future of social media and we thank you for the continued support in our vision.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
