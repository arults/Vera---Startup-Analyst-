2021 Year End Results
Dear Investors,
2021 was another year of unexpected challenges in the Consumer Packaged Good industry, and Salsa God was able to successfully navigate the relentless cost pressures that have invaded every aspect of the company's business.
2021 - Another Year in the Books!
Gross Revenue
In 2021, Salsa God generated approximately $430,000 in Gross Revenue, which was flat or slightly above Gross Revenue in 2020.  While the company's results did not meet the expectations of internal management, there were a number of positive signs.
Australian Market
In 2021, Salsa God shipped its first full 20' container to Real Foods Australia (Salsa God's sole distributor in Australia) generating approximately $30,000 in Gross Revenue.  The company first began exporting to Australia in 2018 and has gradually grown its sales sufficiently to begin shipping full container loads to the land down under!
Sprouts Farmers Market
Salsa God launched nationally into Sprouts Farmers Market in April of 2021, and Sprouts has quickly grown to be Salsa God's largest account by number of stores (over 350 stores across the U.S.).  We are very excited to be a valued partner of Sprouts, and we expect to continue to grow our sales within the premium natural retailer.
World Market
World Market represents Salsa God's first foray into the non-traditional retailer market in the U.S., and it also represents Salsa God's first account using a 12oz jar rather than the traditional 16oz jar.  World Market will allow Salsa God to expand its reach to consumers who may not otherwise see Salsa God products on the local grocery store shelves.
2022 - The Year Ahead
2022 holds some very promising news for Salsa God.  Our first and biggest news item is also something of a re-release.
Chips & Salsa Combo Pack is Back!
Salsa God is re-releasing its Chips & Salsa Combo Pack.  The company originally released the Chips & Salsa Combo Pack in 2019, but after only a short time in the market, the company's salsa cup co-packer decided to no longer produce salsa cups for Salsa God.  This was very difficult news for us to hear, but after 2+ years of searching for another salsa cup co-packer, we have finally found a co-packer who can produce Salsa God's salsa cups again!  To further complicate matters, Salsa God's tortilla chip co-packer informed the company that they would not be able to fill our expected demand of tortilla chips due to labor issues, so Salsa God was forced to find another co-packer for our tortilla chips.  Plus, we had to do all of this during the COVID lockdown of 2020, which made everything take much longer than expected.  But alas, we have our Combo Packs ready to Rock!
COMBO PACK 2.0 -
Our new, soon-to-be re-released Chips & Salsa Combo Pack is an upgrade to our previous version, and we are very excited about this product.  We have reduced the size of our Combo Pack from 7oz (4oz salsa cup and 3oz bag of tortilla chips) to 5oz (3oz salsa cup and 2oz bag of tortilla chips).  Not only is this a more appropriate portion size for a single-serve item, but it also comes in a smaller box that will more easily fit on to store shelves.  We expect our Combo Pack to be a big hit in C-Stores, Airports and even Airlines.
Natural Products Expo West
Salsa God is very excited to be exhibiting at the Natural Products Expo West in Anaheim, California from March 9th - March 11th.  We hope to generate tons of buzz around our Combo Packs!
Sweets and Snacks Expo
Salsa God is very excited to be exhibiting at the Sweets & Snacks Expo in Chicago, Illinois from May 23rd - May 26th.  We hope to generate tons of buzz in the C-Store channel around our Combo Packs!
BIG ANNOUNCEMENT
For the past 6 months or so, we have been in discussions with a very large account regarding a partnership.  Unfortunately, at the time of this post, we are not yet in a position to definitively say that the partnership will move forward, but we are very optimistic that we can get to a point in the near future to allow us to reveal the big announcement to our investors.  Trust us - this is a company-changing partnership, and we have been working night and day to get the deal done.
Investor Perks
Some investors have pointed out that they have not yet received one of the perks from investing $1,000 or more in the company.  The perk was that each investor who met the investment threshold was to receive samples of all new products released in 2020.  Unfortunately, due to COVID, Salsa God did not release any new products in 2020.  However, we are not going to let this deter us from rewarding our investors (U.S. continuous 48 states only).  Once the Chips & Salsa Combo Pack is released (expected June 1, 2022), we will be giving each qualified investor a code for a free 3 pack on Amazon.  Stay tuned for details, and please ensure that you leave feedback on Amazon to help us grow our sales.
Conclusion
2021 did not meet our expectations for growth, but we are excited about the opportunities present in 2022.  We hope to grow the business significantly with the relaunch of the Combo Pack, and we truly hope to be able to share the BIG ANNOUNCEMENT with our investors and the world shortly.
Best wishes for a great 2022 and HAPPY TEXAS INDEPENDENCE DAY!!!
Danny Mayans
Chief Amigo, Salsa God
Danny Mayans
