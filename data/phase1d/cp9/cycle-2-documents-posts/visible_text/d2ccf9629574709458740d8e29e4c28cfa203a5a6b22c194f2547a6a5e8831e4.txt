🔥The Rebrand Is Here.  Say Hello To Unbanked! 🔥
For a few months we have been teasing a Ternio rebrand and today I'm happy to share that the new brand is Unbanked!  We acquired the
Unbanked.com
domain and over the next few weeks will merge Ternio and BlockCard brands to the new domain.
Press Release:
Ternio and BlockCard Converge and Rebrand to Help Consumers Become Unbanked
What Is Unbanked?
"To the tech and finance curious we are the financial institution that provides transformative products and services, because we believe that financial access and control is a fundamental human right."
Over the last 3 years, our company has grown significantly in terms of team size and product offerings. We have an ambitious plan to release even more products and become a global platform that gives people frictionless usage of digital currencies in their everyday life.
Ultimately, we needed a brand that spoke to the full suite of products and services we offer, and we did not want that fragmented across multiple brands.
Click the image to watch a video explainer from me

If you were already following Ternio or BlockCard accounts, then everything should have migrated over completely.  However, if there are some you're missing, please follow or subscribe to the links below

Unbanked Promo Video
What does this mean for Ternio LLC investors?
Nothing changes.  Unbanked is a dba (doing business as) of Ternio, LLC, and your equity investment in Ternio remains the same.
What does this mean for Ternio or BlockCard users?
Unbanked.com
is live now and over the coming weeks, we will be expanding the site and eventually redirecting
Ternio.io
and
GetBlockCard.com
to Unbanked. You will begin to see visual changes to the website and Dashboard UI as we undergo this rebranding process combined with some new product features we have planed.  Most important, none of our card programs functionality will be impacted by these changes.  The plan is to have this migration complete by the first week of July.
Our team has worked towards this for months and yesterday's announcement of the rebrand is just the start of great things to come.  Stay tuned for more products, more features, more white-label partners, and a few other surprises in store. 😎
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
