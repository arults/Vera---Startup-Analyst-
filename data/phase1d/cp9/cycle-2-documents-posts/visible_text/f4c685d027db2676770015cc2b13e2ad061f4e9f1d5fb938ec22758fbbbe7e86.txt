Beacons Q2 Update
Happy summer! Welcome to our first quarterly investor update. We're thrilled to have you on board!
We've had some exciting developments this quarter on the fundraising, hiring, product, and growth fronts, which we dive into here.
If there's anything else you'd like to see in these investor updates, please let us know! We're always learning and keen to hear your ideas both for these updates and in general. Reach out any time 🙂
Fundraising
We raised a $6 million seed led by a16z with participations from the Chainsmokers, Li Jin, Naval Ravikant, and many others! TechCrunch article
here
, and our own blog
here
.
Product Updates
We've had many exciting product developments over the past quarter. A few key updates we wanted to highlight:
Image block: you can now add images to your Beacons page for further customization.
Save themes: you can now save themes on your Beacons page. Along with undo changes, it's easier to experiment with different page formats.
Community features: we have built out a set of community features for creators and fans to more seamlessly engage with each other.
Mobile home screen launch: creators can add our Beacons app to their home screen if they’re on iOS, which will improve engagement and retention and pave the way for future app developments like app store distribution, push notifications, and more
Growth
This quarter, we grew revenue by 68% and doubled number of accounts!
New Hires
Jessica Li
joined as our chief of staff! She’ll be working on a wide range of projects to make everyone maximally effective and improve all parts of the business.
Asks
We're hiring across the board! Check out
careers.beacons.ai
for our open roles in design, engineering, and customer success. We'd love for you to share our careers page and send over anyone you may know who could be a fit.
Neal Jean
