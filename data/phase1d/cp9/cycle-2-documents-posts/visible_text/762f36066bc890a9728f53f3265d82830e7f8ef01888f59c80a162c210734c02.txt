Republic Investor Update – November 2025
Hello Republic investors,
Here’s a brief update on Reental’s progress during November 2025.
Company & Strategy
Reental continued executing steadily on both operational and corporate fronts as we approach year-end. Discussions around strategic partnerships and potential capital reinforcement progressed, alongside internal work focused on strengthening financial structure and long-term positioning in key markets, including the Middle East.
Platform & Operations
The business maintained stable growth, with continued expansion of assets under management and the investor base. Two new projects were launched during the month — Costa Maya 1 (Mexico) and Dubai 2 (UAE) — further diversifying the portfolio and reinforcing our strategy of working with recurring, trusted local partners. Monthly yield distributions were completed as planned across the portfolio.
Key Highlights (November)
Total AUM YTD 2025: ~$63.6M
Active investors on the platform: 2,800+
Average monthly yield distributed: ~13.8%
No project early exits or disruptions during the period
Token Ecosystem
The $RNT ecosystem showed positive momentum, with growth in active SuperReentels, increased capital locked in staking, and higher OTC activity, reflecting continued engagement and confidence from the community.
Product Development
Product improvements focused on usability and investor experience, particularly around investment management and wallet functionality. Progress also continued on Human Wallet, a solution designed to simplify Web3 access by enabling users to operate without managing private keys or external applications, with integration planned for December.
We remain focused on disciplined execution, operational resilience, and transparent communication as we continue scaling the platform.
Thank you for being part of the journey.
— The Reental Team
Carla Castello Richart
