Beacons Q3 Update
Happy fall! 🍁 Welcome to our quarterly investor update.
Read on for updates on the product, team, and growth fronts as well as how you can help!
Product
This quarter, we launched our integration block (read more
here
!) with 4 partners in the creator economy. By adding these integrations, Beacons creators can move beyond just linking and instead provide an interactive preview of their pages on these platforms all in one centralized home base: their Beacons page.
We've also added features like the color picker, theme chooser, custom corner radius, and video backgrounds to help creators further customize their pages to reflect their brands.
We added email blasts - a way to creators to email their Beacons audience directly - (read more
here
!) to help creators truly own their audiences.
Team
We're now 9 people full time! We brought on a head of partnerships, product manager, customer success specialist, and full stack engineer. We also just signed a lease for our first office in SF!
Growth
We more than doubled the total number of accounts! We're starting to work on paid marketing to further drive user and revenue growth.
Asks
We're hiring a few more engineers and would love your recs on great candidates. Here's a permalink to our careers page:
careers.beacons.ai
