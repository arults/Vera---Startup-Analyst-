Q1'26 Currently Wine Co Investor Update
2026 Goals
2,500 cases sold (vs 1,000 in 2025)
Launch new product (Rosé)
Get “sips to lips” in summer
Attract attention in market from press and future acquirer
Sign on a large 2027 account in cruise or airport lounge
Q1 Update
The annual buying window for Currently’s seasonal product is March - May. IF YOU HAVE GOOD FIT ACCOUNT INTROS, NOW IS THE TIME.
Can you ask in your network for F&B Directors of Country Clubs, Yacht Clubs, Ferry / Tour boat operators, hotels with pools / weddings in MA, ME, RI, NH, NY, SC.
There is strong interest from
Disney Cruises
but it’s early in their buying process. This would be a significant step forward and is for ‘27-’28 volume. More updates to come in 3 months.
Some large new accounts (over 1,000 cases of volume for these 3 total) have been signed on that fit the “outside in the summer” thesis:
Classic Harbor Lines (fancy boat tour operator out of Boston, Newport and NYC)
Cap’n John’s Boats (sunset cruise in Cape Cod)
Bartending Service of New England (large Boston / Cape Cod caterer)
Initial thesis around outdoor use cases, events, boats is proving to be true
Brand
We’re building a coastal lifestyle wine brand
We’re active on Instagram and TikTok. @currentlywineco
Our “Currently Engaged” Campaign for bachelorette parties has performed well and gotten good user generated content
Building In Public on LinkedIn has helped bring in investor interest, sales intros and also gotten the attention of large future acquirers of Currently.
Follow me here
to see updates on Currently 3-5x/week.
Go-to-Market
Our GTM strategy is 90% 3-tier (restaurants/stores), meaning our online sales are 10% of our business. 2026 Focus is on on-prem accounts (meaning you drink it where you are buying it).
Biggest Q1 win
: Classic Harbor Lines, from
Ownership Group Member intro!
750 cases total volume expected. Enormous step toward achieving 2026 revenue goal. Thank you, Derek DeGrass!
Currently will be served at over 100 weddings in 2026 in MA (via Boston Museum of Science and Bartending Service of New England)
Intervine, a cruise and airline sales brokerage, is now representing Currently. We will be participating in RFPs for late 2026/2027 sales. Disney Cruises is the big whale that is now in the pipeline.
Got through procurement at Darden Restaurants, owner of Capital Grille. We are now in 2 Capital Grille locations on the by the glass menu– a huge validation step forward. Currently plays bougie– it’s not just casual.
Product
Spring 2026 new product alert: ros
é
. Product will be available May 15, 2026.
Lots of buyer excitement about the rosé bottle design and the wine itself (I’ve pulled tank samples when I’m out selling).
Finance
Cash as of 3/31/26: $487k
Q1’26 Revenue: $26k
Runway: >18 months
The winter was slow. This is the time of year for Currently to achieve many of its goals. It’s an exciting 3 month stretch.
Cheers,
-Daniel
