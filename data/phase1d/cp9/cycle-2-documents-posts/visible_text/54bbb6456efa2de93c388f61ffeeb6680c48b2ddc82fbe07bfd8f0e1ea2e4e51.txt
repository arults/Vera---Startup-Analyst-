The Tide is High,  In Time for Our Newest Designs
For the past three months I have been anticipating sending this Update to
YOU
, our global community of crowdfunding investors. In November, we
signed a deal
with High Tide to create a new line of glassware and everyday cannabis consumption accessories.  Today, I am thrilled to share with you, our esteemed Republic investors, the first renderings of our newest glassware designs - currently dubbed 'The Twenties Collection'.
I want you to know that I am giving my all every day to build this company into something powerful, meaningful and valuable for all of you. On Republic
we have 2500+ investors from 42 countries and every US state and territory.  As investors in our first two crowdfunding rounds, I am thrilled you get the first look at our statement-making new line, inspired by the 20's of the past and to come.
Our design process for the past three months with the
High Tide
team has been exhilarating.  In addition to their in house team of glass design experts,  they also have built the largest cannabis retailer footprint in Canada and their
e-commerce platform
is frequented by over 20 million consumers a year.
These renderings are based on our technical drawings. I will have samples in hand next month! Follow our social channels
@shopjanewest
@thejanewest
to see even more new products in the cue with High Tide and follow their impressive growth as well.
High Tide Inc. (TSXV:HITI) (OTCQB:HITIF) (Frankfurt:2LY)
owns Famous Brandz, manufacturer of Snoop Dogg's 'Pounds' glassware line.
owns Grasscity.com, currently the world's largest online store for consumption accessories*
executed recent agreement to acquire Smoke Cartel, Inc. the second largest e-commerce platform for consumption accessories in the world*
largest cannabis retailer in Canada – primary brand is Canna Cabana, recently acquired META Cannabis Co.
owns Valiant Distribution, with facilities in Calgary and Las Vegas, which distributes to all High Tide retail channels as well as an international network of retail and distribution partners.
*as of January 2021.
Stay tuned for more updates to come,
My Best - Jane
