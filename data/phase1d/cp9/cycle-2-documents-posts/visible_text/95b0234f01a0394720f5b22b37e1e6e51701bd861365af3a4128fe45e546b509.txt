Why its historic user growth has not surprised the team at Applaudable
Here is an excerpt from a recent interview I did with Angels & Entrepreneurs:
Q. You've now attracted more than 5,000 investors. How does that feel?
A. It's humbling. It really is. And we couldn't be more excited about having them on this incredible journey with us. Many of these investors are friends and family, but the vast majority of them, I don't know. I'm committed to making all of their investments extraordinarily successful.
Q. To what do you credit the success of this offering.
A. We're building something truly differentiated with immense potential. We've also validated it through user registrations beyond what the leading platforms in history have had at this stage. We get that not everyone has the ability to see that. But thousands of people have, and again, we're committed to making sure they are right in getting behind what we're doing.
Q. What is it that you are most excited about?
A. We are going to organize the world's greatest experiences and make them accessible and repeatable by others.
This is my 3rd startup. I have genuinely never felt excitement or conviction like this. This, to me, is more than just a business. I believe that awareness and appreciation of what has meaning is actually what gives it meaning. Most of us are not even aware of all of the great things we've experienced throughout our lives. That's the equivalent of not having experienced those things at all. I genuinely believe Applaudable can change that. The potential impact on users is immense, and the scalability of the platform and business can't be overstated. The fact that tens of thousands of users have flocked to it without any advertising or paid media is a testament to the market feeling the same.
To say I'm excited would be an understatement. But, one thing I am not is surprised.
Q. What are your current priorities?
A. I'm focused on product. As I've said before, at this stage, especially given our user growth, nothing is more important than delivering a live product that meets the expectations of the vast amount of users that have registered. And I believe doing this may have a significant impact on the value of the company (and, therefore, on the value of investments).
We have one week left in the current public offering at
republic.com/applaudable
. We don't know when we will reopen the round and if it will be available to the public, but we believe it will be at a higher valuation. While we continue to grow users, delivering a product people are regularly using may enable us to open the next round at a significantly higher valuation.
—
This view is from a third party.  There may be other factors such as various risks discussed or reviewed that are not part of this document.  Please review all other documentation.
