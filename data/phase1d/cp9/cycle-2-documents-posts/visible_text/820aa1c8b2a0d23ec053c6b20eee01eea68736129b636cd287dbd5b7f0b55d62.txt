Updates
May 28
2020
More and more people enjoying cocktails all around!
Dear Wanderers,
We hope you are safe and healthy. We are all in need of good news these days and your investment is certainly doing very well! Our team as been very busy. We are seeing a 500% increase compared to this month last year!
The demand for our product has grown astronomically. More and more bars, restaurants and liquor stores are selling our product and with a much higher turnaround. In order to survive the shutdown, NYC locations are limited to pick up and delivery order. With our cocktails businesses are able to supplement their revenue and offer a high quality, delicious portable cocktail program. We are getting amazing feedback from businesses and consumers. Production has been non-stop and we even made a new addition to our production team!
-------MORE UPDATES-------
NEW STATES
The product launch in most states has obviously paused but we are now seeing orders coming in as they are reopening and allowing cocktails to go such as New Jersey and Connecticut.
PRODUCTION & TASTING ROOM
The built out of the new space has slowed down but we are still hoping to be on track to open in the next 3 months!  Bigger production space is needed more than ever. We hope we will be able to host you all in the tasting room relatively soon!
AND YOUR PERKS...
Don't be impatient!! Your perks are being made. We will email you individually very soon to confirm your address to send you your goodies!
Stay well!
Sincerely , Your Team
ROXANE MOLLICCHI
