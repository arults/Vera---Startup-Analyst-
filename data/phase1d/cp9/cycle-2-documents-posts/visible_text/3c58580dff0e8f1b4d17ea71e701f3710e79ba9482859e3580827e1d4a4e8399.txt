Ray Sturm
@sudharsan-narayanagupta It's a good and fair question that would probably take a long post to describe, but let me try to summarize. In short, if the value of future rounds is higher than this round by a % greater than the ownership given up, then it means the value of your investment increased. As an exaggerated example, if you invest $200K in a  $1M company, you would own 20% valued at $200K. If it raises $10M the next year at a $100M valuation, your ownership would be diluted by 10% but your smaller ownership % would be part of a much bigger company, so everyone wins. If you would like to read more, I'd suggest googling accretive vs dilutive and you will find more information!
