Matt Trenka
6 years ago
After being an investor and a customer for 6 months now I had some additional thoughts on taking ample to the next level:
1- Subscriptions: if someone does not create an ample account but starts a subscription service, there is an email link sent to them to manage their subscription. If that link is ever lost or email deleted there is not an easy way to manage the subscriptions.  This can feel like a bit of a trap as it can be hard to manage the subscription this way, especially if it is a new customer try things out.
2- Shipping: There is a significant delay in shipping from when an order is placed. For example, a 27 June conformation notice with a shipment notice on 30 June is actually delivered on 17 June. Making it nearly 3 weeks before someone receives product. I understand you’re not an amazon but anything longer that 2 weeks is unreasonable and lowers satisfaction of the service as it has been so long many people may have forgot they even ordered.
3- Notification of item not in stock: When orders are placed of two or more and one item is not in stock, there is a shipment of one item and the email notices say “on the way” but nothing about being a partial order. The customer can email to get an explanation but why not be clearer and keep the customer informed. This concern can be compounded with the long 3-week ship time mention above.
4- Price point: Need to find a way to reduce your price by 35%-45% consistently. Right now, a 600-calorie keto pack is going for ~$10-$8 a bottle. While this product is higher quality that just a run of the mill keto drink, it is still a high price for the average consumer. A better price point would be ~$6-4 for a 600-calorie keto bottle.
Hope you will consider these recommendations.
Matt
