Next steps
Dear Investor,
As one of only a handful of active offerings on Republic crossing the $1mm threshold, we’re grateful for the excitement and support we’ve received from thousands of investors. And while we’ve been building and growing Applaudable all along, raising over $1 million in new capital enables us to accelerate key initiatives further.
We’re looking to launch a public version of Applaudable as soon as possible, and we’ll be allocating additional resources to doing so. Over the next month or so, we’ll be growing the product development team by 8-10 new team members. Our top priority is to deliver a user experience that meets the expectations of the significant number of users who have already joined Applaudable. The significance of converting the users who have already registered to active users of Applaudable cannot be overstated. And so, delivering a product they love is the most important thing I can do for the company and all of its stakeholders at this point.
Our goal is to ensure at least 50% of our registered users actively use the live platform. You’ve heard me draw parallels to platforms like Clubhouse and their valuation, which was 10-20x our current $35mm while having fewer users. I believe that achieving our MAU targets will enable us to increase our valuation significantly. And continuing to rapidly grow users at that time can drive that valuation even higher.
We’re working tirelessly to make this happen. We will keep the
offering open
up to $2mm at the current valuation. Beyond the $2mm at the $35mm valuation, our hope and plan is to have active users of the live platform on par with where Clubhouse was when they received a $1 billion valuation before we reopen the offering.
It’s gratifying to bring you this type of news on our progress, and we couldn’t be more excited about what’s ahead.
Again, I’m grateful for your continued confidence and support, and I’m committed to validating your belief in us and making your investment extraordinarily successful.
And as always, I encourage you to see the latest on us
here
and share the opportunity with your respective networks while it is still live.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
