Our First License!
We are proud to make this announcement, and because you are following us, you are the first ones to hear it!
We have acquired our first license! We are working with veteran comic creator, Tim Seeley (
Batman, Nightwing
) to bring his 20 year long running comic book, Hack/Slash to the world of board/card games! We will be opening a pre-launch page very soon, along with an official announcement/ press release.
The gaming market is huge, and by licensing properties we can increase our profit margin from 22% of a successful full service campaign to an estimated 50%.  This addition to our model will compliment our existing full service work.
We have established manufacturing and fulfillment vendors in place from our full service crowdfunding model, and the newest member of our team is a seasoned game designer/ developer overseeing creative.  Our Head of Marketing is a gamer with  a firm grasp  of the games industry and reaching that audience.
Not only will we execute successful crowdfunding campaigns, but also sell via e-commerce, with the opportunity to break into retail at varying levels.  Partners are currently in discussion for retail distribution.
We are very excited about this growth for Zoop.  We thank you for your support that allows us to expand while we continue providing services to clients for crowdfunding.  More on this soon for all of you and we'd love to have you on board!
Jordan Plosky
Zoop
