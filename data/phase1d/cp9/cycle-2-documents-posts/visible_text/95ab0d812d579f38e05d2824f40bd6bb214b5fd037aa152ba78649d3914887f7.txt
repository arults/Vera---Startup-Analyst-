Updates
May  2
2023
Update
The new laser is finally working and making parts. This is a big step forward and will greating improve the fabrication sides' speed. We still have issues of not being able to get the steel we want in the shapes we want. In short we want high tensile steel in the particular shape of our I beams. Unfortunately the mills are still just making the most common shapes in the most common steels and unless we can order 200 tons of a particular shape in a particular strength we aren't getting it. But our engineering depart has devised a work around that will use flat steel which we can get in most tensile strengths.
I mentioned the laser is finally up and running as of last week, this was a 6 month project, but other key items we are still working around such as our overhead crane replacement and misc other production tools. are still months out with no clear deadline for when we get them. The giant plasma table is working partially, it is also waiting on parts to be fully functional, but then since we can't get the 40' lengths of steel is is designed to cut it doesn't really matter.
The switch to the all electric battery system is almost universal. Only 1 unit recently is shipping with the gasoline engine. Everything else is shipping electric. Engineering is stil not quite happy with the battery packs and we are tinkering with different versions including just buying prismatic LiFePo4 cells and making our own. This is probably where we are headed at this point.
In order to accelerate and hire in more personell, we are considering doing another CF raise. I am leaning toward StartEngine because it exposes us to another set of investors. Republic has been great to deal with and we have seen some of our investors be excellent in providing introductions, we did lose a fair number of investors from CA and NY because Republic had a glitch right when we were trying to raise and those investors could not come in. The glitch is long since fixed, but I like the idea of having an entirely new batch of CF investors helping QuickLoadz along. I'll keep you informed and send out an email if we move forward with another CF round.
Still it is all looking good, production is getting faster, we have pretty close to $2M in back orders with just 1 salesperson. We continue to get multiple orders. Engineering and I are very excited about seeing all of the electric units heading out. Produciton is a little less excited because it means there are a lot of changes. Almost from build to build we tweek the electric models. Changes mean slower production and confusion, but it is starting to settle out.
Sean
