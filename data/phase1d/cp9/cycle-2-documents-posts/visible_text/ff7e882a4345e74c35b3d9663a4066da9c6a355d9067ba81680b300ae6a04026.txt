Updates
Mar 13
2023
Acquisition activity heating up and innovation moving forward
Following up after in-person meeting at our industry annual conference in New Orleans we now have two active conversations for acquisitions we expect to move forward this year. The first in the coming weeks and the second in the second half of the year. These two acquisitions that are now in active conversations represent opportunity to grow our business revenue by up to 25% and profits up to 200% creating significant value for our shareholders. Both acquisitions have high synergies and strong compatibilities with ACFN existing customer base and business model. Both are also direct competitors so consolidating their portfolio to ACFN has the additional benefit of removing competitors from our market Niche. We are very excited about these opportunities and dedicating all resources to move this forward as fast as possible. To support that objective we have determined it would be best to close the offering early. You should have received a notice from Republic confirming the new closing date on March 22
nd
. If you are already invested we very much appreciate your support. If you are following and not yet invested we welcome you to join and share in our success going forward.
Other important updates:
­We are in active conversation to install 80 cell phone battery towers in food courts. This would be installed one at each mall with a mall management group that we already have an exclusive ATM relationship with. Assuming this deal gets done it will be the first large deal with this new product and there are other relationships we can pursue with similar offers to increase diversification of ACFN’s product offerings and revenue streams.
Bitcoin software for our Triton ATMs just released!
We have Bitcoin software on some of our ATMs but until now a Triton version was not available. About 85% of our ATMs are manufactured by Triton and we finally received their version of software that will allow us to add this function to our Triton ATMs. We are running tests and have already made arrangements to add this software at 15 locations closed to our HQ. We expect this to be a success and can then add to many more locations. This function is high margin and added at no cost (software only no hardware required) so it is definitely something we want to add to many more locations to continue to build additional income from this high margin revenue stream.
We have a strong road map and look forward to continued development and success!
Jeffrey D. Kerr
American Consumer Financial Network
