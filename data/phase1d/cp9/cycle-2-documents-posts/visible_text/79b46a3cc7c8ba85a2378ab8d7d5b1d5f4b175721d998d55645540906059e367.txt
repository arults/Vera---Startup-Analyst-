👋 Updates from HelloWoofy.com, Last Chance to Learn More about the Latest Round! 😍
Hey awesome people👋, it's Arjun Rai, Founder + CEO at HelloWoofy.com. From the very beginning we set out to help the smallest of small businesses calling them and us, the underdogs. With
over 10,000 registered members now on our platform
, we've made significant progress on that mission and continue to provide cutting edge technology for the price of a cup of coffee. Expanding from a purely social media automation platform using
patented AI technology
to now a blog automation, a google web stories automation, a newsletter automation, a augmented reality for collaboration of marketing content, a smart speaker / amazon Alexa automation and much more. We've left our competition in the dust. And with your help, having raised over $1.1M in equity crowdfunding to date, have made this mission a reality...
every dollar pushed the mission forward, the team grew and our platform provided more to underserved, underrepresented small business owners
...we're also proud to mention majority of our customers are
women led businesses
in real estate, coaching, podcasting etc. We're proud to serve.
Now, did you know our latest equity crowdfunding campaign is about to end in just a few days? See the ending of this article to learn about the opportunity!
Have you seen our augmented reality demo for remote based ad agencies and small businesses working from home, hybrid etc.? Check this out!
Are you a customer at HelloWoofy.com?

Impressive Results:
That's right, we've done nearly
$750,000 in revenue to date
charging 1/10 - 1/15 the prices our leading competitors such as Buffer, SproutSocial, MeetEdgar, RecurPost, AgoraPulse etc. charge providing 5-10x more capabilities. Did we mention unlike a lot of our competitors, we actually have
FULLY ISSUED AI PATENTS
? We project (no guarantees and only projections subject to change) doing nearly $1M in revenue in 2022 (or more). What an exciting year it has been!
Jim Edwards, World Renowned Copywriter Invests + Buys Hundreds of Licenses (Business Partner with Russell Brunson, Founder of Click Funnels)
Yup, from a random facebook ad to an email from Jim to a series of zoom meetings to an investment AND purchase of hundreds of licenses of HelloWoofy.com, Jim believed in us from the beginning. His insights and continued support have helped push our platform forward so much so we even integrated with his platform, serving small businesses owners around the world. The partnership has been a huge success in not only bringing HelloWoofy.com to hundreds of underdogs but also allowing us to begin thinking about how to integrate with existing communities of underdogs technologically via APIs etc. Strong point for expansion in 2022.
