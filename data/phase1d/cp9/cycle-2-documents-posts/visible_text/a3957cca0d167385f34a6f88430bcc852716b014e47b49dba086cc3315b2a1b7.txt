Janover is raising more capital 🚀 🚀 🚀
Janover is raising more capital!
We love Republic and had an amazing experience here and aim to be back in the not-too-distant future. All things considered, we decided to run our new round on StartEngine and are excited to share it with you here.
Here are the facts:
Janover Ventures raised its first round on Republic. We match commercial real estate and business borrowers with the right lenders, making commercial financing… frictionless.
-
Janover launched its all new lender and borrower facing software to its entire ecosystem of more than 1
million
website users per year.
- We converted our FL LLC to a DE C Corp in preparation for this momentous raise!
-
2020 audited financials are in, and here’s the growth (2019 to 2020):
*Revenue grew from: $549,325 to $1,561,183 —
Nearly 3X revenue representing 184% top line g
rowth!
*Income from operations grew from: $102,830 to $393,388 —
282% gro
wth!
*Net income grew from: $104,131 to $319,025 —
206% growth!
Yea, we’re one of those rare revenue generating, profitable startups. 🤑
-
Q1 was the highest revenue quarter Janover's ever had!
(using accrual accounting method, consistent with our audit)
- More than $1B
(yes,
billion dollars
)
in loans have been submitted
to us through our new portal.
-
More than a dozen banks and other lenders have been onboarded to the new lender portal in 2021.
-
More than $180,000,000 in closed loans
in 2020!
- And now we are offering common equity to investors for the first time... EVER.
That's it! You can learn more about our new round over on StartEngine.
