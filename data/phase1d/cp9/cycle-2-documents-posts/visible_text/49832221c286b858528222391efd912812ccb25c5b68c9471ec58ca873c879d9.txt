Ternio 2020 Year In Review Video, Plaid Partnership, and More! 🎄
Another week, another update!  Our team is still going strong through the end of the year and I have a few more things I want to share.
Ternio - 2020 Year In Review
2020 was Ternio's best year yet!  It can be hard to see how far you’ve come unless you take the time to reflect on where you started.  For Ternio's 2020 Year In Review video, Daniel and I take a few minutes to talk about the progress the Ternio team made in 2020.   It's 38 minutes, but I well worth the watch when you have time.
Watch Now
Ternio + Plaid Partnership
We have partnered with Plaid to help streamline the onboarding process for users with our crypto-friendly bank accounts.  Our customers will now be able to connect outside financial accounts to their BlockCard bank accounts faster and more securely while continuing to receive exemplary customer support.

https://ternio.io/ternio-integrates-with-plaid-to-streamline-crypto-friendly-blockcard-bank-accounts/

Brave Takeover!
I'm a little late on this, but if you opened up your Brave browser today you will see we did a sponsored take over!  Ternio was one of Brave's early ads partners and we're constantly looking at ways to leverage their platform and gain more users on BlockCard!   Here are some of the sponsored images you will see.
