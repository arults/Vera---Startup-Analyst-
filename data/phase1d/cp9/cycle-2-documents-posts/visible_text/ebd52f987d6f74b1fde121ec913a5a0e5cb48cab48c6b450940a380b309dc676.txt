Last chance to invest at current terms - offering officially ending next week
I’d like to share some consequential news related to the Applaudable investment offering. Our Board of directors has confirmed our intention to end our capital raise as scheduled in 11 days. On June 4, 2022, we will end access through Republic, and the investment will no longer be available to the public.
In order to achieve our product development and user growth objectives, we are targeting $2mm and will focus on filling any remaining amount through larger investors offline.
We believe that $2mm will enable us to release the web version of Applaudable and acquire 500k total users. We are targeting 50% regular monthly activity among these users, and this is something we believe we will be well-positioned to achieve.
While there are other moving parts, we expect that achieving our product release and user growth/activity objectives would position us to raise a Series B at a significant premium to the current $35mm valuation. While we may make this phase of the offering public, this is not yet decided. We are also considering and having early discussions with institutional investors who may be a better fit for our capital needs.
The offering is live and available at the $35mm valuation. To learn more, visit
republic.com/Applaudable
.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
Founder & CEO
Andrew Christodoulides
