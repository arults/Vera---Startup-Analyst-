CricClubs launches sports fantasy platform CC Fantasy
CricClubs
proudly launches the ideal platform for fantasy users
"CC FANTASY"
accessible to users across the world. CC Fantasy provides a platform for players to create/customise teams and compete with each other in multiple contests to execute their cricketing knowledge and skills and win big.
You can download both the Android and iOS App at
www.ccfantasy.com.
We hope you will have a great time playing on the CC Fantasy App and share the app across your cricket enthusiastic friends and family.
Ganesh Nallapareddy
