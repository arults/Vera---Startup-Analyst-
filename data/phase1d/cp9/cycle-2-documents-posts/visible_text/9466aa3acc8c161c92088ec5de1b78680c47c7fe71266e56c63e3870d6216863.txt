CytoCatch™ is here🔥🔥🔥
Hello everyone!
We hope that you and yours are well and safe.
After many months of work and with all the hardships that the COVID-19 pandemic entailed,
we are thrilled to announce that we have completed the final version of the CytoCatch™ isolation platform
🔥
Here at Delee we’re proud of our team for achieving this milestone during these uncertain times.
CytoCatch™ isolation platform
The next step for us is to clinically validate this unit using blood samples from cancer patients and healthy donors, so that we can begin the commercialization of our technology as a research use only in vitro diagnostic platform. As for the CytoCatch™ imaging system, it is currently being manufactured and we expect to have the final version of the unit ready in the next couple of weeks🚀
To continue with this venture we need to scale in every sense, and for that we are launching a new crowdfunding campaign on StartEngine
Thank you for believing in us!
We're gonna be sending you updates via email. Visit
www.delee.co
and follow us on social media.
Liza Velarde
