ImPRESSive
Our new 400 ton press arrived. In the constant battle to eliminate bottlenecks in production we decided we needed a bigger press just devoted to bending long sections of heavy steel. Our supplier of bent rail sections sometimes would be 5 weeks out. sometimes 10 weeks. It is hard to build trailers when you are waiting 10 weeks for a single part. The 56,000 pound press just barely fit through the door.
