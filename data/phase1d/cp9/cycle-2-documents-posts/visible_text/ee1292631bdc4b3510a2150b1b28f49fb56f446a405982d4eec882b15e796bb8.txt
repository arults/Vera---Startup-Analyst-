🚀 Exciting Update: Support Blue Social in the OnChain Summer BASE Buildathon!
Dear Republic Investors,
We have some exciting news to share with you! Blue Social has officially submitted our project for the
OnChain Summer BASE Buildathon
, sponsored by Coinbase. This is a tremendous opportunity for us to gain significant exposure, as winners will be announced on Coinbase's social channels.
Over the past 30 days, our team has worked tirelessly, making incredible progress in integrating Web3 into Blue Social. We are now
LIVE
on the
BASE Sepolia Testnet
, with smart contracts that enable you to MINE tokens from your in-person social interactions.
As part of our commitment to delivering the best possible outcome for Blue Social, we have decided to hold off on launching the BLUE token on UniSwap. Instead of rushing, we are taking this time to conduct extensive testing on the testnet and ensure our smart contracts undergo thorough security audits. This careful approach will help us avoid any potential issues and ensure a secure and smooth launch.
We are incredibly grateful for the dedication and hard work of our team and the support of our amazing community. We couldn't have made this progress without you!
To help us WIN the Buildathon, please visit our submission page and create a Devfolio account to send us some love with a like and comment. Your vote of confidence will help us win and get noticed by Coinbase.
Let's make Blue Social the next big thing in social interactions. 🌐💙
Thank you for your continued support and understanding.
Best regards,
Jose Montero & Blue Social Team
* If you'd like to be a beta tester for our Web3 application on Testnet please visit:
https://web3.blue.social
Jose Montero
