ACFN Expanding Relationships & New Higher Margin Markets
We just rolled out multiple initiatives to best manage our business and take us to the next level. We continue to secure high value agreements growing and developing our core business primarily with hotels and malls.
Growing High-Value Relationships
As we continue to develop our relationship with Brookfield, one of the largest mall operators in the US now 80 units strong, we recently successfully contracted with Simon malls. Simon Property Group is the largest owner/operator of malls in the US and we submitted multiple bids for some of their locations over the past 10 years. In September we were successful in securing contracts to service 6 locations and have since secured 3 more. All have been installed already and with 200+ retail properties in the US there is a lot of room for this relationship to grow. Also, notably we recently secured an agreement to install 15 units at the Mall of America which is the single largest mall in the US. This was an independent deal and is expected to be a high performer. All units were installed this month and we are off to a great start.
Expanding to
New
Higher Margin Markets
We just completed preparation of marketing materials for a new marketing campaign. We launched the campaign 2 weeks ago and franchise owners are now engaged. There are three initiatives:
Casinos – casinos are the top performing locations with highest net earnings per ATM for the owner franchisee. This is due to much higher volume vs our typical location as well as much higher surcharges. We have been providing services to many casinos in Vegas for many years but we also recently designed a specific flyer targeting casinos and with only a few franchisees participating have already secured several lucrative casino locations in other states.
Cannabis – initially there were extra fees charged by networks and processors to place ATMs at Cannabis locations. The extra fees were related to extra compliance work. As part of our renewal with our processor we have been able to secure better terms that now allows us
to
place ATMs at Cannabis locations without the extra fees
. These locations can be very good producers and we just designed a new flyer specifically targeting this market.
3 in 1 combo - we are in the process of vetting a new unit that has ATM, Reverse ATM and Bill Break all in one unit. It is expensive and only applicable to top locations but we believe this product has strategic importance when pursuing top locations. These will only be offered with a required minimum guarantee paid by the location to offset the higher cost. We have already received multiple indications of interest in this product from top locations.
We see a lot of opportunity for growth in 2026 and look forward to reporting continued success in the coming months.
Happy Holidays!
Jeffrey D. Kerr
American Consumer Financial Network
