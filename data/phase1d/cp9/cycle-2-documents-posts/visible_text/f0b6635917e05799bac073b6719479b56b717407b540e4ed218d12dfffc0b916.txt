One billion users
I wanted to share the results of our executive team’s offsite where we aligned on our vision for where we can take our business.  We are totally committed to delivering a better, more authentic, and more meaningful social media experience for our users.
We articulate this fundamental purpose as Connecting people through their greatest experiences, and it suffuses everything we do.
And our ultimate aspiration is within 10 years, to become the world’s preeminent platform for capturing and sharing the experiences users love, with 1 billion users, and 100 million experiences shared daily.
These numbers aren’t pulled out of thin air; they’re based on the same detailed bottoms-up methodology we detailed in our
previous update
on how we get to 7.4 million users in Year 1 after launch.
We believe our approach is fundamentally better than Instagram and Facebook and that we have a real shot to upend the $177B social media ad market.  And if we do, we’ll have built one of the most valuable new media properties ever created.  We’ll host a webinar shortly to provide more details about the methodology behind our aspirations.
And finally, check out our updated
Deal Offering
while it is still live for a few more days at
https://republic.com/applaudable
.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
