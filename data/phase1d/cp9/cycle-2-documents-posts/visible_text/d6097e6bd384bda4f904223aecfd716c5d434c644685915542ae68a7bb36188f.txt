Your BLUE Tokens Are Coming Soon! Get Ready for the UniSwap Launch!
Hello Blue Social Fam,
Exciting news ahead! Your BLUE tokens from our PreSale last year are about to be airdropped to your wallets! 🎉 As one of our early supporters, we're deeply grateful for your unwavering trust and belief in our vision.
Your BLUE tokens symbolize more than just a digital asset; they represent your commitment to leading the charge in revolutionizing social networking. With BLUE tokens, you're not just supporting our platform; you're supporting a future where social interactions are rewarded and valued like never before.
But that's not all! We minted the BLUE tokens last week and are thrilled to announce that the official launch of BLUE tokens on
UniSwap
is fast approaching, scheduled for
May 1st, 2024
. This is a monumental milestone for us, and we're ecstatic to share it with you.
As we gear up for the UniSwap launch, we invite you to join us in building anticipation for this historic event. With BLUE tokens soon available for trading on one of the largest decentralized exchanges globally, we're opening doors to new opportunities for liquidity and market access.
And here's where you come in! To ensure the smooth operation of our token on UniSwap and to prevent price manipulation, we're calling on you to contribute to our liquidity pool. Your participation will play a crucial role in maintaining stability and fairness in the market. Message me directly if you'd like to help support the liquidity pool and earn fee's from Swaps.
Once again, we want to extend our heartfelt thanks for your incredible support and belief in Blue Social. Your contributions have been instrumental in bringing us to where we are today, and we're thrilled to continue this journey with you.
Stay tuned for more updates and announcements as we count down to the UniSwap launch on May 1st. Together, we're shaping the future of social networking and creating a more rewarding and connected world.
Make sure to update your mobile app to see the latest updates!
-> Update Now <-
Best regards,
Jose Montero & the Blue Social Team
