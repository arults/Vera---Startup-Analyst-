Q42023 Update - New Features and A New Line of Business
Hello investors,
We are off to a good start in 2024, and I'd like to share a few highlights:
Portoflio Widget Pilot
:
As you know, partnership with financial creators is critical to our mission of removing chaos from fundamental investing and making investment decisions more accessible and based on facts and data. We always work closely and stay in touch with successful creators. More than 80 creators are in Stock Card's partnership program. Earlier this year, we learned that there is a more significant opportunity to grow our creator toolkit by giving them access to our tech platform.
We officially launched a Portfolio Widget Pilot program, and we are collaborating closely with creators to learn about their needs and build tools that help their workflow be easier. This feature can create a new revenue stream for Stock Card while enabling financial creators to grow their respective businesses. I will share the results of this pilot in my next update.
You can already see the momentum of this product feature:
Here's Steve Symington
, the creator behind Bottmline Investing, talking about how he is happy about the feature.
And
here's Matt Wolodarsky
, aka The Wealthy Owl, launching his portfolio using our Widget.
Other Highlights:
We've seen some uptake in the overall engagement due to better-than-expected market conditions. Consequently, we saw higher revenue in January than in December, and we expect the trend to continue into February and March 2024.
We are exploring new products and features on the content creation side of the business. It's too early to share details. However, I'd like to highlight that we have onboarded two new B2B customers to our newly emerging Content Services line of business in January and February. I will share more about this new line of business in my future updates.
Next Update:
My next update will be sometime in April, and I hope to share our progress with you then.
In the meantime, subscribe to our content on our growing
YouTube
channel
, listen to it on
Podcast
, and read it on our
blog
. And, of course, keep researching your stock and ETF ideas on Stock Card.
Thank you for your support and interest in Stock Card! Let me know if you have ideas, questions, or comments.
