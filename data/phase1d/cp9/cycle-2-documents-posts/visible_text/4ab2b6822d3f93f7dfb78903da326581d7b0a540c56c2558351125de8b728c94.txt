It has been more than six years since we closed our crowdfunding round on Republic, which is still the most recent opportunity we’ve offered for investors to participate in Ginkgo’s growth. Since then, we’ve focused on building the product, refining our market, and growing the business without raising additional capital.
What began as Citiesense has evolved into Ginkgo, a GeoCRM platform built for organizations working to improve the public realm as a means catalyzing and sustaining economic growth stakeholders and for surrounding communities.
Today, Ginkgo primarily serves Business Improvement Districts (BIDs) and other place management organizations, while also expanding into adjacent areas of civic technology and economic development.
Product development has accelerated significantly over the past year thanks to AI-assisted development tools like ChatGPT and Codex, allowing us to move faster with a lean team.
If you’re curious whether a downtown or Main Street near you might benefit from a place management program, you can explore our District Analyzer—currently available as a free demo:
https://labs.ginkgo.city
Thank you again to the early investors who supported the vision.
- Starling
Founder, Ginkgo
