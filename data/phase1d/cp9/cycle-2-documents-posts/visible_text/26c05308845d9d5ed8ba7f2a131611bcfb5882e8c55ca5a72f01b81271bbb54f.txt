Popularium: Q2 2025 Update
Thank you for your support of Popularium. Our Republic campaign raised a total of $211K, over 4x of our initial $50K goal, and these additional funds have contributed to the rapid progress for Popularium and the development of Chaos Agents. This has also paved the path for a seed round extension which will further solidify Popularium’s runway pre Series-A.
Here are the highlights for Popularium from Q2 2025:
1. Alpha Expansion and Sold Out Live Events in SF, LA, Boston, and NY
The expansion of the Chaos Agents Private Alpha to 1000+ players corresponded with the Second World Championship and roadshow of live events featuring Gold Trials – tournaments that qualify players for the Grand Finals of the Second World Championship.
The Trials were a smashing success, with hundreds of players getting their first taste of Chaos Agents, and the Popularium team able to observe new players learning the game–invaluable new user testing! Watch these 1-minute long highlights from the events:
Brooklyn, NY: Gold Trial #3 highlights.
Boston and LA: Gold Trial #2 highlights.
SF: Gold Trial #1 highlights.
2. Chaos Agents Alpha Signup cross 50,000 players
The Alpha Expansion and live events have taken the overall signups for the Chaos Agents Alpha well past 50,000 players. While we don’t plan to expand the private Alpha beyond the planned 5000 active players, it is a tremendous asset for Popularium to have 10x the number of Alpha players than we have room for. This gives us a lot of resources, at low to no cost, to test the Chaos Agents onboarding and engagement funnel.
3. New
Gameplay Trailer
to celebrate 50k signups and enhanced gameplay and visuals
The v1.9.3.4 and later updates have brought incredible upgrades to the visuals and user experience for Chaos Agents.
To celebrate these enhancements and reaching 50k Alpha signups, the Popularium team has released a new
Gameplay Trailer for Chaos Agents
– enjoy!
4.
New
Gameplay videos
, V1.9.3.5 and v1.9.3.6 launch and the upcoming 1.9.4 update
Check out this new
gameplay video
from the latest builds–and there’s a whole
playlist of gameplay
for you to enjoy if you want more!
2 new updates for Chaos Agents – v1.9.3.5 and v1.9.3.6 launched to players in Q2 2025, with:
Much improved graphics and UX,
The first version of the player’s Chaos Broker Vaults,
The all-new Agent Rank system (similar to MMR/ELO for other games),
The first prototype of the Chaos Surfers Fantasy Game
In-match tutorials and updated new user experience … and much more
The team is now focused on the game-changing v1.9.4 release, which will introduce several new features that will fundamentally change the Chaos Agents gameplay experience and set us up for the expansion to 5000 active players.
5. The road to 5000 Actives and Alpha Expansion #2
After the 1.9.4 release, the team will accelerate its efforts to get to 5k+ active players – a key milestone to enable the Chaos Agents Early Access launch
in addition to the v1.9.4 product launch, the team has expanded its community and marketing team to bring on 2 new team members with significant experience in community growth and management.
The team will now execute on an influencer-based growth campaign that will take advantage of the various v1.9.4 features, including team-based match modes that should be a major driver of growth and engagement.
6. Series A roadshow prep and major deals in pipeline
After wrapping the seed extension round this month, we will start preparation for Popularium’s Series A financing. We have onboarded a key partner to help us manage the Series A roadshow and tap into investor networks beyond the US.
Besides Series A preparations, the Popularium team is also working on finalizing additional publishing and distribution deals over H2 2025.
7. Request from the Team: Join us for a VIP Session
As part of our Alpha expansion, the Popularium team is hosting a series of “VIP Sessions” to onboard and engage our investor, advisor, friends-and-family ecosystem to join and play Chaos Agents.
Joining a session gets you a fast-track into the next Gold Trial tournament and chance to play in the Grand Finals of the Second Chaos Invitational Championship.
We would be thrilled if you joined a VIP Session and our Chaos Agents community–reply directly to me at
arka@popularium.com
and we’ll get you all set!
Let Chaos Reign!
- Arka and the Popularium team
