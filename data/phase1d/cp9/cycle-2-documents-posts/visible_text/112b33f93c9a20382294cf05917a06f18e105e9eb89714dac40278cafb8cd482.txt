Rocket Dollar chosen to join accelerator and receive investment from Alllianz Life & Securian Financial
https://tech.mn/news/2021/05/04/onramp-insurance-accelerator-announces-2021-cohort
What to know about the
OnRamp Insurance Accelerator 2021
cohort:
The OnRamp Insurance Accelerator 2021 (from
gener8tor
and partners
Allianz Life
and
Securian Financial
) has announced its five-business cohort (the lineup is below with descriptions from gener8tor)
Around 500 businesses applied to the program
Participants receive a $100,000 investment and individualized coaching and mentorship from financial and insurance professionals
The three-month program will culminate in July with a month-long series of investor meetings and a Premier Night
The Quote:
Elizabeth Caven, Managing Director of the OnRamp Insurance Accelerator
We are incredibly impressed with this group of resilient and innovative entrepreneurs working on solutions ranging from helping older adults age in place to empowering individuals with the freedom to diversify their retirement savings into crypto or real estate investments. The cohort also includes some deep technical solutions for the insurance and financial services industries. The Securian Financial, Allianz Life and gener8tor teams are all thrilled to welcome each of these companies to the 2021 program.”
Brooke Borden
