Investor Update: Status Money 2.0
Dear Investors,
I hope this message finds you well. Since our last update, Status Money has been through significant changes and challenges. Here’s a summary of the key events:
1. Credit Card Launch:
As we shared in the last update, Status Money launched an innovative credit card focused on helping spenders become investors – it offered an industry-leading 2% cashback on every purchase that could be auto-invested in Bitcoin. Within weeks, more than 200K people had joined the waiting list.
2. Breach of Contract by Railsr:
Unexpectedly, days before the card application was set to open to the public, Railsr, our credit card program manager, abruptly ceased its US operations, including our program, with no transition period. This was a flagrant breach of their contractual obligations, and Status Money swiftly filed a lawsuit, which was subsequently settled.
3. Transition to Quicken:
Against a backdrop of a difficult funding environment, increasing regulatory scrutiny of banking-as-a-service programs, and the breach of contract by Railsr, Status Money was unable to relaunch its credit card program, raise additional funding, or chart a course to profitability. So we made the difficult strategic decision to transition our 1.3 million userbase to Quicken Simplifi in November and deactivate the Status Money app. I can't express how disappointing and heartbreaking it is to shutter something you loved so much, worked so hard on, and believed in so deeply.
4. Focus on Intellectual Property:
Despite these setbacks, our core intellectual property, including our unique social PFM, peer benchmarking system, and customer engagement engine, remained in place and attractive to a variety of enterprises. So we pivoted to focus on commercializing this intellectual property.
5. Shift to Enterprise Software:
In line with this shift, we have secured three software licensing agreements to date, including a deal with one of the largest credit card affiliate marketplaces in the US – powering the way they target offers to their users.
Current Status and Future Outlook
While the past year has presented unforeseen obstacles, the Status Money team remains present, resilient, and optimistic for the future. Our revamped website reflects our new direction, and you can explore it at:
https://statusmoney.com/
As we navigate these changes, I want to express my gratitude for your unwavering support. Despite the user transition to Quicken and the shift in our business model, all Republic SAFE notes remain intact.
Thank you
Majd Maksad
Status Money
