Updates
Nov  4
2021
New Press, New Products, New Features, and More 🔥
Hi Everyone - Apologies for the delay from the last update.  Things have been busy!  I have a lot to share so let's get right into it.
Press
As you know, Unbanked partnered with Plaid to help streamline the movement of cash to the Unbanked bank accounts.  Plaid and Unbanked partnered to put out a case study which you can find here:
https://plaid.com/blog/unbanked-customer-story/
Financial NewTech Challenge 2021Win!
We participated in the Financial NewTech Challenge hosted by EFMA and Capgemini.  Unbanked won in the Banking Startup category!  You can read more on the link below.
https://www.efma.com/article/16431-capgemini-and-efma-announce-winners-of-the-financial-newtech-challenge-2021
Bitcoin Magazine
To help promote our crypto-friendly bank accounts for residents of over 200+ countries/territories we did a paid article in Bitcoin Magazine.  Many countries do not allow consumers to purchase Bitcoin using Fiat currency, so Unbanked is the perfect solution for them!
https://bitcoinmagazine.com/business/with-bitcoin-unbanked-brings-secure-finances-to-the-world
Venture Atlanta
Unbanked was invited to participate again in Venture Atlanta.   It was good to get back to in-person events and start seeing people again!  The presentation is only 3 minutes long, so I recommend taking the time to watch and learn where we're at with Unbanked!
Crypto Payment Profile
We recently released the
Crypto Payment Profile.
It makes receiving crypto easier than ever before by enabling any Unbanked account holder to create a custom profile and set a username which is then attached to a public profile URL. Users can also further enhance and personalize their profile with a photo, bio, and relevant social media links.
Once a Crypto Payment Profile is created, customers can select from hundreds of assets and collect cryptocurrency payments directly to a self-custodial wallet (or any wallet they designate). There is no charge for this feature.  And the best part? Setup takes only seconds and can be turned on or off at any time.
TERN > UNBNK swap
In June of 2021, we rebranded to Unbanked.  Our new identity speaks to the full suite of crypto-friendly services we offer and helps people “get unbanked” from the legacy financial system. Unbanked was founded on the belief that financial access and control is a fundamental human right, best empowered by the blockchain. As part of this rebranding effort, we are going to be updating our digital asset acronym from
TERN to UNBNK
(pronounced Unbanked).
For any of you that hold TERN, you can find a guide I created here as to what to expect in the coming weeks.
https://unbanked.com/announcing-the-tern-to-unbnk-swap/
UNBNK.com
Launch
I'm pleased to announce the next leg of our journey with the launch of the
UNBNK.com website
.  As many of you know, the Unbanked team has committed to supporting the DeFi ecosystem over the past few months.  We have told many of you about our upcoming self-custodial wallet and today, we are announcing our intention to build a DeFi protocol for NFT collateralized loans as well as cryptocurrency swaps.
The UNBNK website will operate under the same acronym as our digital asset.   It will serve as the destination for Unbanked’s DeFi products as well as information regarding our digital asset. As a reminder, there are two native versions of UNBNK on Ethereum and Stellar.   The Ethereum based version of UNBNK will be used on UNBNK.com due to the smart contract functionality.  The Stellar version will remain the asset used on our card products due to its cheap and fast transactions.
Reg A+
Our Reg A+ is still in process, but I do not have any meaningful updates to share at this time.  We are currently in process of corresponding with the SEC and supplying them with additional information they have requested.   The process can be slow going, but I'm hoping I'll have something more material to share in the next update!
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
