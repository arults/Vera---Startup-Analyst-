The BIG announcement: We can turn any truck into a plug in electric hybrid.
25% of emissions from 5% of vehicles
5% of the vehicles on the road are heaavy trucks but they account for 25% of emissions. Only 1% of heavy truck production is expected to be Electric in 2024. Why? Electric semi trucks are expensive, and they have very limited range.
QuickLoadz has solved this problem
QuickLoadz has worked on designing and testing an Eaxle system for years. In late 2023 QuickLoadz partnered with Horizon Motor to bring their Eaxle system to market. Initial response has led us to pivot/expand this sub-product into a product that can immediately be installed on any trailer. This turns any combination vehicle into a plug-in hybrid. 30% better fuel econ makes this ROI work for ICE trucks. This enormous range extension makes electric trucks work. At the TMC show we rolled out the Eaxle, At the ACT show in Las Vegas we will bring a completed container chassis with this system installed.
Step 1, replace the rear axle on container chassis. Turn that container chassis into a plug in electric hybrid with 30% fuel savings. Plus..... add an invertor and plug in refrigerated sea shipping containers, eliminate gen set emissions and collect some hefty incentives.
Step 2, replace the rear axle on refrigerated dry freight vans, get 30% fuel savings, much longer life than the diesel powered coolers currently on refrigerated dry freight vans.
Step 3, replace the rear axle on any heavy truck. In a day you could turn any heavy truck into a plug in electric hybrid with 30% fuel savings. All of the advantages of diesel, long range, quick fill up and all of the advantages of the plug in hybrid.
Size of the market
There are 2 million ICE heavy trucks just in the US. there are 5.6 million semi trailers. The market is vast.
The market is now. We have these Eaxles here, now, and we are installing them. Not next year, not 2026, NOW.
How's that for a BIG announcement?
Sean Jones
QuickLoadz
