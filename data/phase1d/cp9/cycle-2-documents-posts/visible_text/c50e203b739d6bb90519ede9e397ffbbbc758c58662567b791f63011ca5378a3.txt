An update you don't want to miss! 🔥
Hi Everyone, it's been a minute since the last update and there are lots of things happening on our end.   This update will be packed, so let's get right to the details!
Lots of Press!
We have been getting a lot of coverage lately which has been great.   Here are some of the newest articles that have come out.
1) Daniel had an opinion piece in Newsweek with Chris Kelly about the recent infrastructure bill.
Chris Kelly is co-owner of the Sacramento Kings, an investor in crypto businesses and former head of global public policy and chief privacy officer of Facebook.
https://www.newsweek.com/cryptocurrency-language-infrastructure-bill-detrimental-our-future-opinion-1618905
2) I spoke to Invezz about the recent infrastructure bill.
https://invezz.com/news/2021/08/19/unbankeds-kane-on-infrastructure-bill-crypto-will-win-the-war/
3) BTC Manager covered us - Why Some Consumers Want To “Get Unbanked” -
https://btcmanager.com/why-some-consumer-unbanked/
4) Bitcoinist covered us - "Fintech Entrepreneurs Launching Blockchain Cards with Unbanked"
https://bitcoinist.com/fintech-entrepreneurs-launching-blockchain-cards-with-unbanked/
5) I did an interview with Charlotte Biz Journals - "Unbanked's debit card gives customers a way to use their cryptocurrency like cash" -
https://www.bizjournals.com/charlotte/inno/stories/profiles/2021/09/10/unbanked-democratizes-digital-assets.html
6) Did anyone catch Charlie Lee (founder of Litecoin) on Reuters this week?  LTC made some headlines and Charlie mentioned the Litecoin Card and the partnership we formed to make it possible.  Here's a clip:
https://twitter.com/UnbankedHQ/status/1438159196697075713?s=20
VISA Certified Us For Program Management
Press Release:
https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/09/16/2298623/0/en/Unbanked-Receives-Visa-Ready-Certification-for-Digital-Currency-Program-Management.html
It's official! Unbanked has been certified for Program Management within the Visa Fast Track Program.  Last year, Unbanked was approved as a Fast Track Enablement Partner for Card Issuance and this new certification expands our crypto capabilities for all of our white-label partners.
Unbanked is the infrastructure powering many of the top cryptocurrency debit cards. As a Visa Enablement Partner, Unbanked provides the toolkit and expertise you need to get up and running quickly with Visa products.
Startup Incentives
Fast Onboarding
Proven Expertise and Support
Robust APIs
It's great to deepen our partnership and expand our capabilities with a company like VISA.  You can learn more about our VISA capabilities on our website.
https://unbanked.com/business-solutions/visa-fast-track/
New Video Ad
Keep your eyes open for our latest video advertisements!  We have been testing recently on youtube with some great results.
New Features
We have turned on the ability to purchase Litecoin from your Unbanked bank account.  Instantly convert dollars to LTC and settle your Litecoin purchase to your self-custodial wallet.
Read more about the functionality here:
https://unbanked.com/litecoin-arrives-at-unbanked/
BCH Deposits are live!
For a period of time, our team disabled Bitcoin Cash deposits as their network was undergoing some changes. However, I am happy to report that BCH deposits are live once again.   You can log in to your Unbanked dashboard and start funding your account.

A big "thank you" to Raymond Brown and Fares Ksebati who both took the time to sit down with me and discuss why they invested in Unbanked and what they believe the future holds.  Both videos will be used on our upcoming Reg A+ campaign page for new investors.
Reg A+ Update!
I am happy to report that we have completed our 3rd party auditor review (for all years in business) and all documentation has been submitted to the SEC.   We are now in SEC review and have received their first response of questions and comments.   This is part of the Reg A+ process to help the SEC understand our business and allow them to go through the process of checking into everything the 3rd party auditors covered.
I'll be candid, it's a huge undertaking, but we're happy to be at this point.   I can't wait to be able to share more details with everyone once we're allowed.
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
