🏦More Press, Bank Accounts Are Live, New Card Designs, & Metal Card Update!
Hi Everyone - I have a lot to share so let's get right to it! 😎
Press and Media Coverage
Daniel was recently on "The Wolf of All Streets" podcast hosted by Scott Melker.  If you're not familiar,
Scott Melker has a large following on Twitter
and provides advice on different crypto assets.   He and Daniel discussed a number of things - his personal back story, what it's like building
Ternio
, and where the industry is headed!
The interview is just over an hour and you can watch it here:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_vfJQP3USU
In addition, I was on the "Making Waves at C-Level" hosted by Thom Singer.  This interview gives a general overview of what we're up to at Ternio and a high-level discussion around cryptocurrency and blockchain.  You can listen to the full interview here:
https://thomsinger.com/podcast/ternio/
BlockCard Bank Accounts are live!
Our BlockCard Bank Accounts are live and any
US resident
can now login to their BlockCard dashboard to pass KYC and activate their account.   Upon completing KYC you will be given an account + routing number to make your first deposit.  You can also withdraw USD funds as needed.  This will work like any normal bank account.
Next stop.... bank accounts for international users!
Buy Crypto
Bank Accounts are open, but the "buy crypto" function is not turned on for most users.  I am simultaneously testing that feature as I type this. 😀  We will start with a
buy bitcoin
option and then move onto other crypto assets.  We hope to have that turned on in the next week or two.  There will also be a "sell" option in the future so customers can easily liquidate their holdings into USD.
Sneak Peek at Metal Cards
These have been a long time coming and have been in development for almost 2 years.  Too long!  I figured I would give all of our Republic supporters a sneak peek before everyone else.
Keep this between us!
New BlockCard Designs!
That's right! We have brand new BlockCard designs coming.   The existing card design is not going away anytime soon, but we thought it made sense to give people a number of options.  We are truly trying to design the
best crypto debit card
on the market.  I would like to get everyone's opinion on the below.
Please comment below on what card design you like the most.  You can also put them in a list from favorite to least favorite.
Investor Dashboard
The investor dashboard is coming, but is taking a little longer than initially planned.  Republic is still in the process of collecting the last remaining contributions from our investors.  Once that is 100% completed we will finalize things and notify all of you on when/where you can login.
That's all for now!  Great things are on the horizon and more to come soon.
Ian Kane
Unbanked (formerly Ternio BlockCard)
