HelloAva
Updates
Mar 17
2020
Thank you and welcome!
Our Republic campaign has come to a close and we are so grateful to all those who have supported HelloAva throughout this journey. We raised
$197,158
from
873 investors
and we couldn’t be more thrilled!
If you invested an amount that qualifies for our perks please stay tuned as we will be following up with you soon. We expect to receive each of your contact information from Republic, after which we will be able to contact you directly. We'll also continue to update you on our progress on Republic. We also encourage you to continue to follow us on the Republic Platform as well as our other professional and social media platforms as we will be providing regular updates, including email newsletter, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and more.
From everyone here at HelloAva, I'd like to welcome each new investor to the family. The progress we've made over the last few months has been incredibly exciting, yet it is only the beginning. We're looking forward to a very strong 2020 and, of course, beyond.
I also wanted to thank the amazing Republic team for recognizing and supporting our mission - they were amazing to work with throughout this process! If you're a founder yourself and would like to apply, I highly recommend them (you can apply here) Alternatively, if you know of a startup that would be a great fit for Republic, use the referral link here (and receive 1,500 Note if they launch!)
We can all agree that plans have changed over the last few days due to the development of coronavirus. As of today, COVID-19 will not have a major impact on our users other than slightly longer delivery times. These longer than usual delivery times are not only due to our decision to scale down our staff in our fulfillment center as an effort to keep our employees safe, but also due to USPS guidelines.
On behalf of the HelloAva team, we are extending our best wishes to you and your family. Take care of yourselves, your loved ones and stay healthy. We will be strong together again soon!
Thank you again for the support and we look forward to sharing the success of HelloAva with you.
Very Best,
Siqi Mou, Kailu Guan
Co-Founders of HelloAva
Kailu Guan
