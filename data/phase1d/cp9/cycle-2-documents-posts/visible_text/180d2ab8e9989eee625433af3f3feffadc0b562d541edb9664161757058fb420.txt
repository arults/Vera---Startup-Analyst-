We're live!
Dear Investor,
The momentum of our Applaudable social media platform is too powerful to ignore.
Starting today, you’ll see our focus double down on Applaudable, in everything we do as a business. And you’ll see our new focus on the deal page that went live today
here
.
Applaudable is just a better way to do social media.
Every day another story emerges about how
toxic
social media has become.  Instagram feeds are inundated with superficial posts, and sponsored content from advertisers.  And the utility of social media is lousy too: It’s nearly impossible to purchase items featured in posts, because the posts lack the needed data to transact.
That’s why some observers are saying Instagram is
dying
.
Applaudable is better.  It’s about appreciating life’s greatest experiences, not about toxic “living my best life” posts.  It’s about using our Social Search approach to find amazing, authentic content from people whose interests you share - not from advertisers.  And it’s about being able to seamlessly purchase the items you see in users’ posts - right on the Applaudable platform.
We think that's why our user registration numbers continue to grow explosively.  We originally hoped we would have 100,000 users registered for our beta launch in April. That's already more than Clubhouse achieved in its first six months. But we now think we will double –
or even triple
– that number.
Ecommerce remains a core business for us.  Moro, our first e-commerce marketplace in the home furnishing category, is showing excellent traction, with a $4mm revenue run rate. And we continue to look closely at launching ecommerce marketplaces in other verticals that, like home furnishings, are poorly merchandised and will benefit from the enhanced decision support that we can provide through Applaudable. Moro and each of our additional marketplaces will be integrated into Applaudable, enabling the purchasability of products directly from the user-generated photos in which they appear. But we will are approaching escape velocity with Applaudable.  And that’s where our focus will be.
We’re thrilled with our results to date and invite you to see the latest
here
.
With gratitude,
Andrew Christodoulides
Founder & CEO
Andrew Christodoulides
