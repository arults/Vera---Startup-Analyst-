We're onto something...
As the holidays are upon us (and as we brace for another wave of Covid disruptions) we wanted to share some good cheer.
We've told you how gratified we are that the pace of user registrations for our social media platform Applaudable, is not only on track to hit the target of 100,000 we set for our April launch; the pace has significantly accelerated and we now think we could double or even triple that number.
But it's not just the numbers.  We are thrilled and humbled to see how powerfully our core idea of sharing people's stories in the most personal and accessible way, is resonating.  I wanted to share a small sample of the comments we have from new users.  They give us pride, and conviction, that we're really onto something.
