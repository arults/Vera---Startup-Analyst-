Salsa God Taking Flight
Dear Investors -
We have been so excited to share an incredible update with you. Salsa God is now being sold on more than 900 daily flights in the U.S. That's right!
As of October 1, 2022, Salsa God's Chips & Salsa Combo Pack - Medium Heat is available for purchase on Spirit Airlines and Frontier Airlines!  We expect to serve close to 60,000 Combo Packs per month to hungry travelers!
We are very excited to partner with both of these airlines and bring authentic, tasty and spicy snacks to customers taking to the skies with Spirit and Frontier.
Post a picture on Instagram if you buy a Combo Pack on a flight!
Danny Mayans
Salsa God
