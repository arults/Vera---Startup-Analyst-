Q32023 Update
Stock Card content is featured by LinkedIn News Editors
Hello investors,
In my Q2 update, I told you about our content strategy in 2023. Through several experimentations, we have learned content produced by our team is one of the highest converting sources of user acquisition and retention for Stock Card. That's why we set out to produce high-value content and distribute it through Stock Card's YouTube, Podcast show, and blog.
Content marketing establishes trust and improves our brand awareness, but it takes time. I'm happy to report that we are gradually seeing the early results of our content strategy. A few achievements:
Financial media are picking up our content and redistributing it. Example: LinkedIn featuring our
piece on China's Real Estate Crisis
The average number of stocks researched by paying users each month, a key indicator of user engagement and retention, is up by 41% in the last three months, compared to the previous 4-month period. See the chart below.
Paying user conversion is at 2.8% -- an excellent conversion rate vs. the industry average.
Q4 Priorities:
Despite our achievements, overall market engagement is relatively low, and it is much harder to acquire new users. Our priority is experimenting and finding the most effective organic user acquisition channels. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is one of the most effective ways to acquire users, and we have developed a comprehensive roadmap of enhancements and improvements for Q4.
One of the most unique attributes of our product is how successful it is at engaging users. This means that even if the users are not converting to paying users, they still return to Stock Card to conduct their research and benefit from our free plan. We plan to monetize that free user engagement through referrals to brokerage and selective sponsorship across Stock Card. Exciting features are in the works, and I will report on our referral and advertising revenue progress in my next update.
Industry News:
Prominent fintech and WealthTech players have been quite active in the market, taking advantage of the stock market slowdown to acquire good products and teams. The most exciting and latest news is the
acquisition of CommonStock by Yahoo! Finance
. Congrats to our good friends at CommonStock!
Next Update:
My next update will be sometime in November, and I hope to share our progress with you then. In the meantime, watch our content on
YouTube
, listen to it on
Podcast
, and read it on our
blog
. And, of course, keep researching your stock and ETF ideas on Stock Card.
Thank you for your support and interest in Stock Card! Let me know if you have ideas, questions, or comments.
Hoda mehr - Founder and CEO
