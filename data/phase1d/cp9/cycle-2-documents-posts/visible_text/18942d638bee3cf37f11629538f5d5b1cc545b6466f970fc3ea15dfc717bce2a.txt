New Video, New CFO, New Awards, New Partners - New New New ✊
Hi Everyone - The weeks have been flying by and we have a lot going on at Ternio.  Apologies for the delay in getting this update pushed.
New Investor Video
This was a video we shot back in October/November and just took some time to get pushed out.  It features Ternio's leadership team consisting of Ian Kane (me), Daniel Gouldman, Keith Johnson, and Corey Ballou discussing Ternio's business and the growth opportunity in the fintech and crypto space.
It's a great primer for anyone who needs a TLDR on us! 😎
New CFO
We could not be hapier to welcome Natalia Radyushkin as our new CFO!
Press Link:
Ternio Adds Natalia Radyushkin Previously Of Bitcoin.com As CFO
"Radyushkin has worked with Bitcoin.com CEO, Roger Ver who is also a venture capital investor, since 2016, the release stated, and she served as has CFO of the company. Bitcoin.com provides bitcoin and bitcoin cash while offering services like an app, a digital currency wallet, cryptocurrency
exchange, widgets and charts, among other things."
📺 Natalia also did a video interview with PYMNTS which you can watch here:
https://www.pymnts.com/news/b2b-payments/2021/cfos-prepare-for-their-crypto-education/
“Historically, it has been difficult for companies conducting business and cryptocurrencies to set up traditional banking,” Radyushkin said, noting that this particular pain point is exactly why Ternio came to be in the first place."
In addition to Natalia, we have been hiring like crazy for many other open roles.  Our team has been growing fast so if you know anyone who could be a fit, please have them visit our careers page!
https://careers.ternio.io/
