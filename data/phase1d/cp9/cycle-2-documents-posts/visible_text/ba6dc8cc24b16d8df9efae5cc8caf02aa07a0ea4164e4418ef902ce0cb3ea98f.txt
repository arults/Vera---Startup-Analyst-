Final Update + Thank You!
We’re thrilled to share that Watrfall has officially met our fundraising goal of 1 million! This milestone was made possible by the incredible community of investors who believed in our vision to reshape entertainment through transparency, creativity, and shared ownership. To every supporter who joined us—thank you for being part of this movement. While this raise marks the end of our first chapter, the story of Watrfall is just beginning, and together we will be building the future of how stories are funded, made, and shared.
We’ll be in touch through email, our social channels, and our website
Watrfall.com
, so stay tuned. We are also working diligently on providing perks to the appropriate tier levels, so expect to hear from us soon!
You’re officially part of this story now, and we’d love for you to join us every step of the way.
Yours truly,
Dana Landry and Watrfall Team
Dana Landry
