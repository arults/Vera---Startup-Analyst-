Wayzn Q1/2024 Update!
Hi Wayzn Investors,
As we head into 2024, we're overdue for a Wayzn update!  During 2023 Wayzn continued on our mission to provide a beautiful, easy to install, and simple to operate smart home device that solves a real problem for pet owners.  Some of our most engaged customers continue to be first responders, disabled, and elderly who seriously need an easy way to let the pet in and out unattended.
Shipping since 2021, Wayzn has become a company with stable, growing revenue and strong potential. Some of our more recent accomplishments:
Migrated our D2C storefront from WooCommerce to Shopify to streamline operations
Updated the
Wayzn website
to better showcase the products and reduce returns
Hit some impressive benchmarks, like
5k
units shipped and nearly
10 million
open/close cycles
Continue to maintain our 5-star average on Google reviews
Accepted over 1,000 pre-orders for
Wayzn Pet Tag
and overcame significant challenges to complete development, with the product near to resuming final customer testing before general release.
Continued to develop our IP portfolio with a third patent award currently pending
Reached our highest inventory levels to date, with over
1k
finished Wayzn units in stock and
4k
more in process  along with
2k
sets of Pet Tag units procured.
Continued to receive requests to expand internationally to Canada and beyond
Despite these accomplishments, and being a company with stable revenue, our growth rate is much slower than desired. This is mainly due to being undercapitalized.  Post pandemic we simply lack the necessary capital to invest in inventory, advertising and testing new distribution channels. Without a large stockpile of inventory we can’t withstand a large increase in sales. Without an advertising budget we must grow mainly from word-of-mouth. And without available headcount we aren’t in a position to pursue expansion to Canada, Amazon or retail.
For this reason, we are once again placing our focus on raising working capital in the form of equity investment to speed growth. With a popular product, reliable sales and a leadership position in the market, and now with the addition of Wayzn Pet Tag, Wayzn is poised for success. The size and timing of that success, however, depend largely on our ability to raise additional capital.
The good news is that we don’t require millions of dollars. At this stage, an investment of even $1 million would have a huge impact because the R&D is already complete, and the NRE for Wayzn and the Pet Tag is already paid for. Any funds we raise now will go straight to building and selling inventory, and thus will directly impact top line revenue.
We’ve taken a decision not to resume fundraising on Republic at this time because the funds we believe we could raise are insufficient to move the needle. However, we are happy to accept additional investments on our current SAFE (with a $25k minimum) from previous investors. Just reach out and we can share details with you.
We appreciate your investment and your continued interest in the success of the company. Thanks for your support!
Adam and Mike
Mike Demele
Wayzn
