Epower!
EPOWER!! We used to only offer the 38HP EFI Kohler gasoline engine to power our trailers. In 2023 we started offering the all electric. Instantly 90% of the orders went all electric. Makes sense, always ready to go, always charged up, no engine to check on oil or gasoline. But it did create a scramble to build battery packs fast enough. Step 1) charge each cell to the top. Step 2) link all of the cells together and let them cross balance. Each step takes about a day, so I think we are going to need more battery assembly benches.
Don't forget to check out the new CF campaign that just went live on Republic!!
