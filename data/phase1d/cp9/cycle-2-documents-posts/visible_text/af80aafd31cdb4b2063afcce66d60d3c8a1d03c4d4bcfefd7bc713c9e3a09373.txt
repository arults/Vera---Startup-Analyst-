Updates
Jan  3
2020
Welcome!
As you probably already know, the WeLivv offering has officially ended as of December 31st, and we raised $1.07 million. This was the maximum allowed, and we're very excited to have filled it, allowing as many individuals as possible to invest at this relatively early stage. I sincerely apologize to everyone who was unable to invest before we closed out the offering. Given SEC regulations, we are not allowed to accept additional investments beyond the $1.07 million raised.
From everyone here at WeLivv, I'd like to welcome each new investor to the family. The progress we've made over the last few months has been incredibly exciting, yet it is only the beginning. We're looking forward to a very strong 2020 and, of course, beyond.
We expect to receive each of your contact information from Republic shortly, after which we will be able to contact you directly, which we'll do to coordinate perks, etc. We'll also continue to update you on our progress in line with our standard investor updates.
Lastly, I wanted to thank the amazing folks at Republic for seeing and supporting our vision - they were amazing to work with!
If you're a founder yourself and would like to apply, I highly recommend them (you can apply
here
) Alternatively, if you know of a startup that would be a great fit for Republic, use the
referral link here
(and receive 1,500 Note if they launch!)
Very Best,
Andrew
