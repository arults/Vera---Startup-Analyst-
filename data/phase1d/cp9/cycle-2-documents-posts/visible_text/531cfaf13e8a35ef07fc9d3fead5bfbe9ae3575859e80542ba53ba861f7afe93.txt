Major Investor Update
Hello Everyone,
I hope you’re doing great and thank you for supporting Base8 since the early days.
It’s been a while since our last update and it’s for good reason - we’ve been working really hard to completely rebuild the app!  (and we launched a brand new absolutely killer
site
)
https://base8.app/
The new version of the app is now available on
Apple/iOS
and
Google/Android
and here’s what makes it so much better:
Swipe to stack -
The simplest way to buy Bitcoin, just swipe and add SATs right from the home screen. (bet you can’t stack just once)
Trade with SATs -
Now you can swap Bitcoin directly for other cryptocurrencies, no cash and no self custody wallet required.  Other assets (stocks!) coming soon.
Plaid integration -
Connect your bank account in seconds, simple and secure account funding.
Expanded education and news -
Hundreds of expertly written articles, searchable and categorized for beginners, intermediate, or advanced investors.  Plus real-time news.
Improved onboarding -
Easier and faster account opening, plus you can explore the app without KYC.
Zero fees on Bitcoin -
We have negotiated an extremely low spread markup with our liquidity providers and we will pass this on to our more active users.
But wait, there’s (a LOT) more - we are already testing the next set of features that will be rolling out over the next 60 - 90 days:
Auto Stack SATs -
Setup recurring Bitcoin buys daily, weekly or monthly and sit back and let DCA do the rest.
Bitcoin Payments -
Use your SATs anywhere that Bitcoin is accepted with our Lightning Network integration.  Instant settlement and near zero fees. **Square terminals now accept Bitcoin - that’s over 4M merchants**
Base8 Visa Deb
it -
All the advantages of a debit card, backed by your portfolio and with rewards in SATs.
Staking -
Start earning as much as 15% interest/yield (APY) on your Bitcoin and crypto with one click.
What’s next?
Swaps to other assets like stocks, futures, and even physical commodities, Web app, AI powered advisory, Bitcoin collateralized lending and borrowing, direct deposit, peer to peer payments, decentralized/Web3 wallets and features for advanced users, and AI/Agentic Bitcoin wallets.
At this point, a clearer picture of our vision for Base8 is probably starting to materialize, but here is the best way to describe our final product:
A financial services platform built entirely on Bitcoin.
That’s why SATs have always been so important to us and why we went all-in on SATs while every other app was focused on BTC; because in order to shift regular people (“retail”) towards using Bitcoin like dollars, you need a denomination of Bitcoin that is user friendly and affordable.
With Bitcoin as the foundation, and SATs as the denomination, we’re building a solution that can be the only finance app you need:  Invest, Trade, Bank, Earn, Borrow, Pay, and Save - with an asset that is inflation resistant and blockchain native.
Ok, now let’s talk about some setbacks and obstacles that we encountered over the last year and how we kept moving forward:
We had to completely redo our KYC and compliance process in partnership with sFOX.  This had kept us from onboarding new users for the better part of 2025.  This is now resolved and (luckily) coincides with our new app release and site redesign.
That means we start user acquisition NOW.
We were using microdeposits to link people’s bank accounts and our conversion rates from new account to funded account were abysmal.  We integrated Plaid 3 months ago and
users can now link their bank account and initiate an ACH in minutes.
We weren’t moving fast enough on the development side and were running out of money.  Fortunately, we found a capital partner who believed in the vision and in us, and that led to an investment earlier in the year that allowed us to fully internalize our development team, hire an additional full stack dev, hire a rockstar PM, hire a product designer, and even start to accumulate a Bitcoin treasury.  We are now running lean and mean, following agile methodology, and have resources for marketing.
If you’ve gotten this far and aren’t excited, here’s what has kept me, Moe and the entire team going on the hardest days:
We KNOW we have a clear and direct path to success and we have learned how to execute consistently without a lot of resources.
Why? How?
First, we have a team of loyal, hungry, talented, and experienced individuals that all believe in the mission and have proven themselves over and over again.  Often without any short term benefits.
Second, the digital asset space is preoccupied (obsessed) with stablecoins (literally pegged to an inflationary asset), memecoins, tokenization, decentralization/DeFi/L2s, prediction markets, and Bitcoin ONLY as a speculative asset.  Are they wrong? Not necessarily, but that means very few are building FinTech exclusively on Bitcoin.  That’s a massive opportunity.
Third, we are laser focused on the end goal of having a completely Bitcoin based FinApp.  We are thinking only about that long term goal - that means nothing can distract us along the way.
Fourth, Bitcoin by design (and by current adoption) will be the only digital currency that will compete with fiat in the future digital economy (and that includes AI/Agentic commerce).  It is not a question of if anymore, just when - We will be there waiting with our complete solution.
The only question is how fast we get there.
That’s where you come in.  It should be no surprise to anyone reading this that we need to raise capital.  The more we raise, the faster we hire/build/move, the faster we get users, the faster we become profitable, the deeper/wider the moat, and the bigger the lead we gain.
The goal is to raise a seed/round A in the $3-5M range with a lead investor that adds more value than just capital.
Do you want to invest?  Do you have an intro?  Do you believe in what we’re building?  Do you want to join the team?  Are you just excited as hell about what we’re doing?  Do you have questions/feedback/doubts/criticisms?
Let us know - we want to hear from you!
Thank you again for being a part of the Base8 story, and thank you for taking the time to read this.  We’re looking forward to showing you where we go from here.
Best Regards,
Alex
Alexander Shapiro
Base8
