Announcing AlphaFlow's New Digital Servicing Portal!
Today I am thrilled to share that
AlphaFlow
has launched our new Digital Lender Servicing Portal! To local private lenders, who we believe are the lifeblood of residential real estate investing, servicing can be just as important as the sales team in signing the next term sheet. From the timely funding of construction draws to streamlining the payment process to being a
true partner to borrowers when things go wrong
, good servicing is critical to building a successful lending business with great borrowers.
AlphaFlow's new platform now makes it easier than ever to understand exactly what is happening with each loan at any time, empowering lenders to serve borrowers better than ever!
The platform now provides unprecedented access to:
Loan Performance Insights
Real Time Data
Exportability for integration into other platforms
You can read our full post on the new platform launch
HERE
. Thank you for all of your support and for those who have already liked and shared our LinkedIn post, we truly appreciate it!
If interested in investing, we still have about 1 day until our 2nd Republic campaign wraps up here:
https://republic.co/alphaflow
.
- The AlphaFlow Team
Ray Sturm
