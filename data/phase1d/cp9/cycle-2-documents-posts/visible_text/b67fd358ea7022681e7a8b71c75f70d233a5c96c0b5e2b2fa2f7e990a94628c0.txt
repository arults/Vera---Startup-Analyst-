WHAT A YEAR!
Dear Wanderers,
First and foremost, our team wants to wish you a very happy New Year!
We are excited to report that the year was very successful for Wandering Barman.
In our last quarter of 2020 we launched in Louisiana, where Whole Food Market started carrying us in 6 locations throughout the state! This is a very big step forward towards making craft cocktails more accessible.
HQ
We have been working tirelessly on the construction of the new headquarters. We are pleased to show you an exclusive preview of the production space.
Before..
After..
The tasting room will not open for a couple of months due to the COVID restrictions but we are partnering with very talented designers to make sure this will be a unique and curated experience where we can finally pour you a drink from our own branded concrete bar (see photos.. )
Incredible growth
We are pleased to report a staggering growth from 2019 to 2020 we more than doubled our 2019 volume! We sold 205,176 cocktails that’s an additional 106,800 cocktails in one year.
We are now looking ahead and have so much in the works for 2021! The grand opening of our tasting room of course but also the launch of several new developments.. **TEASER ALERT** We are creating a bigger product format without compromising perishability, we will also be adding several new cocktails to our existing line of "classics with a twist" with new spirits and last but not least a full new product line!
Again, the support of all of you has made this all possible and we will not stop here! We cannot wait to host you all in our beautiful tasting room.
Cheers!
Darren, Julian and Roxane
ROXANE MOLLICCHI
Wandering Barman
