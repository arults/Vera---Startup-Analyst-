One of the best ways to achieve both is to find new ways to monetize our existing corporate relationships spanning 2,700 locations in 46 states where we already conduct business. In our last post “ACFN Innovation Working” we discussed our new Pharma-Vend product. Pharma-Vend is a new and separate product offered to existing clients as a new service and generates an entirely independent additional new revenue stream. Another way to monetize existing relationships is by adding new functionality to existing units.\u003c/p\u003e\u003cp\u003e\u003cu\u003e#1 Bitcoin buy and sell –\u0026nbsp;\u003c/u\u003e\u003c/p\u003e\u003cp\u003eWe completed Beta Testing and are currently adding a recently released software upgrade to existing ATM units that will enable these units to facilitate the purchase and sale of Bitcoin at our ATM terminals. We are already seeing transactions at multiple terminals each month and expect volume to grow as we continue to add more locations. Note that we have no exposure to fluctuations in value we simply connect the buyers and sellers with an exchange that completes the transaction and we are paid a fee for facilitating the transaction (connecting buyers, sellers and exchanges). The fees are higher margin than a traditional cash withdrawal and we have a high value role to play in the new and growing Cryptocurrency ecosystem. We can complete purchases very quickly and in a simplified manner; but the more important role for ACFN is exchanging Bitcoin for cash. Without access to an ATM network that facilitates Bitcoin transactions when a Bitcoin owner wants to exchange their Bitcoin for cash, the transaction will involve a few steps and take a few days.\u003c/p\u003e\u003cul\u003e\u003cli\u003eBitcoin first needs to be sold for a stable-coin such as USDT or USDC.\u003c/li\u003e\u003cli\u003eThe stable-coin then needs to be converted to USD on an exchange that can complete that transaction (might need to be transferred to Coinbase or Binance US)\u003c/li\u003e\u003cli\u003eThe USD need to be transferred back to your bank account\u003c/li\u003e\u003cli\u003eYou can then access the dollars at your account when the transaction clears which typically takes several days\u003c/li\u003e\u003c/ul\u003e\u003cp\u003eAs you can see this is long and cumbersome. With our new software upgrade, a Bitcoin owner registered with our network can get cash from any of our participating ATMs within minutes. They log into the app, identify the terminal closest to them, initiate the transaction to get their code then go to our terminal to enter the code and get their cash. The entire transaction takes just a few minutes, and creates a very attractive, low friction method to exchange Bitcoin for cash. Minutes vs days! We expect this will be very popular in the months and years ahead as Cryptocurrency popularity continues to increase. Crypto is a very exciting innovative new technology, and we are glad to participate and help facilitate an important function - access to liquidity! \u0026nbsp; \u0026nbsp; \u0026nbsp;\u003c/p\u003e\u003cp\u003e\u003cimg src=\"https://uploads.republic.com/p/images/attachments/original/000/080/747/80747-1670396146-cee20aac9b74d6d024fb9e5abb94c83338b7978b.png\" class=\"fr-fic fr-dib\" style=\"width: 201px;\"\u003e\u003c/p\u003e\u003cp\u003e\u003cu\u003eCurrency Exchange\u003c/u\u003e\u003c/p\u003e\u003cp\u003eACFN is the largest provider of ATM services to hotels in the US with more than 1,800 hotels in our portfolio. Hotels in proximity to airports and at Gateway cities provide services to many international travelers that hold their currency in bank accounts that are not dollar denominated. When an international traveler wants to access USD using their account in Euro or Yuan or Yen they need currency exchange services. A few years ago software was developed that enabled our ATM terminals to facilitate Dynamic Currency Conversion (“DCC”). We have been deploying this software at targeted locations where we see international activity and currently have about 600 terminals active with DCC software. In the month of July 2022, which is a high travel month, ACFN collected $39,000 in currency exchange fees. This is an income that did not exist just a few years ago as it used to go to Visa, Master Card and the banks. DCC software is an example of a great development for the ATM industry that created an entirely new revenue stream from existing legacy units.
