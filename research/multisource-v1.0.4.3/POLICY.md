# Vera multi-source claim policy v1.1.0

Policy ID: vera.multisource.claim-policy.v1.1.0
Status: prospective additive research amendment, implementation candidate. Effective for new augmented work only after independent release review and explicit researcher/verifier acknowledgement. No historical native-only result is relabelled.

## Estimand and dataset boundary
The augmented layer records source-attributed atomic observations about identified businesses, legal entities and securities. It is a secondary research layer, not a replacement primary estimator or a universal real-world-survival dataset. Frozen Phase 1C platform-observed definitions and canonical research v0.5.0 remain immutable, pinned in native-pins.json. No rates, state transitions, point censor dates, numerical priors or production calibration are emitted. A later version must define control/independence semantics, denominator/round/clock eligibility, estimator support for intervals and paired native/augmented comparisons before enabling those outputs. Additional source coverage does not repair a selected denominator.

## Evidence and claim contract
Every source preserves original public URL, publisher, document/source type, interested-party relationship, factual-origin family with rationale/upstream URLs, public capture and hash, original artifact hash with capture scope, retrieval timestamp or explicit missingness, publication and source-as-of dates. A hash authenticates captured bytes, not the truth or publisher authenticity. Full source-body inspection must be affirmed, with the inspection scope available to the independent reviewer. A headline, snippet or domain alone is insufficient. Original publication date is not proof a mutable page was available with the same content at that date.

Every claim preserves canonical company/round linkage, separate identity review, exact excerpt/locator, source stance, typed predicate, time semantics, missing fields, contradictions and independent disposition. Identity requires exact strong identifiers or two distinct corroborating medium identifiers with provenance and independent review. A name or inherited brand/domain alone is insufficient. Business identity may support a business-level announcement while security-issuer continuity remains unknown. Control, investor-consideration and priced-equity claims require verified security-issuer chain.

An independent semantic reviewer must confirm the precise predicate and temporal reading of the complete source. The executable validator checks the signed-off structured assertion, hashes and constraints; it does not autonomously prove natural-language entailment, independence or authenticity. Review hashes bind complete claim content and the source snapshot. Any material source or claim edit invalidates approval. Source adequacy review separately binds plan, sources and claims; a JEV receipt is decision support, not evidence or an accuracy measurement.

## Claim-specific authority
Official buyer/issuer completed-act announcements can support attributed acquisition completion, even from one original source. Announced intent, asset acquisition, whole-issuer acquisition, control transfer, operational autonomy, enterprise value, shareholder consideration and realized investor return are different predicates. Unknown payout does not erase acquisition evidence, but acquisition does not imply payout, closure or terminal independent-operation state. Buyer/target and syndicated reports sharing one factual origin count as one family. Different independent reporting can strengthen ordinal support without numerical probabilities.

Regulatory/court/registry records establish facts within their actual scope. Active registration does not establish commercial operation. C-TR establishes reporting termination only when its content supports that claim; it is not automatic closure. Missing C-AR is bounded search missingness, not failure; filed reports are not automatically audited. Petition is not liquidation. Dissolution and product/business cessation have distinct effective dates. Transaction evidence must map the actual legal entity and security; enterprise value, SAFE caps, token price or project distributions are not priced equity or realized holder returns.

Affirmative dated issuer business activity may support attributed operation at the stated point or during the stated period. Product release/store activity remains product-scoped. Search snippets, directories, databases and uninspected news are discovery only. No source count substitutes for competent original text.

## Time, contradiction and missingness
Use point, activity_period, event_interval, upper_bound or unknown. Activity sometime in Q1 is not operation at quarter end and not an interval-censored terminal transition. Completed by a dated announcement supplies an upper bound, not exact close. Never substitute publication, retrieval or present date. Keep evidence-availability/known-at limits separate from event time; unknown retrieval prohibits historical availability certification. The report displays retrieval/publication/as-of ages and dates rather than inventing a universal freshness threshold. Newer contradictory evidence reopens review; stale evidence never extends operations to today.

Contradictions are claim-, subject- and time-scoped. Acquisition and continuing product activity are not inherently contradictory. Shutdown and later relaunch can both be true. Record open conflicts and preserve both source histories; an open substantive conflict blocks acceptance. Missingness distinguishes not searched, no relevant result in bounded search, access/auth/tool failure, ambiguity and not reported. None means closure. Conflict resolution requires rationale and a new independent review.

## Mandatory source verifier
Researchers must predeclare a claim- and entity-specific source sweep, including relevant authoritative outside records, primary original text, alternative lawful sources and negative/contradictory queries. Routes record entity/alias/year scope, query, time, source family, result/access status and evidence IDs. Do not repeatedly probe denied routes or bypass access limits. A material unperformed route blocks sufficient-research status; infeasible routes need explicit reasons and remaining uncertainty. Source adequacy and narrow claim acceptance are distinct decisions.

A dedicated independent source verifier checks quality, factual-origin variety and depth, identity/time specificity, original-source inspection, contradiction search, missingness, alternatives, remaining material routes and stopping rationale. No universal two-source minimum. Verdicts are SUFFICIENT_FOR_NARROW_CLAIM, INCOMPLETE_MATERIAL_ROUTE, EXHAUSTED_WITH_UNKNOWNS or ACCESS_BLOCKED. Operational stopping is not adequate research or a global plateau. Before an augmented finding is released as accepted, both claim adjudication and sufficient scoped research review must pass. An accepted atomic observation whose research remains incomplete stays explicitly unreleased.

## Reproducibility and rollout
The release package contains versioned policy, schema, executable rules, real pending-review examples, synthetic adversarial tests and deterministic content-addressed reports. All changes are additive; rollback means selecting the prior input/report hash, never editing original native bytes. Each researcher, independent claim verifier, source verifier and final integrator must acknowledge the exact policy ID and document hash. Pending acknowledgements remain pending; neither generated names nor legacy assignment implies assent. Future handoffs must include policy and role contracts. Never publish credentials, private receipts, access-controlled text or internal orchestration records. Public excerpts and hashes are separate from private receipts. All probability/estimator promotion requires a separately reviewed protocol version.

## Secondary reporting and reviewer trust boundary
Independent journalism may support a secondary attributed assertion when full text, editorial competence, named underlying sources, original URLs and factual-origin provenance are inspected. It is labelled SECONDARY_ATTRIBUTED, never silently upgraded to official confirmation. Specialist databases support separately typed discovery leads until underlying original documents are inspected. A reputable outlet copying a press release retains that release's origin family. Domain reputation alone cannot establish independence or entailment.

ACCEPTED_ATTRIBUTED_OBSERVATION means a hash-bound semantic review supports that source assertion. It is not yet a released research finding. RELEASED_RESEARCH_FINDING additionally requires sufficient scoped source review and both reviewers authorized in a separate operator-controlled registry. The registry is supplied out of band to the CLI, never trusted from evidence JSON. Names and hashes alone do not authenticate a reviewer; the operator must verify actual independent work before adding roles. This local tool does not provide signatures, access control or a remote identity provider. Unknown or unconfigured authority leaves the result unreleased. The internal adjudicate() helper is not a release entrypoint; use report()/CLI, which validates hashes first.


## Prospective1.1.0 amendment: scoped research, honest windows, inspected sections
Implementation1.0.2 may evaluate explicitly selected claims while retaining the complete parent ledger. Include transitive declared dependencies/contradictions and applicable counterevidence routes; same-entity/shared-source exclusion requires independently reviewed relevance. Contextual claims do not all require release eligibility. Scope reviews bind complete parent evidence and exact scope; no old review migrates. Report held remainder separately.

Required search chronology can use independently verified exact times or observed execution windows with exact receipt/hash and bound provenance. Worst-case bounds must establish plan-before-search-before-review/cutoff. Unknown or overlapping order holds. A completed-by marker alone cannot satisfy required prospective search, but may bound collection availability of contextual evidence under independent scope review. Do not fabricate exact seconds. Compare offset-aware instants inUTC while retaining source-local report/event dates. Acquisition of evidence is not earliest public availability.

Claim-appropriate relevant-section inspection may replace literal full-document inspection only with exact document hash, locators, context/qualifications/cross-reference checks, excluded sections and limitations, plus independent adequate-for-this-claim review. Download is not reading. A headline/snippet cannot masquerade as inspected original context; unresolved relevant appendix or operative definitions holds. Full-document inspection remains available.

These amendments supersede the earlier literal universal full-body gate only for newly reviewed1.1.0records. Existing frozen policies/results stay unchanged. Authentic operator-authorized review and new policy acknowledgements are mandatory. No estimator or numerical-prior behavior changes.

A source verifier may separately conclude that selected narrow claims have sufficient substantive research even when the operational collection tranche stopped at its budget. Preserve the original operational stop and unreleased remainder; require explicit scope-bound stopping rationale and never turn budget exhaustion itself into sufficiency. Context-only route classification and changed materiality for selected claims require exact reviewed reasons; all original routes remain visible.

Source-route variety is distinct from factual-origin independence. The same issuer assertion on its own website and in a regulator-hosted filing is one issuer factual-origin group. Regulatory hosting establishes what was filed, not an independent audit of that business assertion. An auditor's or court's own scoped finding may be a distinct origin when actually inspected and independently reviewed. No universal two-origin threshold or probability promotion is implied.
