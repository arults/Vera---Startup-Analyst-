# SapientX source-policy clarification v0.1.1

This additive note clarifies the use of the published SapientX evidence supplement.
Its SEC-linked same-issuer finding is supported external context. It does not close
the frozen study's identity gate: Phase 1C's platform-only restriction applies to
all study evidence, including identity, rather than only outcome evidence.

The prior supplement's `SUPPORTED_STRONG_ISSUER_ID_SECOND_REVIEWED` label describes
the reviewed external identity bridge. It must not be interpreted as a study-gate
admission. A native-only bridge to the frozen 2020 issuer has not been adjudicated
in that packet. The linked later filing remains a separate offering.

The platform-hosted FY2023 financial disclosure remains useful period-specific
issuer-reported context. Neither its period end nor its auditor/filing dates supply
an exact operating or censor endpoint. The April 23, 2024 auditor date versus
Note 12's April 23, 2023 text remains an unresolved internal source discrepancy.
The trailing-three-month revenue interval and sapientx.ai continuity remain held.

The original supplement, source reviews and canonical rows are preserved unchanged.
Its canonical and estimator HOLD values were already in place. No observation,
cohort, rate, prior, eligibility or completion count is changed by this clarification.
SapientX is not an input to the Crua reviewed-identity overlay.

The companion JSON binds this correction to the exact published candidate and
frozen study policy. Any later study-gate decision requires its own native-only
evidence review under that policy, or an explicitly versioned policy amendment.
