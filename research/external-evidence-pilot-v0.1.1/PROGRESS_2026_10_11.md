# External-evidence research progress — 2026-10-11

## Completed bounded work

A versioned local research prototype now separates source-content statements,
source eligibility, endpoint admission and native/enriched comparison checks.
The existing frozen study policy and native baseline remain unchanged. This
publication records generic lessons and progress; it does not install the local
prototype or publish its source packets.

Independent implementation review passed 149 structural checks. A separate
held-gate replay review passed 32 integrity and transformation checks and
reproduced the output byte-for-byte. These results establish the tested scope of
the implementation, not source truth, historical availability or calibration.

The purposely selected stress pilot covered eight existing research units: two
offerings and six campaign-to-issuer links. These are not eight companies or a
representative cohort. Across 24 fixed questions, all 48 lane assessments remained
HOLD and all 24 native/enriched pairs remained NOT_COMPARABLE. Missing admissible
native evidence was not replaced by an assumed baseline result.

## Lessons retained

- Bind each claim to the correct issuer, offering and economic-round scope.
  Legal-entity identity does not establish round equivalence or an outcome.
- Preserve partial dates and conflicting date meanings. A document date, an
  event date, a reporting period and an observation cutoff are different fields.
- Compare matched questions only under the same policy, units, endpoints and
  cutoffs. Preserve the historical native baseline separately from refreshed
  native research at a later cutoff.
- Require proof that the exact content version was available by the declared
  cutoff. A hash proves byte identity; a publication date or local file time does
  not independently prove historical availability of those bytes.
- Assess source authority, document depth and independent origins. Syndication,
  copied releases, shared operators and repeated issuer statements do not become
  independent corroboration merely by appearing at several URLs.
- Retain dissent and contrary evidence. Not searched, not found, unavailable and
  unresolved are distinct states; none implies company failure or survival.
- Keep source verification separate from structural validation. A generic JEV
  design critique is advisory and cannot clear company facts or study gates.

## Remaining work

Historical availability, claim-specific identity/event evidence and source
coverage gaps remain open. Further collection needs a defined scope and a
versioned cutoff treatment; later content must not silently backfill an earlier
cutoff. No canonical event, rate, prior or investor-return probability was added.
**Scientific Phase 1D remains incomplete.**
