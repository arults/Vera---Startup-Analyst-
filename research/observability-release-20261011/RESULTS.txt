Learnings applied
- Provider request duplicates cannot inflate usage or cost across attempts/runs.
- Known inclusive input totals remain usable when cache split is missing, without assuming zero cache.
- Latency cutoff is distinct from final closure and parallel worker duration.
- Partial costs show estimate/invoice basis as well as coverage.
- Stale evaluation artifacts/configurations cannot masquerade as current passes.
- Synthetic/methodology evaluations cannot establish company research quality.
- Successful retries do not erase prior incomplete lifecycle evidence.
- Dashboard denominators exclude incompatible pricing/memory/model scopes rather than mislabeling them missing.

Remaining integration and verification limits
- No central serving-host adapter was found in the inspected repository. ChatGPT/hidden-worker interception, complete provider billing, final-response tokens and remote model/GPU RAM remain unavailable unless the host supplies them.
- Future company reports must expose missing/partial measurements, never invent a total or zero. No measured end-to-end company-average benchmark exists yet.
- Dashboards are local generated artifacts, not a newly deployed multi-user telemetry service. No new external telemetry destination or cross-user data access was created.
- Visual browser QA could not run because the cloud Chromium launch was blocked; HTML generation, escaping, structure and safe links were tested independently.
- Synthetic development fixtures have no independent empirical holdout/calibration claim. Scientific cohort/source/history/return-model gates remain open.
