## AKT London: dated revenue activity

Sources: https://europe.republic.com/akt-london1 and https://europe.republic.com/akt-london

The later campaign reports a £2.3 million revenue run-rate based on July 2023 revenue, with an unaudited-accounts qualification. This supports a company-reported revenue activity period in July 2023, after the earlier campaign's displayed funded date of 26 July 2022. July is a month-period, not an exact operating day or a claim that £2.3 million was earned during that month.

Both pages identify company number 11211232 and the same brand/domain. The later description of the previous campaign matches the earlier page's investor count and funding summary. Their displayed incorporation dates conflict: 7 August 2019 on the earlier page and 18 February 2018 on the later page. That discrepancy remains unresolved. The evidence does not establish uninterrupted independent operation, investment returns, or a completed legal identity audit.

## Altilium: pilot-operation claim and preferred-share terms

Sources: https://europe.republic.com/altilium and https://europe.republic.com/altilium-metal

The later campaign describes an operational pilot line without an explicit operating-event date. Its approval date, 9 June 2025, does not supply that missing date, and planned industrial scale-up remains separate from pilot operation.

The later campaign says investors in that round would receive Series B preferred shares, with a stated 1x nonparticipating preference and weighted-average anti-dilution rights. The earlier campaign's inspected text does not establish the corresponding exact class and rights. A comparison of displayed valuations alone therefore does not establish economically comparable investment terms or investor returns. Offered terms are not proof that shares were issued.

## 3RD ROCK: cash receipt and funded date differ

Source: https://europe.republic.com/3rdrock

The campaign says £250,000 from seven angel investors had been received during an October–November 2019 range, was on the same terms as other investors, and was included in the displayed funded total. The page separately displays a funded date of 21 May 2020. The source's range wording is grammatically defective; it appears to identify 22 October through 13 November 2019, but exact individual receipt dates are not established.

This distinguishes reported cash receipt from the displayed funded date. It does not establish when investors obtained securities or identify the appropriate analytical start date.
