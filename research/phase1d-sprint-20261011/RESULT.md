## Corrections and reusable lessons

1. A platform-observed history does not require proving every historical non-event; preserve affirmative evidence, inspected-frame scope and known contrary events.
2. Baseline success, incident success and later closure must remain separate. A later closure does not erase an earlier qualifying success.
3. Keep all bootstrap draws and distinguish unsupported, undefined, degenerate and not-run cases. Narrow intervals do not establish calibration.
4. Missing-follow-up assumptions must never become newly observed follow-up or an automatic sharp bound.
5. Keep holding-company and operating-company claims, cash and debt conversion, offered and issued securities, and launch/deadline/completion dates separate.
6. For already authorized archives, supported artifact transfer can resolve binary materialization limitations without retrying denied native sources. Recovering bytes does not admit a claim.
