# Vera multi-source evidence

Follow these numbered skills for augmented startup outcome work:

1. Policy and scope: read [POLICY.md](POLICY.md), acknowledge its exact version/hash and preserve frozen native-only data. Pending rollout acknowledgements are not adoption.
2. Claim-scoped research: read [RESEARCHER.md](RESEARCHER.md). Predeclare entity/alias/year routes and search authoritative outside sources, alternatives and counterevidence. Inspect claim-appropriate original text and all relevant qualifications; record failures and missingness.
3. Evidence provenance: preserve URLs, capture/original hashes, excerpts, origins, interested-party dependencies and separate event/publication/as-of/retrieval dates using [evidence.schema.json](evidence.schema.json). Headlines, syndicated copies and domain counts cannot establish independent evidence.
4. Semantic verification: an independent verifier follows [VERIFIER.md](VERIFIER.md), checks exact identity, predicate and temporal meaning, and binds its disposition to claim and source snapshots. Acquisition, control, activity and security-holder returns remain distinct.
5. Dedicated source verification: another assigned source-verifier follows [SOURCE_VERIFIER.md](SOURCE_VERIFIER.md), checks trustworthy-source quality, factual-origin variety and research depth. Material unperformed routes block sufficient research; access or budget stopping is not sufficiency.
6. Critical decision review: use allocated JEV calls for substantive research/verifier decisions against exact artifact hashes. Treat confidence as decision support, never empirical source truth or model accuracy. Preserve unresolved objections.
7. Executable validation: run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` and `python evidence.py demo.json --as-of YYYY-MM-DD --output reports`. Use report()/CLI, not internal adjudicate(), for validated output. Read [README.md](README.md) for source-plan fields and operator-supplied reviewer authority.
8. Release and rollback: release augmented findings only after semantic and sufficient-source reviews plus genuine reviewer-authority checks. Keep reports content-addressed, preserve unknowns and native/augmented separation. No automatic state transition, point censor, estimator or numerical-prior promotion. See [rollout-registry.json](rollout-registry.json) for required roles.
9. Source quality: match source competence to the precise claim. An official register can establish legal status, a buyer can report its completed acquisition, and neither automatically establishes commercial operation or investor payout.
10. Source variety and depth: trace factual origin rather than counting articles; buyer/target statements and syndicated copies can be correlated. Read original documents or independently reviewed adequate sections with exact context and limitations, and investigate substantive alternatives before claiming adequate research.
11. Freshness and conflicts: display evidence age and separate known-at limits from event dates. Preserve contradictions and reopen review when material new information appears; an old observation does not certify current operation.
12. Honest review and adoption: record genuine review/acknowledgement evidence only. Hash-binding detects changed inputs but does not authenticate reviewer identities. Keep synthetic fixtures separate and label inferences separately from source-established facts.
13. Never make up any information. If you do not have data, state that clearly without adding made-up information. Do not invent sources, dates, retrieval timestamps, identifiers, source text, outcomes, test results, approvals or receipts. Preserve explicit missingness; label any supported inference and its basis.

For new source collection, follow [COLLECTOR_TIMING.md](COLLECTOR_TIMING.md): capture the actual request/response execution window prospectively and preserve missing historical times. This collection guidance does not retrospectively repair evidence or change the frozen source-policy contract.

Policy1.1.0: relevant-section inspection requires exact document hash/locators, qualifications and cross-reference checks, exclusions/limitations and independent adequacy for this claim. It never implies the whole document was read. Preserve original historical plan policy/hash; reassessment under a new policy needs fresh explicit scope review, not a rewritten predeclaration.

For selected-scope review, honest execution windows or historical-plan reassessment, read [SCOPED_SCHEMA.md](SCOPED_SCHEMA.md). Verifier-required material routes cannot be downgraded to obtain release.

When withdrawing unsupported historical timestamp precision, read [PRECISION_WITHDRAWAL.md](PRECISION_WITHDRAWAL.md). Bind DOM ownership and exact old/new fields; preserve event dates and obtain fresh independent approvals. Never borrow a nearby comment timestamp for an issuer post.

For report provenance fixes, follow [OUTPUT_PROVENANCE.md](OUTPUT_PROVENANCE.md): recompute from immutable inputs, check both report paths against the engine constant, compare every other field, and preserve original reports.
