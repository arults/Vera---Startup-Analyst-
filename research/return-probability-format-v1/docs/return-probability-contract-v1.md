
## Mandatory investor-return probability panel — contract v1.0.0

This additive output requirement applies to every detailed investment thesis (STANDARD and DEEP DILIGENCE). Preserve all research, calibration, provenance, and decision gates above. It expands the required tail thresholds to **10x, 25x, 50x, and 100x**; it does not authorize unsupported numbers or replace other required return metrics.

For every threshold k, report **P(net realized investor MOIC >= k at the stated horizon measured from entry)**. Show all four rows even when the answer is `NOT ESTIMABLE`. Unknown is never zero. These are unconditional investor outcome probabilities, including failure, financing failure, dilution, and no liquidity by the horizon. They are not probabilities conditional on survival, operating success, company valuation marks, or a successful exit.

Define the denominator and cash-flow accounting before calculating: exact security/tranche, entry date and price/cap, initial and follow-on invested capital, whether strategy assumes participation, ownership/conversion, dilution, pro-rata rights and exercise funding, liquidation preferences/waterfall, debt, fees/carry, investor currency and FX convention, taxes included or excluded, and horizon/date. For a fixed horizon, net realized MOIC uses cumulative net cash distributions received by that horizon divided by the specified invested-capital basis; unrealized marks do not count. Document denominator timing for follow-on capital. Do not confuse this multiple with IRR, public market markups, enterprise value growth, or operating survival.

For each row provide status, point estimate (only if justified), uncertainty interval/range and its meaning, derivation, evidence/source IDs, critical assumptions, and company-specific independent-verifier and JEV feedback. Separate conditional scenario results from unconditional probabilities. Both points and coherent probability-envelope endpoints must satisfy P10 >= P25 >= P50 >= P100, with values in [0,1]. Explain interval type, coverage if applicable, dependence and uncertainty treatment; judgment/sensitivity ranges must never be labelled empirical confidence intervals.

Numeric publication requires the existing calibration gate plus an auditable, reproducible basis: model/version; data cutoff; target population and reference-class fit; denominator, sample size, failures and censoring/missing outcomes; measured return definition and horizon; company-specific update evidence and rationale; scenario weights/paths including loss/no liquidity; treatment of dependence and financing/dilution/waterfall; computation location and input provenance; uncertainty method; and calibration status. State explicitly whether calibration is empirical/out-of-sample, partial, or unvalidated. A registered benchmark, expert judgment range, working script, test pass, or methodology review alone is not empirical validation. Never infer investment probabilities from JEV votes, acceptance scores, reviewer agreement, or confidence about writing quality. Do not count survival as a realized-return event.

If any indispensable input or defensible probability anchor is missing, retain all rows with `NOT ESTIMABLE`, null numeric fields, blocking gaps, and next evidence needed. Where terms support it, separately give exact deterministic hurdle calculations or explicitly conditional dilution scenarios. Do not describe approximate equity-only hurdle arithmetic as an exact contractual payout model. The frozen Phase 1D study remains unfinished unless a separately sourced completion establishes otherwise; this contract creates no validated return model, new prior, or company estimate.

Review the actual derivation of each P with an independent verifier and JEV when those reviews are authorized and available. Reviewers should check estimand/terms, denominator/censoring/reference-class applicability, scenario completeness/dependence, arithmetic, uncertainty/calibration labels, monotonicity, evidence gaps, and whether abstention is appropriate. Record actual reviewer, scope, version/input artifact, timestamp, result, critiques, resolution, and remaining blockers. Distinguish generic methodology review from company-specific review. If review is unavailable, pending authorization, not run, or failed, state that explicitly; never invent feedback or call the company thesis fully verified. External JEV review requires any applicable permission before transmitting private company/user material. Methodology-only reviews cannot clear a company estimate.

The detailed thesis must visibly separate: (1) sourced facts, (2) exact arithmetic, (3) model estimates and assumptions, (4) independent-verifier/JEV status and threshold-by-threshold feedback, and (5) blocking gaps. Use `docs/templates/detailed-investment-thesis-v1.md` and the companion return-panel contract/validator when available. Validation is structural and arithmetic hygiene, not investment validation.

Use the horizon-end cumulative cash-flow ratio, not the maximum ratio reached at an earlier date. With follow-on contributions the denominator may grow; nesting across return thresholds at a fixed horizon does not imply probabilities increase across horizons. State whether each fee is included in contributed capital or deducted from distributions and never subtract it twice. Unobserved distributions are missing/censored, not zero. A fully observed horizon with verified zero distributions can have zero realized cash return without proving economic failure or that remaining holdings are worthless.

A SAFE cap/discount is not a purchase valuation: model conversion and the applicable liquidity/termination provisions. State whether capital paid is gross of subscription/transaction fees or use a mathematically equivalent convention that counts each fee once. Scenario weights must sum to one across mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive paths; never renormalize away failure, missing observations, or unresolved probability mass. Numeric scenarios require defensible weights, not merely a list of possible outcomes. Proposed entry terms and security are indispensable for numeric investor-return estimates. An explicit 10-year horizon from an analysis-time proposed entry may be used only as a clearly labelled analytical assumption, subject to user override; never imply the user supplied it.

Model correlated financing survival, business success, dilution, and liquidity jointly or with supported conditional dependence scenarios. Do not multiply unsupported marginal probabilities as if independent. Unresolved material issues in the estimation basis block numeric publication, even if mechanical hurdle calculations remain possible. Expert elicitation without defensible anchors may inform clearly labelled deterministic/sensitivity scenarios; it does not satisfy the calibration gate for numeric return probabilities.

The shortcut `IRR = MOIC^(1/t) - 1` applies only to one initial cash outflow and one terminal inflow. For multiple contributions/distributions use dated cash-flow IRR/XIRR where well-defined, explain ambiguous/no solutions, and do not present the shortcut as investor IRR.

Before releasing numeric investor-return conclusions, both independent-verifier and JEV reviews must have actually completed for the specific company-estimate derivation, passed, and cover the exact model/input version, with no unresolved material critiques or basis gaps. Until then keep numeric work as a labelled draft, report the pending review/blocker, and do not present it as a released estimate. Methodology-only review cannot satisfy this release gate.
The final detailed thesis uses the RELEASED gate. If either company-specific review is unavailable, unauthorized, pending, stale, or has unresolved material objections, the final four rows abstain (`NOT ESTIMABLE` / withheld pending required review, with the precise reason). Internal draft numbers and a draft's structural validation pass are not approved final probabilities. This format specifies output/review requirements; it does not implement a new return-estimation model.
