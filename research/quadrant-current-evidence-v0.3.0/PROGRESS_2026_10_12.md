## Why the endpoint remains unresolved

The note's **section 1(c), PDF page 4**, contemplates conditional borrower discharge on **13 July 2025 even if all or substantially all assets have not been liquidated**, subject to its stated good-faith-effort and no-default conditions. **Section 1(e), PDF page 5**, preserves lender liens after that borrower discharge. These are contract terms, not evidence that the conditions occurred or that discharge happened on that date. The later release does not identify a cash payment mechanism or prove cash repayment, complete liquidation, all-creditor recovery, shareholder proceeds, operating survival or a cessation date. Equally, it does not prove that liquidation remained incomplete in 2026. The note's broader collateral terms in section 7 also prevent treating the nine indexed applications as an inventory of the whole estate.

USPTO recordation is ministerial and does not validate a submitted transaction or independently verify its recitals. See [USPTO MPEP section 301](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s301.html). The court judgment and recorded private instruments have different authority and claim scopes; neither authenticates the TPLC operator or exact hosted notice.
