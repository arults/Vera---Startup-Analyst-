# External-source research admission v1.2.0

Policy ID: `vera.source-admission.v1.2.0`. Effective prospectively when this amendment is published. This is the current source-scope rule for new Vera research. October 13 pilot captures collected before publication were authorized under the earlier research assignment; they are reassessed under this amendment only after exact final-policy acknowledgement and independent review. Do not imply the final policy/hash was preregistered before those searches or backdate collection/adoption. It activates and clarifies the existing [multi-source claim policy v1.1.0](../research/multisource-v1.0.4.3/POLICY.md); it does not create another estimator or replace its claim/source review contract.

## What changes now

Trustworthy external evidence is eligible to establish independently reviewed, narrowly scoped research facts. It is no longer excluded from every new research gate merely because it is hosted outside the five platforms. Use official filings/registries, competent original company/IR and transaction documents, inspected reputable reporting, and attributable specialist data as appropriate to the exact predicate. A source can resolve identity, financing, operating-activity or transaction facts while later predicates remain unknown.

This amendment supersedes the platform-only rule for **new research-fact collection and admission**. Historical platform-observed results, frozen data and the original [Phase 1C specification](PHASE_1C_SPEC.md) remain unchanged. The existing native engine and method guard remain a historical route; they do not govern whether a new external fact can enter the separate reviewed research layer. New work must record this policy ID, exact policy hash and acquisition cutoff, rather than call external observations native-compatible.

## Minimum per-company checklist

Before collecting, name the legal issuer/business, aliases, indexed round where relevant, time window and exact missing predicate. Reuse prior inspected evidence with provenance; do not repeat an unchanged retrieval just to fill a checklist. Log these four source families for each company or selected claim:

1. **Official record sweep:** applicable SEC/Companies House or other competent registry/filings, including relevant amendments and attachments. A reasoned jurisdiction/predicate-specific not-applicable entry is allowed.
2. **Company/original-document sweep:** company website, investor relations, original announcements and material issuer updates, including old names/domains when supported.
3. **Funding-tracker sweep:** Kingscrowd plus one relevant accessible startup-funding tracker, or a documented reason a route is unavailable/not material and an appropriate lawful alternative. An attempted search does not imply licensed access, a retrieved company profile or complete coverage. No purchase or new access grant is implied.
4. **News and contradiction sweep:** reputable original business/sector reporting; search issuer aliases with the relevant financing/transaction/activity terms and adverse terms such as shutdown, insolvency, administration, acquisition, dispute or relaunch. Preserve both supportive and contrary results.

These are mandatory scoped attempts and coverage records, not an arbitrary quota of successful sources. Inspect the relevant original context, qualifications and cross-references. Search snippets are leads. Retain not searched, searched/no relevant result, access blocked, unavailable, retrieved/not inspected and inspected as distinct states. A material unperformed route prevents a sufficient-research verdict; an irrelevant route needs a reviewed rationale. A search with no relevant result does not establish no funding, closure or survival.

Conditional routes become mandatory when material: archives for historical content availability or changed/dead pages; buyer/investor/transaction documents for control or financing; court, insolvency and dissolution records for distress; sector regulators for regulated milestones; alternative lawful originals for inaccessible material evidence. Do not bypass denied access or repeatedly probe a blocked route.

## Admission rubric

- **Competent direct evidence:** regulatory/court/registry records establish their actual recorded or adjudicated fact; inspected original issuer, buyer or investor records establish appropriately attributed assertions. One competent original can support a narrow fact. Regulatory hosting is not an audit of an issuer assertion, and company self-report is not independent confirmation.
- **Secondary evidence:** reputable original reporting can support a `SECONDARY_ATTRIBUTED` claim when its text, named underlying sources and factual origin are inspected. Kingscrowd and other trackers are useful discovery/corroboration sources; their underlying original documents and definitions must be traced before admitting a decision-critical underlying fact. A retrieved database assertion without that bridge remains an attributed lead, not verified completion or failure.
- **Identity and event linkage:** use the existing strong-identifier or independently reviewed two-medium-identifier rule. Record legal entity/registry or CIK, brand/domain links, economic round, security and date scope where relevant. A company-level funding item does not prove the indexed crowdfunding round closed; later filings can belong to a different offering. Investor payout requires its own security/holder chain, but missing payout cannot erase a supported acquisition fact.
- **Independent origin:** record origin-family ID, upstream sources and why origins differ. A press release, its syndicated stories, a tracker copying them and the same issuer assertion in a filing are one factual origin. Two domains do not imply two confirmations. No universal two-origin requirement replaces claim-specific authority and independent review.
- **Time and historical availability:** record event date/interval, original publication date, source-as-of/reporting period, retrieval window and archived-content/known-at evidence separately. Later documents may establish earlier events in declared retrospective research; they cannot be represented as information available at an earlier prediction or evidence cutoff. Mutable-page dates and byte hashes do not prove historical availability. Do not impute exact dates from month/year, report date or deadline.
- **Conflict resolution:** preserve contrary sources and both histories. Resolve by entity, predicate, time, direct competence and explicit correction, with reasons and new independent review; do not vote by URL count. Unresolved material conflicts hold only the affected claim/dependencies. An accepted earlier-stage fact remains accepted within its supported scope.

Use the existing independent [claim verifier](../research/multisource-v1.0.4.3/VERIFIER.md) and [dedicated source verifier](../research/multisource-v1.0.4.3/SOURCE_VERIFIER.md). The source verifier must determine whether enough trustworthy outside sources were actually searched, retrieved and inspected for the question, including remaining material alternatives. It returns `SUFFICIENT_FOR_NARROW_CLAIM`, `INCOMPLETE_MATERIAL_ROUTE`, `EXHAUSTED_WITH_UNKNOWNS` or `ACCESS_BLOCKED` with exact scope and reasons. Search adequacy and factual entailment are separate verdicts. No automatic source-count score or source-reputation score substitutes for either.

For accepted release, bind actual researcher, independent claim-verifier, dedicated source-verifier and integrator acknowledgements to this policy hash and the inherited claim-policy hash. The existing executable validates the v1.1.0 claim contract only; it does not enforce this amendment automatically. Keep its expected claim-policy ID unchanged. The operator must additionally verify a companion release receipt identifying both policy IDs/hashes, exact evidence-packet hash, actual role acknowledgements and independent claim/source review references before marking amended findings released. Record genuine roles in the existing operator-controlled mechanism; do not retrofit the historical pending registry or invent names, signatures or approvals. A manually reviewed amendment receipt is not a new runtime capability. Until both independent gates and this operator check pass, label the observation pending/held, even if a particular source appears persuasive.

## Evidence is separate from statistical identification

The admission unit is an atomic, attributed, dated research fact. Legal dissolution is not automatically the operating-cessation date; a living brand/product is not necessarily the original issuer; acquisition is not investor profit; operating at a dated point is not proof of continuous operation or complete intervening event history. Reporting termination or missing annual filings is not company failure. Preserve unknowns and interval precision.

This amendment does not automatically construct histories, graph edges, origin/censor dates, rates, numerical priors or production calibration. A new empirical method contract must separately specify expanded-source ascertainment, denominator/round eligibility, state and event definitions, exact/interval timing, endpoint observation, selection and censoring assumptions, and sensitivity/abstention rules. More trustworthy facts do not by themselves establish independent censoring or remove informative missingness. Preserve the frozen native multistate method's PRE/POST requirements whenever that method is chosen.

Do not edit frozen policy bytes or the pins in `endpoint-method-guard-v0.1.0` to make expanded evidence appear old-method compatible. Select the reviewed external-research route for facts; use the old guard only to reproduce or assess its declared native-only method. A later estimator amendment needs its own review, not a new hash that rubber-stamps this source change.

## Immediate application and bounded stopping

Apply the checklist now to predeclared unresolved predicates in the selected company pilot. Report exact prior gap, new original evidence, independent admission result and remaining gap. A supported issuer identity, dated operation or completed acquisition can be useful progress even while survival/return estimation remains held. Stop each bounded question at sufficient narrow evidence, an explicit irreducible/blocked gap after feasible routes, or a user-directed boundary; an operational stop is not global research completion. The pilot is selected research, not a representative cohort or measured system-wide performance.

Preserve earlier pilot results, including held and not-comparable outcomes. Native-versus-expanded comparisons must declare the source-policy difference and otherwise align units, endpoint and cutoff; do not label them same-policy comparisons. No numerical promotion follows from a few successful factual admissions.

This review does not certify the final implementation or any source finding.

Public grounding: [SEC issuer guidance](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/regulation-crowdfunding-guidance-issuers) distinguishes offering progress/final proceeds, annual reporting and multiple lawful reporting-termination reasons; [Kingscrowd's data description](https://kingscrowd.com/data/) describes aggregation from filings, platforms and company information, so shared provenance must be checked. The existing claim policy supplies the detailed source/time/review contract; this amendment changes its operational adoption and source scope, not its evidentiary safeguards.
