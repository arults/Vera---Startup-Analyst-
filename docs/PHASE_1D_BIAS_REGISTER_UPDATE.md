Date: 2026-10-17

## New items from night research
- **Platform enforcement actions**: Wefunder ($1.4M FINRA fine 2022) and StartEngine ($350k FINRA fine 2022) for Reg CF compliance issues. These are platform-level, not issuer-level, but must be disclosed when using platform volume as context. Do not use as company-specific outcome evidence.
- **Acquisition of Seedrs by Republic (2021)**: Creates cross-platform identity and follow-on graph opportunities. Native-only vs graph-aware estimates will differ for Seedrs-origin companies that later appear on Republic US.
- **SPAC vs traditional IPO**: BOXABL used SPAC route. Phase 1C success definition includes IPO/direct listing; SPAC public listing should be treated as qualifying if public trading and shareholder consideration are established. Document the distinction.
- **Court filings as closure**: Bankruptcy petitions are explicit terminal failure evidence even if not platform-hosted. Prefer primary docket over secondary news summaries.
